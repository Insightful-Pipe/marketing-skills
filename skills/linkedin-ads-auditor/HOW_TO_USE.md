# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. Audit my LinkedIn Ads account and tell me why my cost per lead is so high.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your LinkedIn Ads account added.** The skill pulls campaigns, ads, conversions, lead forms, placements, reach, demographics and daily spend itself. You don't need to export anything.
- **Your target cost per lead or per conversion,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses the account's own average.
- **Who you sell to,** if your campaigns don't already say it: job functions, seniorities, company sizes.
- **Optional:** your main competitors, to check their staff are excluded from your targeting.
- **Optional, for lead quality:** how many LinkedIn leads became opportunities and customers, and what a customer is worth. Without these, the skill grades only what LinkedIn can see.
- **Optional:** the forecasted audience size Campaign Manager shows for each campaign.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. Run a full audit of my LinkedIn Ads for the last 30 days. Our target cost per lead is $150.

**High cost per lead**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. Our LinkedIn cost per lead is about $180. Is that normal for us, and what's pushing it up?

**Lead quality**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. Sales says our LinkedIn leads aren't qualified. Last quarter 120 leads turned into 9 opportunities and 2 customers, worth about $25,000 each. Find out why.

**Targeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. I'm not sure my LinkedIn targeting is right for a B2B SaaS that sells to IT and finance directors. Review it and tell me what to change.

**Campaign structure**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. I'm running 5 LinkedIn campaigns and I'm not sure the structure makes sense. How should I reorganise them?

**Audience Network and expansion**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. Compare my campaigns with the LinkedIn Audience Network and audience expansion on against the ones with them off.

**Formats**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. How are my video, document, event and message ads doing? Show me where people stop watching the video.

**Pacing and tracking**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-auditor" skill. Check my campaign group budgets are pacing to spend on time, and that my Insight Tag conversions actually record.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown (withheld when too little can be graded).
2. **A scorecard for eight areas:** tracking and conversions, efficiency and campaign structure, Audience Network and expansion, targeting and who the ads reached, frequency, bidding and pacing, ads and formats, and lead gen and lead quality.
3. **Up to 5 top fixes,** ranked by the spend they touch, each with the numbers behind it.
4. **A campaign table** with a Scale, Optimize, Fix or Pause tier for each campaign once there is a target or conversions to judge by, and **a proposed structure:** how to group and split your campaigns, and which ones compete for the same people.
5. **Format reports** for the formats you use: the video watch curve, document reads, message open and click rates, event registrations, and thought leader ads against company-page ads.
6. **Who the ads reached,** by seniority, job function, company size, industry, title and country.
7. **Lead quality and the cost chain** from cost per lead to cost per customer, when you share your CRM numbers.
8. **A monitoring list,** what couldn't be checked and why, and the next skill to use for each area.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- turn the LinkedIn Audience Network off on a campaign
- turn audience expansion off on a campaign
- pause a campaign, or a weak ad that has a stronger one beside it

It never changes budgets, bids, targeting, conversion rules or form questions. It recommends those.

## Tips

- If the last 30 days had no spend, the skill audits the last months that did and says so.
- Thought leader ads and the copy-length check need the text and author of each ad's post. Connect your LinkedIn Page to InsightfulPipe, or paste the intro text and say which ads are thought leader ads.
- Judge LinkedIn on cost per opportunity and per customer. A $150 lead that turns into a deal is cheaper than a $50 lead that doesn't.
- No connection? Share Campaign Manager exports (campaign, ad and demographics reports) and screenshots of each campaign's targeting and bidding. The skill runs the same areas and marks what it can't see.
- Run it monthly, and again two weeks after the fixes.
