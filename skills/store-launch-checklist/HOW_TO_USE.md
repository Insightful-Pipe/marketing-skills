# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "store-launch-checklist" skill. Check my online store before we launch and tell me what has to be fixed first.

## What you need

- **Your store address and launch date,** and whether the store is still password-protected.
- **The countries you sell to,** and where you're registered to collect sales tax or VAT.
- **Either pastes or connections.** With no connection, paste your store settings, policy pages, a products export and your recent orders. If Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento is connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, the skill reads them itself. GA4, Google Tag Manager, Klaviyo and Merchant Center add their own checks when connected.
- **Nothing for the URL checks.** The site crawler and PageSpeed only need the address.

## Example requests

**Full check on a Shopify store**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-launch-checklist" skill. Our Shopify store opens next Monday. Check everything you can reach and give me the go or no-go.

**From pastes, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-launch-checklist" skill. Here are our WooCommerce settings, policies and a products export. We sell to the UK and Ireland. What's missing before we open?

**Still password-protected**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-launch-checklist" skill. The store is still behind a password. Check what you can now, and tell me what to check in the first hour after we open.

**Before ads and email**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-launch-checklist" skill. We're already taking orders. Is tracking, the welcome email and Merchant Center ready before we start ads?

## What you get

1. **A verdict:** hold, take orders but hold promotion, or ready to launch.
2. **The fails first,** grouped by what they block: real orders, promotion, or the first week.
3. **A 32-item checklist** across domain, policies, payments and taxes, shipping, products, tracking and consent, search basics, mobile speed, email capture and Merchant Center. Each item shows pass, fail, unknown or n/a, with the evidence.
4. **Cross-checks** a plain list can't do: the refund policy against the Merchant Center return window, the countries you sell to against your shipping zones, and your test order against the GA4 purchase event.
5. **What couldn't be checked,** and the one step that settles each.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- turn a draft Klaviyo welcome flow live
- add a missing Merchant Center return policy

Everything else (shipping zones, taxes, prices, policy text) it drafts for you to change in your store.

## Tips

- Place one real order with a real card and refund it before you run the check. It's the only way to prove live payments work.
- Run it again on launch day, right after the password comes off, so the speed and search checks see the real store.
- The skill doesn't give tax or privacy-law advice. Tell it where you're registered and what your adviser says, and it checks the store against that.
