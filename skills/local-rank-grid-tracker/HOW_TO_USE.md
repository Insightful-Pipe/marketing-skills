# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "local-rank-grid-tracker" skill. Check where my business shows in the Google map pack for my main services in each town I serve, and tell me where I'm weak.

## What you need

- **Your keywords and areas:** your service keywords and the towns, neighbourhoods or ZIP codes you serve. Each keyword in each area is one check, and a run covers up to 12 checks by default (for example 3 keywords in 4 areas, or 2 keywords in 6 areas). You can raise it to 30 checks in one run.
- **The DataForSEO MCP server connected through InsightfulPipe,** to pull live results for each area. Without it, paste a rank export (keyword, location, position) or your own notes, and the skill builds the same report from that.
- **Optional: your Google Business Profile connected.** The skill reads your listing's name, website and Maps link to find you in the pack, and the search keywords your profile showed for in the last 3 months.
- **Optional: a Google Sheet** for history, so the next run shows what changed.

## Example requests

**First run across the service area**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-rank-grid-tracker" skill. I'm a plumber in Lakewood, Colorado. Check "plumber", "water heater repair" and "emergency plumber" in Lakewood, Arvada, Littleton and ZIP code 80401.

**Check by ZIP code**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-rank-grid-tracker" skill. Check local rankings by ZIP code for "dentist" in 80202, 80205, 80210 and 80220, and tell me which rival holds the pack where I don't.

**After profile work**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-rank-grid-tracker" skill. I updated my Business Profile services on October 2. Rerun last month's local rank check from my sheet and show me what changed in each area.

**From an export**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-rank-grid-tracker" skill. Here's a CSV from my rank tool with positions by city. Map my strong and weak areas and name the rivals.

## What you get

1. **An area map:** each keyword in each area, with your map pack position and your organic position kept apart.
2. **A verdict per area:** strong, partial, gap or no pack, with the coverage behind it.
3. **The rivals** that hold the pack where you don't, with their ratings and review counts.
4. **A profile check:** how your profile's own search keywords line up with the areas you win, and keywords worth tracking next.
5. **What changed since the last run,** area by area, and whether it followed the work you did.
6. **Next steps,** ranked, each tied to the areas and keywords behind it.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can start a history tab in the Google Sheet you name and save each run there. It never changes your Business Profile, your website or your ads.

## Tips

- It reads the three-listing map pack in Google search results for each named area. It is not a pin-by-pin grid, and it doesn't report positions lower than 3 in the Google Maps app.
- Keep the same keywords, areas and device on every run, so the comparison holds.
- Rerun monthly, and 3 to 4 weeks after a profile or site change.
