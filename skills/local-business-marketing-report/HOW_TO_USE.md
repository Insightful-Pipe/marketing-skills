# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Build a local marketing report for my business from our Google Business Profile and Search Console, and give me a 30/60/90-day plan.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Business Profile added.** The skill reads the profile, reviews, photos, posts and performance itself; you don't export anything.
- **Search Console for your website,** for brand and local searches. Without it, the website search area is marked unknown.
- **Google Ads, optional.** Only for the calls and store visits section, and only if the ad account belongs to the same business. Claude asks before using it.
- **How your customers buy:** they come to you, you go to them, or online only. It changes which checks apply.
- **For the 90-day marketing plan, nothing has to be connected.** Describe the business, the area, your slow days and months, the hours a week you can spare and the cash a month. A connected Business Profile, CallRail, GA4 or DataForSEO account makes the baseline and the season calendar exact.

## Example requests

**Recurring local report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Give me my bakery's local report for the latest 28 days: Maps and Search views, calls, direction requests and reviews, compared with the 28 days before.

**Clinic growth audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Audit our dental clinic's local marketing and tell me what's stopping new patients from finding us.

**Several locations**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Compare our six locations and show me which one is falling behind and why.

**Calls from ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Add our Google Ads call results to the report: how many calls, how many were missed, and whether they count as conversions.

**Local SEO plan, then a post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Build the report and the 30/60/90-day plan, then draft our next Business Profile post. Show it to me before you publish anything.

**Restaurant marketing plan for the next 90 days**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-business-marketing-report" skill. Write a 90-day marketing plan for my pizza place in a small town: Mondays and Tuesdays are slow, I have 4 hours a week and $400 a month, and a gym and a school are on our street. Give me a weekly task list and a way to count what each idea brings in.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown.
2. **A scorecard for six areas:** profile setup, reviews, freshness (photos and posts), Google visibility and actions, website search, and paid local.
3. **Up to 5 top fixes,** each with the numbers behind it and who does it.
4. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** why, and how to fix it.
5. **A 30/60/90-day local SEO plan** built from the findings, with the dates the next post and the next report are due.
6. **For several locations,** a league table that ranks them.
7. **In plan mode, a 90-day local marketing plan for one location:** your baseline, a calendar of busy and slow months and weekdays, the moves that fit your hours and cash (profile and reviews, partnerships, events, local groups, flyers and door hangers, local press, seasonal pushes, slow-day offers), each with its break-even and its own QR, code or call number, a 13-week task list, and check-ins at weeks 4, 8 and 12 to keep or cut each move.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact text, it can:
- reply to a review
- publish a Business Profile post
- add a CallRail tracking number for a plan channel (CallRail bills each number)
- write the plan's task list and tracking sheet to a new Google Sheets tab

It never edits your hours, categories, description or photos, and it never changes anything in Google Ads; it only reads it.

## Tips

- Run it every 28 days, about once a month; the plan gives the next report's date. It compares the latest 28 days with data against the 28 days before, not calendar months, and Business Profile numbers lag 3 to 5 days, so the report ends a few days before today.
- If the report says Google refused the performance data, check that the connected Google account is an Owner or Manager on the profile, reconnect it, and run it again.
- One business per report. Ask for a second report for a second business.
