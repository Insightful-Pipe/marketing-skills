# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "content-pre-publish-qa" skill. Fact-check this article before it goes live, test every link, and give me the fix list with the corrected lines.

## What you need

- **The draft,** pasted or uploaded with its links (HTML, Markdown, or a Word or Google Doc export). Or, with WordPress connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, just name the draft and the skill reads it.
- **The planned publish date,** and whether it is a new piece or an update.
- **Your current prices, plans and product names,** or the pages that list them.
- **Any notes behind the quotes:** a transcript, call notes or the email.
- **Your style rules and disclosure policy,** if you have them: which links earn a commission, what counts as sponsored.
- **Optional, the InsightfulPipe MCP** with the crawler (to status-check every link) and Search Console (to pick related reading from your pages that already earn clicks). WordPress lets it read the draft and, with your yes, save the corrections.

## Example requests

**Full editorial QA**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-pre-publish-qa" skill. Here is our blog post for Thursday. Run the full editorial checklist: claims, quotes, links, dates, byline, disclosures, call to action, related reading and our style rules.

**Fact check only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-pre-publish-qa" skill. Fact-check this article. List every statistic and claim, open each source, and tell me which ones don't match or have no source.

**Straight from WordPress**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-pre-publish-qa" skill. Check our scheduled WordPress post about pricing pages before it publishes, and show me what to fix.

**An update of an old post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-pre-publish-qa" skill. We updated our 2023 guide. Check it for dated figures, old prices and product names, and broken links before we republish.

**Re-check after fixes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-pre-publish-qa" skill. The writer fixed your list. Check the draft again and tell me if it is ready.

## What you get

1. **A verdict:** Hold, Ready after fixes, or Ready, with the number of Blockers and Fixes.
2. **The fix list:** each problem as the line before and the corrected line after, with why and the evidence.
3. **A claims ledger:** every claim with its source, the source's date and age, and whether it matches.
4. **Link results:** every link's status and where it ends up.
5. **Byline, dates and disclosures:** what passes, what's missing and what isn't needed.
6. **Related reading:** up to three of your own pages to link, ranked by the clicks they earn on the topic.
7. **The corrected draft,** with every fix applied and new lines marked.

## What it can change for you

Nothing on its own, and it never publishes. With WordPress connected and your yes on the exact text, it can save the corrected draft to the same post, leaving its status and date as they are.

## Tips

- Send the notes behind every quote. Without them, quotes come back as "could not verify".
- A source behind a sign-in can't be checked. Paste the page or attach the report.
- Run it again after the writer's fixes. It only re-checks what failed and what changed.
