# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. Find my LinkedIn ads with the lowest cost per lead and write the next round of intro text and headlines from what's winning.

## What you need

- **What the ad promotes and who it is for:** a demo, a guide, a webinar or an event, and the job functions and seniorities you target.
- **Your past ads and their results, in any of three ways.** Best: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude with your LinkedIn ad account added, so the skill pulls creatives and results itself (and reads each ad's words through LinkedIn Pages when your company page is connected). Or a Campaign Manager ad performance export. Or paste a few past ads with their spend and leads. A brand-new account works from the brief alone.
- **Facts the copy may use,** such as numbers, customer quotes, features or dates. Without them, the copy makes no claim your own ads or landing page don't already make.
- **Your target cost per lead,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your account's own median.
- **For Conversation Ads or Message Ads:** the person the message comes from.

## Example requests

**Next round from your winners**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. Look at our LinkedIn ads from the last year, rank them by cost per lead, and write five intro texts and five headlines for single image ads based on what wins.

**Conversation Ad**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write a Conversation Ad that offers either a demo or our guide, with every button path mapped to where it goes. The sender is our head of sales.

**Document and carousel ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. We're promoting a 20-page benchmark report. Write document ad intros that tease it without giving it away, and a carousel with one point per card.

**By seniority**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. Tell me which of our LinkedIn ads works best with directors versus VPs, and write a version for each.

**From pasted ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. Here are our last six LinkedIn ads with spend and leads. Tell me which angles win and write the next round for an event ad and the lead form intro.

**Write, then save**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write the single image variants, then save the best one as a draft creative in my lead gen campaign. Show me the payload first.

## What you get

1. **What wins:** your past creatives ranked by cost per lead (or the result your objective counts), with winners, losers, and groups that have too little data to rank.
2. **Who it reaches:** each winner broken down by seniority and job function, and the best creative for each seniority.
3. **The pattern:** why the winners win, what the losers share, and your company page's best organic openings.
4. **The copy, per format:** single image and video intros and headlines, document ad teasers, carousel cards, Message Ads, a Conversation Ad tree with every button's destination, event ad intros and lead form text. Every line has its character count, the limit it is checked against, the ad it comes from and the one thing it changes, and every feed intro shows the first 150 characters LinkedIn displays before "see more".
5. **A test plan and a checks list:** lengths, the opening, claims, repeats, the Conversation Ad tree and LinkedIn's ad policies.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact copy, image and campaign, it can:
- upload the image for a single image ad
- save one single image creative as a draft in the campaign you pick

It never switches a creative on, never edits a live ad, and never changes budgets or targeting. Carousel, document, message and event ads are built in Campaign Manager from the copy it writes.

## Tips

- Run it when a winner's cost per lead starts to climb, or before a new quarter's campaigns.
- Give it real facts: a line with a real number beats a line with none.
- Read the test after each new creative has spent at least twice your target cost per lead, then run the skill again so the new winners feed the next round.
