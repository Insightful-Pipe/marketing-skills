# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. Rank my products and collections for the last 90 days and tell me what to feature on the home page, how to sort each collection, which product pages need work and what to retire.

## What you need

- **Product sales for the last 90 days,** one row per product (units, orders, net sales). A Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento product sales report is enough.
- **A GA4 item report for the same days:** items viewed, items added to cart and items purchased per product (GA4, Reports, Monetization, Ecommerce purchases).
- **Unit cost per product,** or one store-wide gross margin if you don't track costs.
- **The order of each collection** as it shows on your site, and the products on your home page now.
- **Or skip the exports:** connect your store, GA4 and Merchant Center through the InsightfulPipe MCP and the skill pulls everything itself.

## Example requests

**Full merchandising review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. Here are my product sales, my GA4 ecommerce purchases report and my unit costs for the last 90 days. Rank every product and collection and give me the plan.

**Best sellers report that counts views and margin**
Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. Build me a Shopify best sellers report that ranks products on margin per view, not just units sold.

**Home page picks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. My home page has 6 product slots. Which 6 products should be there, and which ones should come off?

**One collection's sort order**
Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. My Kitchen collection is sorted by best-selling. Tell me the order it should be in and which products are buried too low.

**Product pages that need work**
Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. Which products get lots of views but rarely sell, and is the problem the page or what happens after the cart?

**Review, then apply on Shopify**
Hey Claude, I just added the "product-merchandising-analyst" skill. Run the review on my connected Shopify store, then add the home page picks and tag the products to fix. Show me every change first.

## What you get

1. **A scoreboard** of every product ranked by margin per 1,000 views, with views, add-to-cart rate, purchase rate, units, sales and margin.
2. **Product pages to fix,** with where shoppers drop (before or after the cart) and the margin at stake.
3. **Buried winners:** products that convert well but sit below the first screen of a collection.
4. **Collections** compared with the store's own conversion rate, with the weak products on their first screen.
5. **A home page plan** (keep, add, drop) and **a new order for each collection,** with every move.
6. **A retirement review list,** plus products with too few views to judge and new products left alone.
7. **The changes it can make,** each with its exact payload, run only after your yes.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, on Shopify it can:
- switch a collection's sort order (for example from best-selling to manual)
- add products to the custom collection that feeds your home page
- add a tag to the products to fix, review or test (existing tags kept)

On WooCommerce it can add the same tags. It never sets positions inside a collection (it gives you the order to drag), never removes products from a collection, and never archives or deletes a product.

## Tips

- Use 90 days so slower products have enough views to judge.
- Tell it your real number of first-screen and home page slots; the defaults are 8 and 8.
- Run it again a month after a re-sort to see whether the top slots earn more per view.
