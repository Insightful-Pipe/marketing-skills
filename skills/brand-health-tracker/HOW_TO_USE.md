# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-health-tracker" skill. Track our brand health for last month: share of search against our three main competitors, plus everything else you can pull, and tell me what really changed.

## What you need

- **Your brand and 2 to 6 competitors.** The skill drafts a brand term list for each and asks you to confirm it before any paid lookup.
- **Nothing else is required.** You can paste a Keyword Planner or Google Trends export, a Search Console export, GA4 numbers, review exports and survey counts.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** The skill then pulls share of search from DataForSEO, and your own figures from Search Console, Bing, GA4, Google Ads, Google Business Profile, the app stores, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube. Anything not connected is marked "not measured", never guessed.
- **Optional:** a dated events log (launches, PR, price changes), survey waves with sample sizes, your share of market, and a Google Sheet to keep the tracker in.

## Example requests

**Monthly tracker**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-health-tracker" skill. Run the brand health tracker for September. Our competitors are Competitor A, B and C. Here is our events log.

**Share of search only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-health-tracker" skill. Work out our share of search for the last 12 months from this Google Trends export and tell me if the latest move is real.

**Brand audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-health-tracker" skill. Do a brand audit: a 12-month baseline for every brand signal, how we stand against our competitors, and a tracker sheet I can update every month.

**Survey wave**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-health-tracker" skill. Our new awareness survey has 412 respondents and 151 said they know us; last wave was 128 of 405. Is that a real increase?

## What you get

1. **A verdict for the month:** stronger, steady or weaker, with the count of real moves behind it.
2. **A scorecard:** each signal's value, change, normal range and verdict (normal, watch or real).
3. **Share of search** by brand and month, with this month's change split into your own demand, the category and each competitor.
4. **What moved it:** evidence for each real move, and "cause unknown" when there is none.
5. **Survey and AI mention figures** with their margin of error.
6. **This month's tracker row,** written to your Google Sheet only after you say yes.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact row, it can write this month's figures to your Google Sheet. It changes nothing else.

## Tips

- Keep the brand term list the same every month. If it must change, the skill recomputes the past months so the series stays comparable.
- Run it on the same day each month, so follower totals compare like with like.
- Start with a brand audit; it sets up the term list and the sheet the monthly runs build on.
