# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-strategy-plan" skill. Build us a 12-month PR plan from our goals, our news and what past coverage actually delivered.

## What you need

- **A short brief:** what you sell and to whom, up to 4 business goals, the news you know is coming (with dates or months), any data you own, and who can speak to press and for how many hours a month. The skill asks for all of it in one message.
- **Past coverage, if any:** date, outlet and website for each piece in the last 12 months, and whether it linked to you.
- **Your rates, if you want costs:** the annual PR budget, an in-house hourly cost and hours, a freelancer rate and any agency quote. Without them you get hours only.
- **Optional, the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With GA4 and Search Console added, the skill measures what past coverage sent and how brand search moved. With DataForSEO, it finds which news sites and blogs covered your category and competitors in the last 12 months. Without any connection, it works from what you paste or export.

## Example requests

**A full-year plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-strategy-plan" skill. Build a 12-month PR plan. We launch in the UK in March and have a year of booking data nobody has published.

**How to get press coverage with no news**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-strategy-plan" skill. We have no launch coming. Find the stories we can credibly tell and the outlets that cover our space.

**Founder visibility**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-strategy-plan" skill. Make a 6-month plan to get our CEO quoted in the trade press and on conference stages.

**PR on no budget**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-strategy-plan" skill. We're an early startup with no PR budget and 5 founder hours a month. What should we do for the next 6 months?

## What you get

1. **What past coverage delivered:** referral sessions, key events and links per placement, and brand search in coverage months against the months without.
2. **Goals and measures:** each business goal turned into a PR goal with one measure and a target set from your own history, never advertising value equivalency.
3. **Your stories, scored:** every candidate scored on six newsworthiness tests, checked for proof, with the 3 to 5 worth pitching and what would make the rest ready.
4. **Outlets in tiers,** from who really covered the space, with the stories each one fits.
5. **A month-by-month calendar** of announcements, pitches, bylines, award and speaking deadlines and reactive windows, with aims and hours.
6. **Spokespeople and resourcing:** who fronts what and when they're overloaded, and the cost of in-house, freelancer and agency options against your budget.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can write the calendar to a new tab in a Google Sheet you name. It never overwrites a tab, sends a pitch, publishes a release or contacts anyone.

## Tips

- Give dates for the news you know about; dated news becomes the spine of the calendar.
- Say which outlets you must win; they join the top tier even if they haven't covered the space lately.
- Rerun it at each quarterly review with the new coverage list, so the targets move with what actually happened.
