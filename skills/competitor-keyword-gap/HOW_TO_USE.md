# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-gap" skill. Compare my site with my two main competitors and show me the keywords and topics they rank for that I don't.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with DataForSEO added.** The skill pulls the keyword and backlink data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Search Console for your site,** if you can. It's how the skill tells a true gap from a keyword you already show up for, and which of your pages to upgrade.
- **Your competitors,** if you know them. If not, the skill finds the sites you share keywords with and lets you pick.
- **What you sell, in searchers' words,** if it differs from your page names (for example "gantt" for a page you call Timeline).

## Example requests

**Keyword gap**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-gap" skill. Which keywords does competitor.com rank for in the top 20 that my site doesn't rank for at all? Only keep the ones that fit what we sell.

**Content gap**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-gap" skill. What topics do my competitors cover that I don't? Tell me which pages to build and which to upgrade.

**Top pages of a competitor**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-gap" skill. Show me the pages that bring competitor.com the most organic traffic, and which of them are about things we sell.

**Authority comparison**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-gap" skill. Compare my domain's rank, referring domains and organic keywords with my three competitors.

**Where they beat me**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-gap" skill. On the keywords we both rank for, where does my competitor outrank me?

## What you get

1. **Who you really compete with in search,** sorted into business rivals, platforms and directories.
2. **An authority table:** DataForSEO domain rank, referring domains, organic keywords and estimated visits for each site, and the link gap.
3. **The keyword gap:** the top 20 true gaps, ranked by volume, intent and difficulty, each checked against your Search Console. Keywords you already show up for are listed apart.
4. **Content gap topics:** the competitor pages behind the gaps, each with your matching page and one status: upgrade, build and link, or build.
5. **Where they outrank you** on keywords you share.
6. **Each competitor's top pages,** with brand, login and docs pages kept apart, and each page marked on or off topic.
7. **An action plan** for the five topics worth the most.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can save the gap table to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It never edits your site.

## Tips

- Two competitors cost 9 DataForSEO calls and three cost 12, plus 1 if you name a competitor the skill didn't find in its discovery list. The skill asks before going past 15.
- Pick business rivals, not big platforms. A site like github.com shares keywords with everyone and tells you little.
- Run it quarterly, or after a competitor launches a new section of its site.
