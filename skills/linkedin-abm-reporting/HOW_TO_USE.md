# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-abm-reporting" skill. Build my LinkedIn ABM and pipeline report for last month.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your LinkedIn Ads account added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything from Campaign Manager.
- **Your target account list,** if you have one: LinkedIn company ids or company page links with the number in them. Without it, you get who you reached instead of coverage.
- **GA4,** if it's connected, to check how many ad clicks reach your site.
- **Your CRM numbers for the month,** if you want pipeline value: opportunities created, pipeline value and closed-won value from LinkedIn deals.

## Example requests

**Monthly pipeline report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-abm-reporting" skill. Give me the monthly B2B pipeline report for LinkedIn for September. We created 6 opportunities worth $84,000 from LinkedIn-sourced leads.

**Account penetration**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-abm-reporting" skill. Here are our 150 target accounts as LinkedIn company links. How many did our ABM campaigns reach last quarter, and how deep did we get into each one?

**Matched audiences and retargeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-abm-reporting" skill. Compare our retargeting and matched audience campaigns with prospecting. Are the website visitor and video viewer audiences pulling their weight?

**Buying committee**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-abm-reporting" skill. Our buyers are directors and VPs in marketing and sales. Show me how much of our LinkedIn spend actually reached them.

## What you get

1. **A status table** for five sections: campaign map, account penetration, buying committee, matched audiences and retargeting, and the monthly pipeline.
2. **Penetration:** coverage of your target list, how deep each company was reached, and how much went to companies off the list.
3. **Buying committee:** the seniority and job function mix, and how much each campaign leaked outside its targeting.
4. **Audiences:** retargeting and matched audiences compared with prospecting, or a plan to build them from your own video viewers and site visitors.
5. **A month-by-month funnel** from reach to leads, checked against GA4, with your CRM numbers added and labelled.
6. **Up to 5 next moves,** each with the numbers behind it, plus a list of what couldn't be checked.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can turn off audience expansion or the LinkedIn Audience Network on an ABM campaign. It never changes budgets, bids, targeting or campaign status.

## Tips

- LinkedIn only reports a company once it has 3 or more impressions, and hides company-level clicks under that threshold. On a small account, judge depth by impressions and video views.
- Company rows are company ids, not names. Give your list as company page links with the number in them, so the skill can match them exactly.
- Run it monthly with the same list, so the coverage trend means something.
