# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. Give me an SEO performance report for my site: the last 28 days against the 28 before and against last year.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Search Console property added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Your brand terms,** if they aren't obvious from your site name. The skill shows you the brand searches it matched, so you can correct the list.
- **Your own page groups,** if your folders don't match how you think about the site (for example, blog guides vs blog news).

## Example requests

**Monthly SEO summary**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. Write my SEO summary for August against July, with the five-line summary at the top.

**Year over year**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. How does organic search this month compare with the same period last year?

**Branded vs non-branded**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. Split my Google clicks into brand and non-brand searches. Our brand terms are "acme" and "acme tools".

**Devices and countries**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. Compare my mobile and desktop search performance, and tell me which countries have low click-through rates.

**Site sections**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. How are /blog/ and /docs/ doing in search compared with last month?

**Image, video, Discover and News**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-performance-report" skill. How does my site do in image search, video results, Google Discover and Google News?

## What you get

1. **A headline table:** clicks, impressions, CTR and position against the previous period and last year, each change marked real or noise, with the threshold shown.
2. **What drove the change:** the click change split by brand vs non-brand searches, device, country and site section.
3. **Brand vs non-brand,** including the searches Google hides and how much of your traffic its query list covers.
4. **Devices, countries and page groups,** with flags for a mobile ranking gap, countries with a low click-through rate, and folders losing traffic.
5. **The pages and queries that moved most.**
6. **A month-by-month trend,** with partial months marked.
7. **Image, video, News, Discover and Google News results,** plus rich-result appearances.
8. **Up to five takeaways,** each with the number behind it and one next step, and a list of what couldn't be checked.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It only reads Search Console.

## Tips

- Search Console data runs 2 to 4 days behind. The report says which day it ends on.
- Year-over-year needs Search Console data from a year before the period. A newer property gets the monthly trend instead.
- Run it monthly, and again about four weeks after a big change to the site, to see whether the change shows up.
