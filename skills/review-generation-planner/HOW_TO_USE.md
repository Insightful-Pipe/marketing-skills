# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "review-generation-planner" skill. We have far fewer Google reviews than the shop down the road. Work out how many we need and by when, check how we ask today, and write the texts and emails to ask customers for reviews.

## What you need

- **Your Google review count and rating,** or your Google Business Profile connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP so the skill reads every review itself.
- **Two to five local rivals,** or ask it to find them. With DataForSEO connected, it pulls their rating and review count in one call. Bring the counts from your last run if you have them; two dated counts show how fast each rival grows.
- **How many customers you serve a month:** jobs finished, visits or orders.
- **How you ask for reviews today,** honestly: who is asked, when, and whether anything is offered.
- **For an online store:** your product review export and units sold per product (WooCommerce or Shopify export, or the store connected), and where store reviews come from, if anywhere.

## Example requests

**Close the gap with a rival**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-generation-planner" skill. We're a dental clinic with 80 Google reviews; the clinic across town has 210. Plan how we pass them within a year.

**How to ask customers for reviews**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-generation-planner" skill. I run a plumbing company. When and how should we ask customers for reviews after a job? Write the text message and the follow-up email.

**Check what we do today**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-generation-planner" skill. We give 10% off the next visit for a Google review and only ask happy customers. Is that allowed? Fix our process.

**Product reviews for a store**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-generation-planner" skill. Here's our product review export and our orders. Which products need reviews first, and how far are we from star ratings in Google Shopping?

**Review request template**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-generation-planner" skill. Write a review request template for our salon: SMS, email and a QR card for the counter.

## What you get

1. **Stop first:** anything in your current asking that breaks Google's review policy or the FTC rule on fake reviews (gating, rewards, staff reviews, pressure), with the fix.
2. **The gap:** you vs your rivals by reviews, rating and monthly pace; a dated target; the reviews you need each month; and the share of your customers who need to leave one.
3. **For stores:** review coverage by product against units sold, the products to ask about first, and how far you are from Google's minimums for product ratings and store ratings.
4. **The plan:** when to ask, on which channel, and ready-to-use SMS, email, QR card and receipt templates with your own review link.
5. **Rollout and tracking:** a weekly target, what to check each month, and today's rival counts for the next run.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the review request email as a template in Klaviyo. You then add it to a flow or campaign yourself. It never sends a message, never replies to reviews and never edits your Business Profile.

## Tips

- Rerun it monthly. Each run's rival counts become the next run's starting point, so rivals' pace gets more accurate.
- Ask every customer in the same moment with the same message. Asking only the happy ones is against Google's rules, and it hides what you need to fix.
- Keep the request short and give one link. The skill keeps every SMS to one segment.
