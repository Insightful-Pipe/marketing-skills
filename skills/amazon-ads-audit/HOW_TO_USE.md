# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ads-audit" skill. Here are my Amazon Ads bulk sheet, search term, placement and advertised product reports, plus my costs per product. Audit the account and tell me which fixes are worth the most money.

## What you need

- **Your Amazon Ads exports for one 30-day window** that ended at least 14 days ago (7 if you're a seller running only Sponsored Products): the bulk sheet, the Sponsored Products search term, placement and advertised product reports, and the Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display or DSP reports if you run them. Paste them, upload CSV or XLSX files, or point to the Google Sheet you keep them in.
- **Your costs per product:** price, cost of goods, referral fee, FBA fee and any other cost per unit. This is how the skill works out each product's break-even ACOS.
- **Your Business Report** (sales and traffic by child ASIN) for the same dates, and total sales and ad spend for the last three months, for TACOS.
- **Stock, ratings and budget history:** FBA available and inbound units, star ratings, and which campaigns ran out of budget and when.
- **Your goal per product:** profit with a target ACOS, or launch with the highest ACOS you'll accept.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ads-audit" skill. Run a full Amazon PPC audit on these exports. Our target ACOS is 30% on everything except the new herb keeper, which can go to 60% while it launches.

**Why is my ACOS high**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ads-audit" skill. Our ACOS went from 24% to 31% this quarter. Find out where the extra spend is going and how to lower ACOS on Amazon without losing sales.

**Do my ads make money**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ads-audit" skill. Here are my costs and fees per ASIN. Tell me which products make money on ads and which ones lose it.

**Brand versus generic**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ads-audit" skill. Split my Amazon ads into brand and non-brand searches and tell me if the brand campaign is hiding a problem.

## What you get

1. **Break-even ACOS for every product,** from your own price, costs and fees.
2. **A scorecard of ten checks:** the data window, profit by product, TACOS, brand and non-brand, search terms, structure, placements, budgets, listing health, and DSP.
3. **Anything urgent first,** such as a product about to run out of stock.
4. **The top 5 fixes,** ranked by the money at stake each month, each with the math and the numbers behind it.
5. **A change list** in bulk sheet terms (negatives, placement adjustments, budgets) for you to upload yourself.
6. **What couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can save the report to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It never changes anything in your Amazon Ads account: every change is drafted for you to upload or make in the console.

## Tips

- Export after the window has settled. Amazon keeps adding orders to clicks for 7 to 14 days, so this week's ACOS always looks too high.
- Include the fees from your own FBA fee preview or settlement report; a guessed fee moves every break-even.
- Run it monthly, and again after you upload the change list, to see the money move.
