# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. Look at what I have connected, tell me what's really holding back my marketing, and give me the skills to run in order.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with the sources you use: GA4, Search Console and your ad accounts at least. The skill reads which ones are connected and checks a few numbers itself; you don't export anything.
- **The one number you care about most,** if it isn't obvious: sign-ups, leads, sales, traffic or retention.
- **Nothing else.** If you haven't connected anything yet, it still works from your answers to five short questions, and says that the plan wasn't checked against your data.

## Example requests

**Where do I start**
Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. I need better marketing and I don't know where to start. Check my connected accounts and tell me what to do first.

**Ads aren't working**
Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. Our Meta ads get clicks but nobody signs up. Which skills should I run, and in what order?

**Ask anything**
Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. Which channel brought us the most sign-ups last month, and did it grow?

**Launching soon**
Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. We launch in six weeks and have nothing ready. Lay out the skills to run between now and launch.

**Traffic but no leads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. We get traffic but not enough leads. Find what to fix first.

**Automate the routine**
Hey Claude, I just added the "orchestrator" skill. I build the same ads report every Monday and check spend every few days. What can I put on a schedule?

## What you get

1. **What's connected,** by family, with anything that needs attention: inactive accounts, read-only connections, or accounts that belong to a different business.
2. **A live snapshot** of the last 30 days against the 30 before: GA4 sessions and key events by channel, Search Console clicks, and spend and results on Google Ads, Meta and LinkedIn Ads, whichever are connected.
3. **The diagnosis:** what you asked for, and what the numbers say is actually in the way.
4. **A layer check** from measurement up to optimisation, each graded with one line of evidence.
5. **A plan of three to five skills,** each with why it comes at that point, what it produces, what it needs from the step before, what it needs connected, and a rough effort. Skills still in the release queue are marked "coming soon", with the live option for now.
6. **A quick win** when there is one, **a schedule** for the work that repeats, and **what to connect first**.

For a plain question, you get the answer first, the numbers and the calls behind them, and the one skill that goes deeper.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads and routes. Any fix or build happens in the skill it sends you to, and only after you confirm there.

## After the plan

1. Say "start step 1" and run that skill.
2. Move to the next step when it's done.
3. Come back to the orchestrator when you get stuck, finish a milestone or change the goal. It takes a fresh snapshot each time.

## Tips

- If your workspace holds several businesses (an agency's clients, a second brand), say which one this is about.
- Be honest about what you already have. The most common finding is a gap one layer below where you're looking, such as a sign-up nobody is counting.
- The quick win isn't always the right first step, but it helps when you need momentum.
- Install the skills it names so it can hand off directly; it links each skill's page either way.
