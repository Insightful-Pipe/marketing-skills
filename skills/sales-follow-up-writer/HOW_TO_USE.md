# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-follow-up-writer" skill. Here are my notes from today's demo call. Write the follow-up email with the next steps we agreed.

## What you need

- **Your call notes or the transcript,** pasted as they are. The recap quotes the buyer from them, so the more of their own words, the better.
- **For each deal:** the stage, the people at the buyer and their roles, the date of the buyer's last reply, and the emails you've sent since.
- **Any date the buyer named,** such as a board meeting or a return from holiday. The skill never chases before it.
- **Proof you're allowed to share:** customer results and the product pages that answer common questions. The skill never invents a result.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with Google Sheets, to read your deal tracker instead of pasting it, and the site crawler, to find dated news on the buyer's website.

## Example requests

**Recap after a sales meeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-follow-up-writer" skill. Here's the transcript of my call with Fernhollow Physio. Write the recap with what we agreed, who does what and by when.

**Follow up on a proposal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-follow-up-writer" skill. I sent a proposal two weeks ago and followed up twice with no answer. What should the next email say, and when should it go?

**My whole pipeline**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-follow-up-writer" skill. Read my deal tracker sheet and tell me which follow-ups are due today. Write those, and give me the dates for the rest.

**Bring the decision maker in**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-follow-up-writer" skill. My contact says her finance director has to approve the spend. Write a note she can forward to him.

**Closing the loop**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-follow-up-writer" skill. This prospect hasn't replied to three follow-ups. Write a polite last email.

## What you get

1. **A follow-up board:** every open deal with the chasers sent, the business days since your last email, which email is due, and when.
2. **The emails due today,** each with the subject, the body, the sources for every fact, and a NEW line on every chaser saying what it brings.
3. **A recap in the buyer's own words,** with each next step, its owner and its date, and anything unclear marked to confirm.
4. **The next dated touches** for each deal, up to the cap of three chasers and one closing email.
5. **A list to check before you send:** facts to confirm and proof each email uses.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can write the next follow-up date and the last email into your Google Sheets deal tracker, in the columns you name. That needs a Read & Write connection, and an owner, admin or operator can switch it off.

It never sends, schedules or queues an email. You send from your own inbox.

## Tips

- Paste the notes on the day of the call. The recap is due that day, the next business day at the latest.
- Tell it about holidays and out-of-office replies; they move the dates.
- When a chaser has nothing new to bring, the skill asks you for something instead of writing "just checking in".
- For an automated sequence sent from your email tool after a demo or quote, use the email-sequence-builder skill if you have it installed.
