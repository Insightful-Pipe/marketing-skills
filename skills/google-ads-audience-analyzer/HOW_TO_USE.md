# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-audience-analyzer" skill. Review my Google Ads audiences, remarketing lists, Customer Match and demographics, and tell me what to change.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **What your business sells,** if it isn't clear from your campaign names. Google's policy decides which audiences you may use, and the skill checks that first.
- **A target CPA,** only if you want one. Without it, each segment is compared with its own campaign.

## Example requests

**Full audience review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-audience-analyzer" skill. Review every audience in my Google Ads account for the last 90 days.

**Remarketing lists**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-audience-analyzer" skill. Are my remarketing lists big enough to serve on Search and Display, and how do they perform?

**Customer Match**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-audience-analyzer" skill. Check my Customer Match lists: size, match rate and results.

**Demographics**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-audience-analyzer" skill. Which ages, genders and income bands cost me too much, and should I exclude any?

## What you get

1. **A policy check:** whether Google lets your business use remarketing, Customer Match and demographic targeting.
2. **A scorecard for four sections:** audience segments in observation, remarketing lists, Customer Match and demographics.
3. **Actions ranked by the spend they involve,** each with the numbers behind it.
4. **Segment findings you can trust:** each segment is tested against its own campaign, so one or two lucky conversions don't decide anything.
5. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- exclude an age range or gender from an ad group, when the data backs it
- add a remarketing or Customer Match list to an ad group in observation

It never changes budgets, bid strategies or targets, and it never uploads customer data.

## Tips

- Run it every quarter. Segments need about 90 days of data before a verdict means anything.
- Keep audience segments on Search campaigns in observation, so they report without cutting reach.
- If your business is in a sensitive category, such as criminal defense, health or gambling, expect the skill to rule out remarketing and Customer Match. That is Google's policy, not a data problem.
