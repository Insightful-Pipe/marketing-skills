# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-recommendations-reviewer" skill. Go through my open Google Ads recommendations and tell me which ones to accept, adapt or dismiss, with the numbers behind each one.

## What you need

- **Either the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with your Google Ads account,** so the skill pulls the recommendations, auto-apply settings, change history and campaign numbers itself,
- **or a paste:** your Recommendations page (a screenshot works), the auto-apply list, and a 30-day campaign report with cost, conversions and impression share lost to budget.
- **Your target CPA or ROAS,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your account's own 30-day average.
- **Anything Google can't know:** claims your ads must not make, pages that don't sell, and image rules.

## Example requests

**Review every open recommendation**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-recommendations-reviewer" skill. Should I accept my Google Ads recommendations? Check each one against my numbers. Our target CPA is $65.

**Stop auto-apply**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-recommendations-reviewer" skill. Tell me which recommendations Google is auto-applying, what it already changed, and turn off the ones that shouldn't run without me.

**Automation settings**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-recommendations-reviewer" skill. Check which automation settings let Google write ads, pick landing pages or widen reach in my campaigns, and tell me which to switch off. We never quote prices in ads.

**Optimization score**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-recommendations-reviewer" skill. My optimization score is 64%. What does it actually measure, and what should I do about it?

**From a screenshot**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-recommendations-reviewer" skill. Here's a screenshot of my Recommendations page and my campaign report. Which of these should I apply?

## What you get

1. **A verdict for every open recommendation:** accept, adapt, dismiss or your call, with the test and the numbers. Budget raises are tested on budget-lost impression share and the cost of the next conversion; broad match on conversion volume, bidding and budget.
2. **Auto-apply:** which types to keep or pause, and the changes auto-apply already made in the last 29 days, each judged on its results since.
3. **Automation settings:** one row per campaign and setting, with the fact about your business that decides keep or turn off.
4. **Optimization score,** explained, so nobody manages to it.
5. **The order to act in,** one bidding or match change per campaign at a time, with re-check dates.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, through the connector, it can:
- change a campaign budget, target CPA or ROAS, or bid strategy
- add an accepted keyword in the match type it recommends
- turn search partners or Display expansion on or off
- add sitelinks or callouts written from your own pages
- pause auto-apply recommendation types

Dismissing a recommendation and switching off asset automation have no connector action, so the skill gives you the clicks for those.

## Tips

- Run it every couple of weeks: Google adds new recommendations all the time.
- Pause auto-apply first, so Google doesn't undo or re-apply what you decide.
- Dismissing a recommendation raises your optimization score just like applying it does.
