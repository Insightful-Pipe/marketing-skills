# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "roi-business-case-builder" skill. Build the business case for my prospect from these discovery notes, with a range, the payback and the spreadsheet behind it.

## What you need

- **Your prospect's numbers.** Paste discovery notes, a call summary or a sheet: volumes, time per task, hourly cost, rates, value per sale or visit, margin, and the tools you would replace. Say who gave each number.
- **Your price.** Plan or quote, usage fees, setup and contract term.
- **What your product changes for them,** in three or four plain sentences.
- **Nothing to connect.** The skill works from what you paste. If the InsightfulPipe MCP is connected, it can also read public signals about the prospect (page speed, active ads, and search traffic estimates if you sell marketing services) and write the spreadsheet into Google Sheets after you say yes.

## Example requests

**A business case for finance**
Hey Claude, I just added the "roi-business-case-builder" skill. My champion at a 4-clinic physio group needs to take our proposal to their finance director. Here are my discovery notes and our quote. Build the business case.

**Show ROI to a customer at renewal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "roi-business-case-builder" skill. A customer renews next month. Here is their usage and what they told us at kickoff. Show the ROI they got and what the next year looks like.

**An agency pitch from public data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "roi-business-case-builder" skill. I run an SEO agency and I'm pitching a dental group. Use public data on their site and ads against two rivals, plus their numbers below, and build the value case for our retainer.

**Just the spreadsheet**
Hey Claude, I just added the "roi-business-case-builder" skill. Build the ROI spreadsheet for this deal with live formulas and put it in my Google Sheet. Show me the tabs first.

## What you get

1. **An input ledger:** every number tagged as the buyer's, yours, public or an assumption, with its source.
2. **Value drivers:** time saved, revenue gained at margin and costs avoided, each with its formula and marked cash or capacity.
3. **Low, expected and high results:** year-1 net, ROI, 3-year net, NPV at the buyer's rate, and payback on total value and on cash alone.
4. **A sensitivity table,** the break-even point for the biggest assumption, and a stress test with the revenue drivers at zero.
5. **A one-page business case** in the buyer's numbers, ready for their finance team, with a day-90 check.
6. **The spreadsheet:** Inputs, Drivers, Cashflow, Scenarios and Sensitivity tabs with live formulas.
7. **Questions for the buyer** that would replace your widest assumptions with their own numbers.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payloads, it can add the five tabs to a Google Sheet and fill them. It never edits tabs that already exist and never shares the sheet.

## Tips

- The more numbers come from the buyer, the stronger the case. Ask them to measure the one assumption that swings the result most.
- If the low case loses money, the skill says so. Offer a pilot or a smaller first scope instead of stretching the assumptions.
- Rerun it after the day-90 check with the real numbers. That becomes your renewal case.
