# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "media-list-builder" skill. Build a media list for our new data report: find the journalists who covered this topic and our competitors in the last 12 months, and rank who to pitch first.

## What you need

- **Your story in a sentence or two:** what's new, when it can run, and the proof behind it.
- **Two to four topic phrases and your main competitors.** The skill asks for them if you don't say.
- **Optional, for live coverage: the InsightfulPipe MCP with DataForSEO connected.** Claude then pulls dated news and blog articles, the outlets that already link to your competitors, YouTube shows and trade titles itself. A typical list takes 10 to 13 DataForSEO calls, billed to your DataForSEO account.
- **Without a connection,** paste the articles, outlet lists or PR database export you have. The skill cleans, scores and ranks those the same way.
- **Optional: Google Sheets connected,** if you want the list written into a new tab.

## Example requests

**A launch or data story**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-list-builder" skill. We publish our customer survey on November 10. Build a media list of writers who covered small business payments and our two competitors this year.

**How to find journalists to pitch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-list-builder" skill. I've never done PR. Who should I pitch about our new app for dog groomers, and why each one?

**Fill in a media list template**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-list-builder" skill. Here are 40 articles from a news search. Turn them into a media list with beats, recent articles, scores and contact routes, and drop anything that isn't a real writer.

**Local press**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-list-builder" skill. We're opening a second clinic in Riverton. Build a local media list from the last 6 months of coverage.

## What you get

1. **A ranked media list:** one row per writer with tier, score, beat, up to three recent relevant articles with links and dates, outlet reach, warm signals, why they fit and where they say to send pitches.
2. **YouTube shows** that covered the topic, with the matching videos and their views.
3. **Outlets to research:** good coverage with no byline yet, and trade titles or newsletters to check.
4. **Already in touch:** your existing contacts who covered the topic, with their newest article.
5. **A dropped log:** wire copies, vendor pages, directories, off-topic matches and old articles that were cut, with counts.
6. **The sheet:** the same columns, ready for tracking, as a new Google Sheets tab after your yes, or as a table you can paste.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact tab name and rows, it can add a tab to your connected Google Sheet and write the list into it. That needs a Read & Write connection. It never sends a pitch and never guesses an email address.

## Tips

- One list per story. A list built for a funding round is the wrong list for a customer story.
- Give it your do-not-contact list, so nobody who opted out comes back.
- Rerun it before each new story: writers move outlets and beats change.
