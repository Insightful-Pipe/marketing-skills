# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-capacity-planner" skill. Here is last month's timesheet and our retainers. Which clients are we losing money on, who is overloaded, and can we deliver next month's backlog?

## What you need

- **A timesheet export** for the month you want to review, from any time tracker or a Google Sheet: date, person, client, task, hours, billable or not. A column marking ad hoc work and the estimates on finished tasks make the plan sharper.
- **Your team:** role codes or names, skills, contracted hours a week and days off.
- **For an agency:** each client's monthly fee, hours sold, scope and tool costs, each person's loaded cost per hour, and your target margin.
- **Next period's backlog:** task, client, skill, estimate, due date and priority.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** with the clients' Google Ads, Meta Ads, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts. The skill then counts active campaigns, ads and posts itself to size the recurring work. Without it, paste the counts.

## Example requests

**Agency month-end review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-capacity-planner" skill. Here is our October timesheet export and the retainer list. Show me hours used against hours sold for each client, any out-of-scope work, and each client's margin. Our target margin is 35%.

**Is a retainer under-priced?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-capacity-planner" skill. Our dental client always runs over its hours. Count the campaigns and ads in its Google Ads and Meta accounts and tell me how many hours the account really needs and what the fee should be.

**Team capacity planning for next month**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-capacity-planner" skill. Here is our team, the days off and the November backlog. Build the plan per person and tell me what doesn't fit.

**In-house sprint plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-capacity-planner" skill. We're a four-person in-house marketing team with no clients. Here is the next two weeks' backlog and last sprint's timesheet. Who takes what, and who is overbooked?

**Mid-month check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-capacity-planner" skill. We're halfway through the month. Here is the timesheet so far. Which retainers are burning faster than the month?

## What you get

1. **Billable utilisation per person** against the band you set (70 to 80% by default for delivery roles), with overtime flags.
2. **Each client's burn, out-of-scope hours and margin** (fee minus hours cost minus tools), plus the effective hourly rate.
3. **Hours behind the fee:** the hours each client's account really needs, from its size and your team's own hours per campaign, ad or post, and the fee that would hit your target margin.
4. **The next period's plan:** plannable hours per person after internal time and a reserve for unplanned work measured from your own timesheet, recurring work placed first, the backlog by priority with your own estimate accuracy applied, what is on hold for a change order, and what does not fit, with options.
5. **Forecast burn** per client before the month starts.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact rows, it can write the plan to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It reads your ad and social accounts and never changes them.

## Tips

- Run it at each month end, before you send invoices and before the next plan is fixed.
- Ask your team to tag ad hoc requests in the time tracker. The reserve for unplanned work then comes from your data, not a guess.
- An under-priced retainer has two fixes: a higher fee or a smaller scope. The report gives the numbers for both conversations.
