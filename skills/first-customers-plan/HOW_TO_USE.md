# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "first-customers-plan" skill. I built a product and haven't sold it yet. Plan how I get my first paying customers in the next 30 days.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected to start.** Tell Claude the product in one line, what buyers use today instead, the price, and the kinds of buyer it could be for. The skill plans from what you type.
- **Your real selling hours a week:** time for conversations, posts and partners, not building. Every target comes from this number.
- **Who you can message this week,** grouped by kind of buyer, plus any partners who already serve those buyers (a supplier, a reseller, an association, a newsletter).
- **Your cash budget for the 30 days and your goal,** if you have one. A goal the hours can't carry gets an honest gap, not a raised rate.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With DataForSEO it measures the questions buyers search and finds the communities and creators that rank for the problem. With the ad libraries it shows which channels rivals pay for to reach each kind of buyer. Once visits start, GA4 and Search Console feed the weekly check, and Google Sheets can hold the tracker.

## Example requests

**Plan the first customers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "first-customers-plan" skill. I built an inventory app for cafes and haven't sold it yet. I have about 12 hours a week for selling and a small budget. Plan how I get my first 10 paying customers this month.

**Pick where to start**
Hey Claude, I just added the "first-customers-plan" skill. My scheduling tool could be for dentists, physical therapists or hair salons. Which one should I start with, and where do those owners already ask for help?

**Road to the first 100**
Hey Claude, I just added the "first-customers-plan" skill. I'm a solo founder with 8 hours a week. How long will it take to reach my first 100 customers, and what would make it faster?

**Weekly check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "first-customers-plan" skill. Here's my log from week 1 of the plan. Set the bar, tell me which channel to drop and give me next week's targets.

## What you get

1. **A segment scorecard:** each kind of buyer scored on five factors with the evidence next to every score, the one to start with, and what unlocks the next.
2. **Where those buyers gather:** communities, publications, creators and partner events, each with the evidence that found it and how to show up there as the founder.
3. **What rivals pay for:** which ad channels they use, how long their ads have run and which buyers the ads name.
4. **Targets from your hours:** conversations and paying customers per week, the gap to your goal if there is one, and an estimate of the road to 100.
5. **A dated 30-day plan:** week by week, with the hours on each activity, the people and places it covers, partner pitches, one launch moment and the one thing that must be true by each Sunday.
6. **Stop and scale rules with numbers:** the week-1 bar, when a channel is dropped, when the segment itself is wrong, and when a channel has earned more hours.
7. **A tracker** with the columns to log each week, and tracking links for every channel.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can create a tracker tab in your Google Sheet and fill it with the plan's weekly targets. It never sends a message, posts in a community, contacts a partner or starts an ad: it plans, and you do the selling.

## Tips

- Give honest hours. A plan for 20 hours a week that you work 6 of fails in week 2.
- Only paying customers count. Sign-ups and waitlist names are leads.
- Run the weekly check every Sunday: the starting rates are placeholders until your own week-1 numbers replace them.
- For the messages themselves, use `cold-email-writer` or `linkedin-outreach-writer` if installed; for a Product Hunt day, `launch-strategy`.
