# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "gift-guide-seeding-planner" skill. Plan our holiday gift guide and product seeding push: which products to put forward, who to pitch and by when, and who gets a sample.

## What you need

- **Your products,** with price, unit cost, stock and units sold in last season's peak. Paste them, or connect Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento through the InsightfulPipe MCP and the skill pulls them itself.
- **Last season's gift guides** you want to be in: outlet, guide title, writer, the date it went live and what it featured. With DataForSEO connected, the skill can find the guides that ranked instead.
- **Your lead times** for each kind of outlet (print, online, newsletters and blogs, YouTube), or a dated source for them. Outlets differ, so the skill won't assume one.
- **A sample budget and your cost to pack and post one parcel.**
- **Last season's sample log,** if you kept one. A connected Google Sheet works.

## Example requests

**Holiday gift guides**
Hey Claude, I just added the "gift-guide-seeding-planner" skill. Plan the winter holiday gift guide push: margin floor 55%, sample budget $400, $12 per parcel. Here is our product list with costs and stock, last season's guides and my lead times.

**Getting reviewed by bloggers and YouTubers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "gift-guide-seeding-planner" skill. How do I get our products reviewed by bloggers and YouTube reviewers? Here are three products and the channels I watch. Plan who gets a sample and write the notes.

**A smaller occasion**
Hey Claude, I just added the "gift-guide-seeding-planner" skill. We want to be in Mother's Day gift guides. Here is our product list as a CSV and the five guides we were in last year. What do we pitch, to whom, and by when?

**Measure last season first**
Hey Claude, I just added the "gift-guide-seeding-planner" skill. GA4 and the store are connected. Tell me what each gift guide placement sold last season, then plan this one.

## What you get

1. **Last season's results,** if there was one: each placement's referral sessions, plus orders matched by referring site, tagged landing page or discount code, revenue and revenue per sample dollar.
2. **A product verdict** for every product: put forward, fix first or hold, from margin, price bracket and stock cover, with the reason.
3. **Ranked targets** with each guide's expected publish date, your pitch-by date, whether the window is still open, and the product to pitch.
4. **YouTube reviewers** ranked by the median views of their recent reviews in your category.
5. **A pitch calendar,** earliest deadline first, with a follow-up date for each.
6. **A sample plan and log** inside your budget, with the disclosure each recipient owes: bloggers and YouTubers disclose a gifted product under the FTC rules, and editors at publications follow their outlet's policy.
7. **Drafts:** an editor pitch for each top target, a template for the rest, a reviewer note and a one-page product sheet.
8. **A measurement plan** for each placement, and a list of what couldn't be checked.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact rows, and a Read & Write connection, it can add the sample plan to your seeding log in Google Sheets.

It never sends a pitch or an email, never contacts anyone, and never creates discount codes, changes prices or edits products. It drafts those for you to do.

## Tips

- Run it early. Print guides can close months before they reach readers, so the first deadline is often sooner than you think.
- Ask each editor for their real deadline. The plan's dates are last season's publish dates minus your lead times, and outlets move.
- Give every YouTube reviewer their own discount code and a tagged link for their video description before the sample ships, so their sales can be counted.
- Run it again after the season to see what each placement sold.
