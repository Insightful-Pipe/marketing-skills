# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-comms-plan" skill. Write our crisis communications plan, with holding statements for every scenario that could hit us.

## What you need

- **A short description of the business:** what you sell, to whom, how many customers, whether you hold personal data or sell physical products, and any past incidents.
- **The people:** who leads, who speaks, who gives legal review, and a backup for each. Missing people show up as gaps; the skill never invents a name.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected** to GA4, Search Console, Klaviyo or Mailchimp, Facebook Pages, Instagram, the Social Media Scheduler, Meta Ads or Google Ads. The skill then reads your channel reach, followers, Mailchimp list sizes, the branded search baseline and everything scheduled or running. Brand mention and support ticket counts (and Klaviyo list sizes) are still pasted, since no connector reads them. Without a connection, paste all of it.

## Example requests

**Full plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-comms-plan" skill. Write a crisis communication plan for our online store. Here's our company profile and team.

**Before peak season**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-comms-plan" skill. Black Friday is in four weeks. Build the plan and list every scheduled post, email and ad we would pause if something went wrong.

**Template for a small team**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-comms-plan" skill. We're a 12-person software company with no PR person. Give us a crisis communication plan template we can actually run.

**Statements only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-comms-plan" skill. Draft pre-approved holding statements for an outage, a data breach and a product defect, for our lawyer to review.

## What you get

1. **A risk register:** every scenario scored on likelihood and impact, with the reason for each score.
2. **Three response levels** (Watch, Incident, Crisis) with numeric triggers set from your own baselines for mentions, support tickets and branded search.
3. **The crisis team and sign-off chain,** with the gaps to close.
4. **A first-hour checklist,** time-boxed, with an owner on every step.
5. **A holding statement per scenario,** plus a general one for anything not listed, with fill-in fields, the approver and a legal review flag.
6. **A channel playbook** with the reach of each channel, what each one waits for, and a staff brief.
7. **A pause list** naming the real scheduled posts, emails, flows and ads, and the level that pauses each.
8. **A dated drill schedule.**

## What it won't do

- It reads only. It never pauses, posts or sends anything; the plan names who pauses what.
- It isn't legal advice. Your lawyer reviews every statement before it is approved.
- It prepares; it doesn't run a live incident. If something is happening now, start with the first-hour checklist.

## Tips

- Rerun it every six months, before your busiest season, and after any incident: the pause list changes as campaigns change.
- Give it eight weeks of mention and ticket counts; the triggers are only as good as the baselines.
- Store the plan where people can open it from a phone, with the statements ready to copy.
