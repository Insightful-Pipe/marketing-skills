# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Build me a 30-day marketing plan from my real channels. The goal is more sign-ups, and I have about 10 hours a week.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with the channels you use: GA4 and Search Console at least, plus any of Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Klaviyo and YouTube. For the annual calendar, DataForSEO and Shopify or WooCommerce add market demand and sales by month. The skill pulls the data itself; you don't export anything. With nothing connected, paste your monthly numbers instead.
- **Your goal:** one metric, a target and a date. Without a target it uses +20% as a placeholder and says so.
- **Your hours per week and your paid budget,** so the plan fits what you can actually do.

## Example requests

**30-day plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Build a 30-day plan from my connected channels. Goal: 40 demo requests by the end of next month. Budget $2,000, 8 hours a week.

**90-day growth plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Give me a 90-day growth plan, in three phases, with the test each phase has to pass.

**This week's tasks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. What should I do this week? I have 6 hours. Give me a task list.

**Prioritise my list**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Score these tasks with ICE against my data and tell me which three to do first: new landing page, restart LinkedIn ads, a welcome email, a webinar.

**Conversions dropped**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Our sign-ups fell off a cliff last week. Find when and where it broke, and what to fix first.

**OKRs**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Draft our marketing OKRs for next quarter, with baselines from the real numbers.

**Retrospective**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Run a retrospective on September: what moved, what didn't, and what we keep, stop and start.

**12-month plan and marketing calendar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Build our annual marketing plan for 2027 as a marketing calendar: date the campaigns from our real seasonality, split the $60,000 budget by month, and check that Sam (15 hours a week) and our agency (10 hours a week) can carry it.

**Strategy and frameworks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-plan-builder" skill. Apply STP and the 4Ps to my business using what my search and site data show.

## What you get

1. **A measurement check first:** whether GA4 and your conversions can be trusted. If they can't, fixing that is the first task.
2. **Where you are:** each channel, this period against the last, with real changes told apart from noise.
3. **What the data says:** the few findings that decide the plan, each with its numbers.
4. **The plan:** in the shape you asked for, with every move scored on Impact, Confidence and Ease, and sized in hours to fit your week.
5. **A "Later" list** for the good ideas that don't fit yet.

For an annual plan you also get a seasonality table (each month's index, and its peak, shoulder or quiet label), quarter themes, a dated 12-month marketing calendar, the budget by month and each person's hours by month.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- put the task list or the 12-month calendar in a new tab of your Google Sheet
- save the plan as a Notion page
- mark an event that already fires (like `sign_up`) as a GA4 key event

It never changes budgets, campaigns or emails.

## Tips

- Give it a real target. Plans built on placeholders are weaker.
- Run it again at the end of each period in retrospective mode, with the old plan pasted in.
- Connect every channel you spend time on. Anything it can't read shows up as an unknown, not a guess.
