# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "event-marketing-plan" skill. We have a stand at a trade show in March with a $48,000 budget; plan it from our past shows and tell me if it's worth it next to LinkedIn Ads.

## What you need

- **The event:** name, city, dates, daily open hours, the kind (a stand, a day you host, or a dinner or roundtable for target accounts), and for a stand the meeting tables you'll have.
- **The budget:** the total and the lines you know.
- **Your past events,** one row each: cost, staffed hours, leads, qualified leads, meetings, opportunities, pipeline, outreach sent and meetings booked before the event. A CRM export or an events sheet is fine.
- **Your cost per opportunity from your best other channel.** It decides whether the event is worth it.
- **Your target accounts,** with their LinkedIn page addresses if LinkedIn Ads is connected.
- **Facts you can stand behind** (product claims, customer results, what's new, the stand number). The emails and posts use only these.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** Google Sheets or BigQuery for past events, GA4 for past event links, LinkedIn Ads for which target accounts already see your ads, and, after you confirm, Klaviyo email drafts, a Google Sheets lead tab or an approved LinkedIn Pages post (it publishes at once).

## Example requests

**A trade show stand**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-marketing-plan" skill. Build our trade show marketing plan for the expo on March 9 to 11. Our past shows are in the "Events" sheet and LinkedIn Ads is our comparison channel at $4,900 per opportunity.

**A dinner for target accounts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-marketing-plan" skill. We want to host a dinner for 12 target accounts during next month's conference. How many people should we invite, and what's the follow-up plan?

**Is it worth it?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-marketing-plan" skill. A show is asking $22,000 for a stand. Here are our last three shows from the CRM. Should we book it?

**After the event**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-marketing-plan" skill. The show ended on Thursday. Score these 140 leads, set the follow-up dates and draft the P1 emails.

## What you get

1. **Rates from your past events,** pooled across events of the same kind, with each event's cost per meeting and per opportunity.
2. **Targets with the arithmetic shown:** staffed hours or invitations, leads, qualified leads, meetings, opportunities and pipeline, plus meeting capacity, how many meetings to book ahead and how much outreach that takes.
3. **A budget check and a verdict** (go, go with changes, or rethink) against your best other channel, with the cheapest change worked out.
4. **Target accounts sorted** into engaged, seen or not reached on LinkedIn, each with its outreach step.
5. **A dated timeline** from 16 weeks out to the first event day, with owners.
6. **The stand plan:** shifts, layout and a talk track. For a hosted event, a run of show.
7. **A lead scoring rubric** (P1, P2, P3) and the capture fields.
8. **One tagged link per touchpoint,** with the QR codes marked.
9. **The follow-up schedule** with real dates, and the invitation, follow-up and LinkedIn copy.
10. **Day 30 and end-of-window readouts,** with what each one checks.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- create the invitation or follow-up email as a Klaviyo draft (or a Mailchimp draft, once Mailchimp is connected)
- add a lead capture tab to a Google Sheet you name
- publish an approved post on your LinkedIn company page (it goes live at once, so only on the planned day)

It never sends an email, books space or contacts an attendee.

## Tips

- Tag every link from past events with the event name in `utm_campaign`. Next year's plan reads them back from GA4.
- Score leads at the stand within the hour; the follow-up is only as good as the one-line note.
- Book meetings before the show. Your own past events say how much outreach each booked meeting takes.
