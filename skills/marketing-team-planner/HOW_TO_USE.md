# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-team-planner" skill. Design our marketing team for the next 18 months: who to hire first, what to give to a freelancer or an agency, and what each option costs.

## What you need

- **A short description of the company:** what it sells, its stage and revenue, and the goal (one metric, a target and a date).
- **Your people budget per month,** kept apart from media spend.
- **Your prices:** salary bands for the roles, freelance hourly rates and any agency or fractional-leader quotes you have. The skill never invents a price; an option with no price is marked "needs a quote".
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** with GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads or LinkedIn Ads connected. The skill then measures where your results come from instead of asking. With nothing connected, it works from your description or pasted exports.

## Example requests

**Who to hire first**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-team-planner" skill. We're a founder and one content marketer. Who should our next marketing hire be, and what should stay with freelancers?

**In-house vs agency**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-team-planner" skill. Our agency charges $7,000 a month for paid media. Compare that with hiring in-house and with a freelancer, using our real Google Ads and Meta spend.

**Team for a bigger goal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-team-planner" skill. We plan to double ad spend next year. What team do we need, in what order, and what triggers each hire?

**No connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-team-planner" skill. We're a local services company. Most leads come from Google Ads and referrals, a few from Facebook. Budget for people is $9,000 a month. What should the team look like?

## What you get

1. **Where results come from:** each channel's share of conversions, with brand searches taken out of SEO's credit when Search Console is connected.
2. **Hours needed** per channel at the spend and output you plan, with the formula shown so you can change it.
3. **Roles,** each priced in-house, freelance and agency with your numbers, the choice and the rule that made it.
4. **Cost by channel** for each option.
5. **An org chart** as a text tree and a Mermaid diagram.
6. **A build order** (Now, Next, Later), each step with the number that triggers it.
7. **A budget check,** with the headroom or what was deferred.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your accounts and writes a plan.

## Tips

- Give your planned spend and output for next year, not just today's. The team is sized for where you're going.
- If you know how many hours a channel really takes, say so; your number replaces the starting formula.
- Rerun it when a trigger in the build order is hit, or when the budget changes.
