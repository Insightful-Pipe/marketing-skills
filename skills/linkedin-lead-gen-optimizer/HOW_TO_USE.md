# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-lead-gen-optimizer" skill. Review our LinkedIn lead gen forms: completion rate, cost per lead by campaign and audience, and lead quality.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your LinkedIn Ads account added.** The skill reads your forms, campaigns, ads and results itself; you don't need to export anything from Campaign Manager.
- **Your target cost per lead,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your own blended cost per lead.
- **Who counts as a good lead:** the seniorities, job functions and company sizes you sell to. Without it, the skill uses your campaign targeting.
- **Optional: your lead export or CRM statuses.** LinkedIn's form answers can't be read through the MCP, so to grade individual answers, attach the Campaign Manager lead download or a CRM report. The skill reports counts and shares only, never names or emails.

## Example requests

**Form completion rate**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-lead-gen-optimizer" skill. Where are people dropping off in our LinkedIn lead gen forms? Show me completion rate by form and by ad.

**Cost per lead by audience**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-lead-gen-optimizer" skill. Break down our LinkedIn cost per lead by campaign, seniority, job function, company size and industry. Our target is $120.

**Lead quality review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-lead-gen-optimizer" skill. Here's last quarter's LinkedIn lead export with our CRM statuses. Which campaigns and forms bring leads sales can actually use?

**Draft a new Lead Gen Form campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-lead-gen-optimizer" skill. Draft a Lead Gen Form campaign for our product guide, using the targeting that worked best. Show me every payload before you create anything.

## What you get

1. **A status for six sections:** inventory, form design, completion funnel, cost per lead, lead quality, and the website path for comparison.
2. **A check of every published form:** length, free-text questions, a question to qualify on, work email, privacy policy, the next step after sending, and review status.
3. **The funnel** from impressions to form opens to leads, with open and completion rates by campaign, form and ad.
4. **Cost per lead** by campaign and by audience, with any audience that spends without producing leads.
5. **Lead quality** from work-email share and audience mix, plus your export or CRM statuses when you give them.
6. **A build plan** for a new Lead Gen Form campaign, with the exact payloads, marked "not run" until you say yes.
7. **Up to 5 next moves,** each with the numbers behind it.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payloads, it can create a new campaign group, lead form, Lead Gen Form campaign and ad. The ad reuses the post from your best campaign. If you'd rather use a new image, it can upload one when you give it the file and Claude has a tool that can send files, such as a shell in Claude Code. The campaign is created paused, so nothing serves until you turn it on in Campaign Manager.

It never edits a live form, campaign or ad, and never changes budgets, bids or status.

## Tips

- Run it monthly, and two weeks after any new form goes live.
- If no lead gen campaign has run yet, it still checks your forms and drafts the first campaign from your best-performing targeting.
- Give it your CRM statuses when you can: cost per qualified lead says far more than cost per lead.
