# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "agency-performance-review" skill. Review our marketing agency against the contract and tell me whether we should keep them.

## What you need

- **The agreement:** the contract, statement of work or proposal, or a typed summary of the fee, goals, deliverables and notice period.
- **The agency's reports** for the last three months.
- **Who is who:** the agency's email domain and your own, so changes in Google Ads can be attributed.
- **What the agency doesn't control:** budget cuts you made, site or form changes, and the date you asked for the review.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with your Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console, LinkedIn Ads or Microsoft Ads accounts. Without it, paste exports and the Google Ads Change history download instead.

## Example requests

**Review the current agency**
Hey Claude, I just added the "agency-performance-review" skill. Here's our contract and the last three monthly reports. Is our PPC agency doing a good job? Check Google Ads and Meta.

**Prepare the review meeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "agency-performance-review" skill. We meet our agency next week. Grade them against the contract and write the agenda, with the evidence and the questions to ask.

**Check the reports only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "agency-performance-review" skill. Our agency says we got 700 leads last quarter. Check that against Google Ads, Meta and GA4 and tell me how they count a lead.

**Choose a new agency**
Hey Claude, I just added the "agency-performance-review" skill. We're looking for a new marketing agency. Write the RFP from our real Google Ads and Meta numbers, and a scorecard to compare the pitches.

**Compare pitches**
Hey Claude, I just added the "agency-performance-review" skill. Here are three agency proposals. Score them on the scorecard and flag anything that should rule one out.

## What you get

**In review mode:**
1. **A scorecard for five checks:** results against your goals, how often anyone works the accounts, whether the reports match the platforms and GA4, wasted spend and tracking breaks, and the contracted deliverables.
2. **A fair read:** your budget cuts, seasonality and problems on your side, taken into account before grading.
3. **A verdict:** keep, improvement plan, or start an exit plan, by a stated rule.
4. **The meeting agenda,** with the numbers to show and the questions to ask.
5. **An improvement plan** with owners, dates and exit triggers, or an **exit plan** that keeps your accounts, data and tracking safe.

**In choose mode:** a brief or RFP built on your real numbers, a weighted scorecard fixed before the pitches arrive, the red flags that rule an agency out, and a comparison table.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your accounts and writes the report. It never contacts the agency or removes anyone's access.

## Tips

- Google Ads keeps 30 days of who-changed-what and 90 days of what changed. For older months, download Change history from Google Ads and paste it.
- Tell it the date you asked for the review. A burst of changes right after that date is worth a question.
- If you have CRM numbers (booked jobs, closed deals), paste them. They settle any disagreement between the platforms and GA4.
- Run it again at the plan's checkpoint to see whether the grades moved.
