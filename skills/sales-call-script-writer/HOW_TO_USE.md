# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-call-script-writer" skill. Write a cold calling script, a 30-minute discovery call guide that fills our CRM fields, and a demo flow for each persona, using the call notes I'm pasting.

## What you need

- **Your offer and who you call.** Paste it, or, with the InsightfulPipe MCP connected, the skill reads your home and pricing pages with the site crawler and asks you to confirm.
- **Your qualification fields:** BANT, MEDDICC, or the fields your reps fill in the CRM. Without them, it uses BANT.
- **Past call notes, loss reasons or transcripts.** This is where the branches come from. Without notes, every branch is marked untested.
- **Claims and proof your reps may use.** Anything without a source is written as "[proof needed]".
- **For a call review:** the transcript with speaker names, recorded with consent.

## Example requests

**Cold calling script**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-call-script-writer" skill. Write a cold calling script for our SDRs calling operations managers at logistics firms, with a voicemail. Here are 15 call notes from last month.

**Discovery call questions**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-call-script-writer" skill. Write a 45-minute discovery call guide where every question fills one of our MEDDICC fields, and say what counts as filled.

**Sales demo script**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-call-script-writer" skill. Build a 30-minute demo flow for a finance director and another for an IT lead, from these discovery notes.

**Score a call**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-call-script-writer" skill. Score this discovery call transcript against our guide and tell me what the rep missed, with the lines.

**With buyer questions from search**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-call-script-writer" skill. Pull the questions people search before they find our site from Search Console and work them into the discovery guide.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:** every reply from your notes, how many calls it came up in, and which persona said it. Replies heard in 2 or more calls get a branch; one-offs go on a watch list.
2. **A cold call guide:** openers whose hook carries a count from your own notes and whose question fills a CRM field, with seconds per line (the first question lands within 30 seconds), branches in order of how often you hear them, a meeting ask, a gatekeeper line and a voicemail per persona under 25 seconds.
3. **A discovery call guide:** sections built from your fields, with minutes per field that add up to the call length, a check-in per section, and every question tied to a CRM field with a "filled when" rule and a follow-up for vague answers.
4. **A demo flow per persona:** the pains that persona raises most, what to show for each, a check-in per section, branches and a dated next step.
5. **A call review:** a score out of 100 (fields 60, guide moments 20, next step 20) with the transcript line behind every row, the rep's share of the talking, every claim the rep made checked against your approved claims, and three coaching notes.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads your site, Search Console and CallRail when they're connected, writes guides and scores calls. It never creates calls, contacts or notes anywhere.

## Tips

- Log the reply and the outcome after every call. After 10 more calls, run it again and the branches will reflect what you hear now.
- Score a few calls a week against the same guide, so reps see the same fields every time.
- Check Do Not Call lists, calling hours and recording-consent rules where you call. This skill isn't legal advice.
