# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-account-audit" skill. Audit my Google Ads account and tell me the five fixes worth the most money.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Your target CPA or ROAS,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your own 30-day average.
- **Your brand terms,** if they aren't obvious from your account name.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-account-audit" skill. Run a full audit on my Google Ads account for the last 30 days. My target CPA is $80.

**Taking over an account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-account-audit" skill. I'm taking over this Google Ads account from another agency. Audit it and tell me what's broken before I change anything.

**Performance dropped**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-account-audit" skill. Our Google Ads leads fell this month. Audit the account and show me where the problem is.

**Audit, then fix**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-account-audit" skill. Audit my account, then add the negative keywords you find. Show me the list first.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown.
2. **A scorecard for eight areas:** measurement, wasted spend, structure, keywords and Quality Score, ads and assets, bidding and budget, settings, and change hygiene.
3. **The top 5 fixes,** ranked by the money at stake each month, each with the numbers behind it.
4. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.
5. **Next steps,** pointing to the right skill for a deeper look at each area.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- add negative keywords taken from your own search terms
- add sitelinks
- add callouts

It never changes budgets, bids or bidding strategies.

## Tips

- Run it monthly, and after any big change to tracking or campaign structure.
- If you manage several accounts under one manager account, say which client account to audit.
- Run it again after the fixes to see the grade move.
