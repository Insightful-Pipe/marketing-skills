# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-reach-frequency-planner" skill. Plan a 4-week Facebook and Instagram awareness flight for $18,000 and tell me what frequency and cap to use.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is required.** Paste or upload an Ads Manager export of past awareness ad sets broken down by 7 days (reach, impressions, frequency, amount spent and your response column), plus the audience size Ads Manager shows for the targeting you plan.
- **Better: the Meta Ads MCP server connected through InsightfulPipe.** The skill then pulls your campaigns, ad sets, 7-day insights, the audience estimate and Meta's minimum daily budget itself.
- **The flight:** dates, total budget, audience, and the creatives you have ready.

## Example requests

**Plan a flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-reach-frequency-planner" skill. We launch in Canada on 1 March. Plan a 3-week reach flight for $9,000 aimed at people aged 25 to 44.

**What frequency is right for us**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-reach-frequency-planner" skill. What is a good weekly frequency for our Facebook ads? Use our past awareness campaigns, not an industry rule.

**Auction or reservation**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-reach-frequency-planner" skill. Our media plan promises 1.5 million people reached 3 times. Should we book a reservation campaign or buy at auction?

**Read out a running flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-reach-frequency-planner" skill. Our awareness flight is in week 2. Is frequency too high, and should we change the budget or end date?

## What you get

1. **One-line recommendation:** goal, buying type, weekly target frequency, cap and budget.
2. **Goal choice** between Reach, ThruPlay and ad recall lift, with why the others weren't picked.
3. **Your account's exposure curve:** results per 1,000 people reached at each 7-day frequency band, where results level off, and how much past spend went beyond that point.
4. **The flight plan:** CPM from your own history, weekly impressions and reach, an audience size check, the flight reach range and a daily budget check.
5. **Auction or reservation,** condition by condition.
6. **Brand lift:** where to check whether a study is possible, and the proxy to use if it isn't.
7. **Payloads and Ads Manager steps,** and a read-out date.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- create the awareness campaign and a Reach ad set with the frequency cap (both created paused)
- lower an ad set's budget or move its end date
- pause an ad set

It can't change the cap on an existing ad set, set a weekly target frequency, book a reservation or start a brand lift study through the connector, so it gives you the Ads Manager steps for those.

## Tips

- The curve gets better with every flight. Run the read-out after week 2 and at the end so the next plan uses this flight too.
- Keep the same response metric across flights (for example link clicks), so the curve compares like with like.
- If you only have a few weeks of awareness history, the skill says so and starts from a labelled starting point instead of guessing.
