# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "self-reported-attribution" skill. Check our "how did you hear about us" question, code the last 60 days of answers, and show me what GA4 and our ad platforms miss.

## What you need

- **Your survey answers.** Either paste or upload the export from your post-purchase or signup survey (order or signup id, date, the answer picked, any "Other" text, and the order value if you have it), or connect the store or sheet that holds them through the InsightfulPipe MCP: Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or Google Sheets.
- **GA4, if you want the customer-by-customer comparison.** Connected through InsightfulPipe, or a pasted GA4 export with transaction id and first-user channel.
- **Meta Ads and Google Ads, if you want their claims checked.** Connected, or the purchase and conversion totals typed in.
- **Your existing question,** word for word, with the options in the order shown. Skip this if you don't have one yet.

No answers yet? The skill still writes the question, the answer list and the follow-ups for you.

## Example requests

**Write the question**
Hey Claude, I just added the "self-reported-attribution" skill. We sell skincare on Shopify and spend on Meta, TikTok creators and two podcasts. Write a "how did you hear about us" question for our thank-you page.

**Fix an existing question**
Hey Claude, I just added the "self-reported-attribution" skill. Here's our signup survey question and its options. Tell me what's biased or missing and give me a better version.

**Analyse the answers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "self-reported-attribution" skill. Here's our post-purchase survey export for Q3. Code the answers, including the "Other" text, and give me each channel's share with a margin of error.

**Find what tracking misses**
Hey Claude, I just added the "self-reported-attribution" skill. Join our survey answers to GA4 orders and tell me which channels hide inside Direct, and whether Meta's purchase count matches what customers say.

## What you get

1. **A question audit:** answer order, wording, missing channels, unclear labels, length and placement, each pass or flag with the fix, plus a recommended question with its options and follow-ups.
2. **A coding table and log:** every answer put into one channel, with how it was coded (picked, keyword rule or read) and examples.
3. **Shares with a margin of error:** each channel's share of new customers and of revenue, with a range you can trust.
4. **A check on who answered:** response rate, and whether respondents look like all your new customers.
5. **What GA4 misses:** the match rate, a survey-by-GA4 matrix, what GA4 calls Direct broken down by what customers said, and a verdict per channel.
6. **Platform claims against the survey:** Meta and Google Ads purchases set against the range customers imply, and how often your brand search campaign closes demand made elsewhere (from GA4's campaign on each buyer's session, so non-brand clicks stay out).
7. **Three to five findings and next steps,** including which channel to test before you move budget.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the coded answers to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It never changes your survey, store, checkout or ad accounts; you put the new question live yourself.

## Tips

- Ask about where people **first** heard about you, after the purchase or signup, never before it.
- Aim for about 400 coded answers for a margin of about plus or minus 5 points. With fewer, the skill widens the window or tells you how wide the ranges are.
- Randomise the answer order. Until you do, read the top option's share as an upper bound.
- Rerun every quarter, and after any new channel, sponsorship or creator deal.
