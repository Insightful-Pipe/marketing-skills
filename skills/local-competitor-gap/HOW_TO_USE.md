# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "local-competitor-gap" skill. Find out why other businesses rank above mine on Google Maps for my main services, and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Paste the names or Google Maps links of the businesses that beat you, plus what their listings show (categories, rating, reviews, photos, hours, website) and the same for your own. The skill compares what you give it.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With DataForSEO added, the skill finds who holds the map pack for each keyword and reads every listing the same way. Google Business Profile adds your own categories, hours and review history. The Web Crawler checks the page each listing links to.
- **Up to 3 keywords** your customers search, and the city to search from.
- **The services you really offer,** so it only suggests categories that are true for you.
- **Your last report from this skill,** if you have one. Review pace comes from comparing two runs.

## Example requests

**Why they rank above me**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-competitor-gap" skill. We're a dental practice in Denver. Why do three other dentists beat us in the map pack for "emergency dentist" and "teeth whitening"?

**Category research**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-competitor-gap" skill. Which Google Business Profile categories do the top plumbers near me use that I don't?

**Check a rival listing**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-competitor-gap" skill. A competitor's Google listing is called "Best Roofer Austin 24/7". Does it break Google's rules, and how do I report it?

**From a paste**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-competitor-gap" skill. Here are the three salons that show above us on Google Maps, with their ratings, review counts, categories and hours. Compare them with our profile below.

**Second run**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-competitor-gap" skill. Run the same comparison as last month and tell me who is gaining reviews faster. Here's last month's report.

## What you get

1. **The map pack for each keyword,** with your position or "not in pack".
2. **A side-by-side table** of you and every business above you: categories, rating, reviews, review pace, photos, hours, claimed status and website signals.
3. **Gaps ranked by likely impact,** High, Medium and Low, each with your number, the number you're up against, the fix and where to make it.
4. **Where you're ahead or level,** so you don't fix what isn't broken.
5. **Category research:** which categories nearby businesses in your field use that you don't.
6. **Rival listing checks:** listings that appear to break Google's guidelines, with the evidence and how to report them, and shared addresses to check first.

## What it changes

Nothing. It is read only. Each fix tells you where to make it: your Business Profile, your website, or how you ask customers for reviews.

## Tips

- Use the searches customers really type, not your brand name.
- Rerun in about 30 days with the same keywords and city to see review pace and whether the gaps closed.
- Report a rival only with the evidence the skill shows. Outranking you is not a violation.
