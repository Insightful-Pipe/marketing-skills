# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. We missed this month's leads target for a client. Write the email to their CEO explaining why, with the numbers, and what we need them to decide.

## What you need

- **Who will read it and where:** the founder, the marketing manager or finance, and email, Slack or a call.
- **The target and how it is counted,** plus the return the client works to (target ROAS or cost per result).
- **What was agreed recently:** budget changes, promotions, site releases, anything the client asked for.
- **The facts, one of two ways:**
  - paste a report, platform exports or an earlier analysis, with no connection needed; or
  - connect the client's Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console or LinkedIn Ads through the InsightfulPipe MCP and the skill pulls the numbers itself.
- **For a scope note:** the scope or contract wording, your hours estimate and your rate.
- **For a reply:** the client's message, pasted in full.

## Example requests

**Missed target or drop update**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. Leads fell 30% this month for our dental client. Write a short Slack update for their marketing manager that says what we know, what we don't, and what we're doing.

**Budget request**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. I think this client should spend more on Google Ads. Check whether the next dollar would still pay off, and if it does, write the budget request for their founder as a test with a stop rule.

**Pushback on a client idea**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. The client wants to pause the brand campaign to save money. Write a reply that shows what that would cost and offers a safer way to test it.

**Scope change or extra fee**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. The client keeps asking for landing page edits that aren't in our scope. Here's the contract wording. Write a note offering three options, at our rate of $110 an hour.

**Renewal or upsell**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. Our 6-month contract ends next month. Write the renewal email with results against where we started, and pitch LinkedIn Ads only if the numbers back it.

**Reply to an upset client**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. Here's an angry email from a client saying we wasted their budget. Check each claim against the accounts and draft a calm reply.

**Call agenda and talking points**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-conversation-writer" skill. Prep me for a 30-minute call with the client on Thursday: agenda, what to say on each point, the questions they'll ask with answers, and the decisions we need.

## What you get

1. **A fact sheet:** every number the messages may use, with its source and dates.
2. **Every possible cause graded** confirmed, likely, ruled out or can't tell. Only confirmed causes are written as causes.
3. **A gap bridge** that shows how the shortfall splits between causes, and adds up.
4. **A budget case** built on what the next dollar buys in this account, with the season taken out, a ceiling, and a stop rule. If the next dollar wouldn't pay off, you get a "hold the budget" message instead of an ask.
5. **The drafts,** sized for the reader and the channel, each with the facts it uses.
6. **Send checks** on each draft: numbers traced, no promises, no blame, one clear decision.
7. **Open questions** and what would settle each one.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It writes drafts for you to edit and send. It never sends an email or a Slack message, and never changes a client's account.

## Tips

- Paste the client's exact words when you have them. The reply and pushback drafts answer what they actually said.
- Tell it what you already fixed. A message only says something was done when you confirm it was.
- The sample on this page is invented: a garden store that got 304 paid orders against a target of 470 in October. The draft splits the gap into the budget cut (about 88), two days of missing tracking (about 22), the season (43) and normal swing (13). It then asks for a 14-day Shopping test, because the next dollar still returns $4.60 against a $4 floor.
