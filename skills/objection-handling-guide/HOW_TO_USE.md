# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "objection-handling-guide" skill. Build our sales objection handling guide from these deal notes and lost reasons, and tell me which objections cost us the most pipeline.

## What you need

- **Your evidence:** call notes, transcripts or a CRM export with each deal's value, outcome and lost reason. Paste them or upload a file. No connection is needed.
- **Your proof list:** case studies, ratings, contract terms, comparison pages and calculators, each with a link. Every reply points to one of them, or says which proof is still missing and what to collect.
- **Your product's true limits,** such as an integration you don't have, so the guide never argues with a buyer who is right.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with your Facebook Page, Instagram, LinkedIn Page, YouTube channel, Google Business Profile or Search Console connected. The skill then reads the comments, reviews and search queries itself. App Store and Google Play reviews of your own app work too.

## Example requests

**From CRM notes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "objection-handling-guide" skill. Here is our CRM export for the last six months with notes and lost reasons. Write the objection handling guide for the sales team.

**One objection keeps coming up**
Hey Claude, I just added the "objection-handling-guide" skill. Buyers keep saying our annual contract is too long. Check our notes and comments and write the reply, with proof we can show.

**Before a sales kickoff**
Hey Claude, I just added the "objection-handling-guide" skill. Build the objection handling script for our reps from our call transcripts, ranked by how much pipeline each objection touched.

**For the website**
Hey Claude, I just added the "objection-handling-guide" skill. Read the comments on our Facebook and YouTube posts and our Search Console queries, and tell me which objections our pricing page should answer before anyone books a call.

## What you get

1. **A ranked list of objections,** each with the number of deals and public voices behind it, the pipeline it touched, and how often it lost the deal against your overall loss rate.
2. **Deal killers and product gaps,** called out so you know which objections lose deals and which ones are simply true.
3. **An answer card for each objection:** the buyer's own words, what is fair in it, one question back, one proof point with its link (or the proof still to collect), the next step and a reply of 60 words or less.
4. **What to answer before the call,** and where: the pricing page, a comparison page, an ad or a one-pager.
5. **Objections heard once, and proof gaps:** the objections only one deal or voice raised, and the proof you still need to collect.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your evidence and writes the guide. It does not reply to comments or reviews or edit your CRM.

## Tips

- Include won deals as well as lost ones. Without them, the guide can't tell which objections your reps already handle.
- Quote the buyer in your notes when you can. Quotes become the "their words" line on each card.
- Run it each quarter, or after a price or packaging change, and compare the ranking.
