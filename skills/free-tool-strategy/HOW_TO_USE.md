# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "free-tool-strategy" skill. We sell [your product] to [your buyers]. Which free tool or lead magnet should we build first to bring in leads from search, and how?

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with DataForSEO added.** The skill finds and measures the searches itself; you don't need to export keyword data.
- **Search Console and GA4 (optional).** With them, it also checks which tool searches your site already shows for, which of your pages already turn visitors into leads, and which events you already track.
- **A sentence on what you sell and who buys it.** The jobs your buyers do before they need you help too; without them, the skill drafts the list for you to correct.
- **Your customer lifetime value and lead-to-customer rate,** if you want a return estimate. Without them, you get the formula.

## Example requests

**Pick the first tool**
Hey Claude, I just added the "free-tool-strategy" skill. We make scheduling software for dental clinics. Find the calculator or generator worth building first, and tell me what to skip.

**Validate one idea**
Hey Claude, I just added the "free-tool-strategy" skill. We're thinking about a "marketing budget calculator". Is there demand, who already ranks, and can we win?

**Lead magnet ideas**
Hey Claude, I just added the "free-tool-strategy" skill. Give me lead magnet ideas for our B2B accounting firm, with the search demand behind each, and tell me which ones to put behind an email form.

**Gating decision**
Hey Claude, I just added the "free-tool-strategy" skill. We have an ROI calculator. Should the results sit behind an email form, or stay open?

## What you get

1. **Demand found** around your buyers' problems, with each search counted once.
2. **An idea table:** demand, CPC, seasonality, who ranks (and how many weak results there are), and how many links the ranking tools earned.
3. **A scorecard out of 40** for each idea: three factors measured, five judged with a stated reason, plus the kill criteria.
4. **A clear call:** build first, build next, and why the others lost.
5. **A strategy for the winner:** format and gating backed by the results page, build scope, the tool page plan with its FAQ, GA4 events, success metrics, promotion and risks.
6. **Lead magnet ideas:** templates and checklists with their search demand and where each captures an email.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can mark the tool's lead event as a GA4 key event. It never builds the tool or edits your site.

## Tips

- About 11 DataForSEO calls per run. It asks before going past 15.
- Seed with your buyers' problems, not your product category: "invoice" and "net 30", not "invoicing software".
- Run it again 60 to 90 days after launch with Search Console connected to see which phrasings the tool picked up.
