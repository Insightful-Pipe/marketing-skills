# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "repeat-purchase-planner" skill. Here is my orders export for the last 24 months and my products with unit costs. How do I increase my repeat purchase rate, and should I start a subscribe and save program?

## What you need

- **Your orders for the last 24 months** (12 at the least). An export from Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento works, and so does any sheet with the order date, a customer id or email, the status, the items and the subtotal. If your store or Klaviyo is connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, the skill pulls the orders itself.
- **Your products** with price, unit cost, product line and size (sample, full size, refill, or a one-off such as a gift or an accessory).
- **Two costs:** what a regular order costs you to ship and pack after the shipping fee customers pay, and what a subscription box would cost to send on its own.
- **The flows you run now** (post-purchase, replenishment, win-back) and their delays, so the skill can check their timing.

## Example requests

**Second orders**
Hey Claude, I just added the "repeat-purchase-planner" skill. Most of our customers buy once. Use our orders to show which first products bring people back and which don't, and what we should put in the box.

**Subscribe and save**
Hey Claude, I just added the "repeat-purchase-planner" skill. We're thinking of a subscribe and save program at 15% off. Which products should be on it, at what cadence, and does 15% pay?

**Refill reminders**
Hey Claude, I just added the "repeat-purchase-planner" skill. When should our refill reminders go out for each product? Our win-back email goes at 45 days, check that too.

**Connected store**
Hey Claude, I just added the "repeat-purchase-planner" skill. Pull 24 months of orders from Shopify and plan our repeat purchases. Shipping a regular order costs us about $3 after the fee.

## What you get

1. **Your repeat customer rate and second-order rate,** inside a return window set from your own orders, and when second orders land.
2. **Which first products lead to a second order** and which are dead ends, plus your sample-to-full-size and refill paths.
3. **Each product's reorder interval,** from at least 20 repeat buyers.
4. **A subscribe and save check:** which products qualify, the cadence and shipment size, and the discount that still beats a loyal regular buyer's margin.
5. **Reminder timing for your email flows,** and a check of the flows you already run.
6. **Inserts and a second-order code,** each with the lift it needs to break even, a target second-order rate, and a plan in order.

## What it can change for you

On WooCommerce, with your yes on the exact change, it can create a one-per-customer second-order coupon with an expiry. It never creates subscriptions, flows or discounts on Shopify or Magento; those come as steps for your store admin, your subscription app and your email tool.

## Tips

- Twenty-four months gives the steadiest return window: the skill needs a year of follow-up for its own 80th percentile.
- If you already sell subscriptions, give the app's retention per month: the discount check uses it instead of the defaults.
- Make one change at a time and keep a small holdout, so the second-order rate tells you what worked.
