# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-specs-checker" skill. Check my active Meta ads against every placement they run in and tell me what to crop, shorten or switch off.

## What you need

- **Either your files or your ad account.** Upload the images, video frames or screenshots, and paste the primary text and headline. Or connect your Meta ad account through the InsightfulPipe MCP, and the skill reads each ad's image size, video shape and length, copy and placements itself.
- **Where the ads run:** automatic placements, or the list you picked. With the account connected, the skill reads this from each ad set.
- **A frame of every vertical video,** if you want the safe zones checked: the opening, the moment captions or a logo appear, and the end card. Claude can read where text sits in an image, but it can't play a video.
- **For a video from your files, its length in seconds.**

## Example requests

**Before launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-specs-checker" skill. Here are the four images and the video frame for next week's campaign. Will they work in Feeds, Stories and Reels?

**Facebook ad sizes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-specs-checker" skill. What size should my Facebook and Instagram ads be, and which of my current ones are the wrong shape?

**Instagram Reels safe zone**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-specs-checker" skill. Check this Reels frame: is my caption or logo covered by Instagram's buttons?

**Live account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-specs-checker" skill. Go through my active Meta ads and tell me which placements each one fails, with the crop to make.

**Text cut-off**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-specs-checker" skill. Which of my ads have primary text or headlines that get cut off, and where?

## What you get

1. **A grid** of every ad against every placement it can run in: pass, warn or fail, with the reason (small, cropped, soft, cards, cut, covered).
2. **Fixes, most serious first:** the exact crop box in pixels or the size to export, the cut for Stories, the text to shorten, and the element to move.
3. **Safe zones** for each 9:16 asset in its own pixels, what sits inside them, and where the safe area is.
4. **Text cut-off** per placement, quoting what viewers see before "...".
5. **Preview links** for the ad and placement pairs that need a look by eye (up to 20 per run), to open on a phone-sized window. Facebook Reels has no preview format, so use the Instagram Reels preview or Ads Manager for it.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can switch placements off for one ad set. It shows you first which other ads in that ad set lose those placements, and it recommends a new version instead whenever one can be made. It never uploads, crops or edits your images, videos or copy, and never switches anything on.

## Tips

- Upload the frames that carry text. A clean opening shot tells you little about a caption that appears at 0:04.
- A square or landscape image rarely crops well to 9:16; export a vertical version from the design file.
- Meta updates its specs. The report names the date of the specs it used.
