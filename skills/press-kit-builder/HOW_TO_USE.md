# How to use Press Kit Builder

Hey Claude, I just added the "press-kit-builder" skill. Build our company press kit and check our press page: fact sheet, boilerplate, bios, logos, coverage and releases.

## What you need

- Your company facts: founding date, headquarters, team size, what you sell to whom, and any public funding.
- The people in the kit: names, titles, a few career facts, headshots, and who may speak to the press.
- A press email that someone reads, and the numbers you want to quote, each with where it comes from.
- Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:
  - the Web Crawler server, to audit your live press page and every asset link
  - DataForSEO, to find news and blog coverage of your company
  - GA4 or Shopify, to check traffic, order and customer claims before they go in the kit
  - WordPress, to save the finished page as a draft

No connection at all? Paste your facts, links and press page text, and Claude builds the same kit from them.

## Other ways to ask

- "Write our company boilerplate in a short, medium and long version."
- "Audit our /press page: broken logo links, old numbers, missing contact."
- "Make a press kit template for our launch next month."
- "Build an 'as covered in' list from articles that mention us."
- "Write press bios for our two founders."

## What you get

1. An audit of your current press page, if you have one: contact, asset links, broken links, dates, freshness, markup.
2. A table of your key numbers, each with its date and source, and whether it is stale, overstated or ready.
3. The kit: fact sheet, boilerplate in 25, 50 and 100 words, press bios, product descriptions, spokespeople and contact, an asset checklist with usage rules, the coverage list and the release archive.
4. Organization and NewsArticle markup, ready to paste.
5. A short list of what still needs you, and anything that couldn't be checked.

## What it can change for you

Nothing goes live. Only if you say yes to the exact page, it saves the newsroom page as a draft in WordPress for you to review and publish. That needs a Read & Write connection, and an owner, admin or operator can switch it off.

## Tips

- Give every number a date. "12,000 customers" from two years ago is the claim journalists check first.
- Name one person as the press contact and promise a reply time you can keep. A reporter on deadline decides in minutes whether to wait for you.
- Run it again before a launch or a funding announcement, so the numbers and coverage are current.
