# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "rebrand-planner" skill. We're thinking about renaming the company. Tell me whether we should, and plan it from the decision to the weeks after launch.

## What you need

- **Your reasons for changing,** with any evidence: how your customer or revenue mix has shifted, complaints about the name, a legal notice, a merger.
- **The new name and launch date,** if you have them, and whether the domain changes too.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With Search Console, Bing, DataForSEO, Google Ads and GA4 added, the skill measures your brand search itself. With Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Merchant Center, the app stores, Klaviyo or Mailchimp added, it reads every profile and listing that carries your name. Without connections, it works from what you paste.

## Example requests

**Should we rebrand at all?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rebrand-planner" skill. Our name says "salons" but half our new customers are clinics. Is that a reason to rename, or should we just refresh the logo and messaging?

**Full rebrand plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rebrand-planner" skill. We're renaming to Agendaly and moving to agendaly.example on 1 December. Build the rebrand checklist from our connected accounts and schedule it backwards from launch day.

**Check the new name**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rebrand-planner" skill. Before we commit to the new name, check who already ranks for it and how much search it gets.

**How is the rename going?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rebrand-planner" skill. We launched the new name six weeks ago. How much of our brand search has moved to it, and are we losing anyone?

## What you get

1. **The decision:** keep, refresh, reposition, rename, or rename with a new domain, with each of your reasons tested against evidence and what a rename puts at risk in branded clicks.
2. **A name check:** search volume for the old and new names, who already uses the new name in search, and the gates to clear first (trademark, domain, handles).
3. **The asset inventory:** every connected profile, listing, ad account and email sender that still shows the old name, plus what no connector can see.
4. **A work plan scheduled backwards from launch day** by each item's lead time, with anything already late flagged.
5. **The announcement order:** who hears first, and when.
6. **A baseline and targets** for the handover, then a **weekly report** after launch: how much brand search has moved to the new name, and whether total brand demand is holding.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can add the new name as brand keywords in your Google Ads brand ad group, so the new name has paid cover from day one. Everything else (profile names, handles, listings, ad text, sender names) you change in each platform; the skill tells you the exact field and when.

## Tips

- Run the plan at least 8 weeks before launch: some items take that long.
- Keep "formerly" with the old name on your site and profiles for the whole bridge period the plan sets.
- Ask for the weekly report every Monday after launch until the handover target is met.
