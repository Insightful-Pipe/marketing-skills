# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "media-pitch-writer" skill. Write a media pitch for each journalist on my list about our new report, with two follow-ups each.

## What you need

- **The story,** in a few lines: what happened or what you found, and the date it goes public, if there is one.
- **Your media list:** names, outlets, emails, and whether each one gets a news pitch, a contributed article pitch, a TV or radio segment pitch or a local pitch. Paste it, upload a CSV or point to a Google Sheet.
- **Your proof:** the numbers and customers you can stand behind, or a connected GA4 property or Shopify store to pull them from.
- **Past pitches,** if you have them, so nobody is pitched twice in a month.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP. With DataForSEO connected and funded, the skill looks up each writer's recent articles itself; with GA4 or Shopify connected, it pulls the numbers; with Google Sheets connected, it reads your list and can save the tracker.

## Example requests

**Pitch a report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-pitch-writer" skill. Our report goes public on the 10th at 9am ET. Pitch it to the 8 writers in this sheet, offer the first one an exclusive, and give me the follow-ups.

**Op-ed and local press**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-pitch-writer" skill. Pitch an op-ed by our founder to these two opinion editors, and pitch our local paper and morning show on the city numbers.

**Answer a reporter's request**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-pitch-writer" skill. A reporter is asking for experts on reducing no-shows, deadline Thursday 5pm. Write my reply.

**Update the tracker**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-pitch-writer" skill. Two writers replied and one passed. Here are the emails. Update the tracker and tell me what's due this week.

## What you get

1. **A list check:** who is ready, who is on hold (pitched in the last 30 days, declined, asked not to be pitched, or no recent on-topic article) and when they can be pitched again.
2. **A hook record per writer:** the recent article the pitch names, with its link, date and how the byline was confirmed.
3. **A proof ledger:** every number in every pitch with its source, date range and method, so a journalist can check it.
4. **The terms:** who gets an exclusive, who gets an embargo, and the dates that follow from them.
5. **Each pitch:** two subject lines, a body of about 150 words with one clear ask, two follow-ups with their dates, and a printed check (length, one story, no attachments, numbers, spam triggers).
6. **Replies to calls for sources,** written for the deadline.
7. **A tracker** of drafted, sent, replied, declined and covered.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the tracker as a new tab in your Google Sheet. It never sends email.

## Tips

- One story per run. A second story gets its own run and its own pitches.
- Name your first-choice writer if you want to offer an exclusive.
- Paste replies back in as they arrive; the tracker and due dates update, and follow-ups stop for anyone who answered.
