# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Build last month's marketing report from all my connected sources and tell me what moved.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your sources added:** ad accounts (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft, Pinterest, Snapchat, X or OpenAI Ads), GA4, Search Console, Bing Webmaster and Klaviyo, whichever you use. The skill finds them and pulls the data itself; you don't export anything.
- **Who the report is for,** if it isn't you: a client, your boss, the board or the team. That decides the shape.
- **The one result that matters** (leads, sign-ups, purchases or revenue) and a target cost per result, if you have one.
- **No connection?** Paste or upload your platform exports, a past report or a campaign brief instead. The same checks run on what you give it.

## Example requests

**Weekly report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Give me last week's marketing report across every channel, against the week before.

**Monthly client report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Write the September client report for this brand from all connected sources. Keep it client-friendly.

**Executive one-pager**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. I need a one-page KPI summary of last month for our CEO. Five numbers, three sentences.

**Board slides**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Outline six board slides on this quarter's marketing results, with speaker notes.

**Quarterly business review deck**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Build our Q3 QBR deck for the client: results against the goals in our targets sheet, against Q2 and Q3 last year, the tests we ran, next quarter's roadmap and the budget ask. One chart per slide, speaker notes, as a pptx in their logo and colours.

**Campaign wrap-up**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Write the wrap-up for our "Spring Sale" campaign (March 3 to 30) across Google Ads, Meta, Klaviyo and our Facebook page. Here's the brief with the targets. Include the lift against the weeks before launch and a learnings paragraph for the next brief.

**Batch client reports**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Run the October report for every client in our agency sheet, each in its own logo and colours, with a work-done section from the account change history. Hold back anyone whose data looks broken, give me a cover sheet of who needs attention, and draft the send emails.

**Team stand-up**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Write this Monday's stand-up brief for the marketing team. Under 150 words.

**Explain a report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-performance-report" skill. Here's the report our agency sent. Explain it in plain English and check the numbers against my accounts.

## What you get

1. **A source list:** every connected ad, analytics, search and email account, marked active, idle, broken, unknown or left out, plus one line naming the organic social sources a regular report doesn't read, so nothing is silently skipped. The campaign wrap-up also reads your Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn page posts for the campaign.
2. **Trust checks before the story:** whether GA4 agrees with Search Console, whether ad clicks reach your site, and whether tracking broke mid-period.
3. **What moved and why,** with the number behind every claim and no % changes on numbers too small to mean anything.
4. **The report in the shape you asked for:** weekly report, monthly client report, executive one-pager, stand-up brief, a QBR, annual or board deck, a campaign wrap-up, or a batch of white-label client reports, in plain English or as a narrative.

## What it changes

Nothing in your ad, analytics, search or email accounts. If you ask, it can save the report's tables to a Google Sheet tab or post an approved client note to Slack, each only after you confirm.

## Tips

- If one brand holds several businesses (an agency's clients, for example), say which one the report is for. The skill never mixes two businesses in one report.
- Reconnect any source the report lists as broken, then run it again.
- Run the weekly shape on Mondays and the monthly one on the 3rd, so GA4 has finished processing the last days.
