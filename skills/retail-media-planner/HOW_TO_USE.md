# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "retail-media-planner" skill. Here are last month's exports from Walmart Connect, Instacart Ads and Roundel. Tell me which network is really working and how to split next month's budget.

## What you need

- **One export per network for the same dates:** Walmart Connect, Instacart Ads, Roundel, or any other retail network you run. Item level is best, plus the search term report. Paste it, upload a CSV or XLSX, or point to the Google Sheet or BigQuery table you keep them in.
- **Your sales at each retailer** for the same dates, at retail price.
- **What a retail dollar is worth to you at each retailer:** what you were paid, what the goods cost, and any trade funding. Without it, the skill grades against your target only.
- **Next period's budget,** and your target ROAS and new-to-brand goal if you have them.

## Example requests

**Split the budget**
Hey Claude, I just added the "retail-media-planner" skill. Split $60,000 for November across Walmart Connect, Instacart Ads and Roundel. Our sales by retailer and margins are in the sheet.

**Which network is really working**
Hey Claude, I just added the "retail-media-planner" skill. Roundel shows a 6x ROAS and Walmart shows 5x. Put them on the same terms and tell me which one actually earns more.

**Items and search terms**
Hey Claude, I just added the "retail-media-planner" skill. From these item and search term reports, tell me which products to push or pause on each retailer, and which winning terms to copy to the other networks.

**Starting on a new retailer**
Hey Claude, I just added the "retail-media-planner" skill. We sell at Target but don't advertise there yet. Plan a test budget alongside our Walmart and Instacart ads.

## What you get

1. **How each network counts,** in one table: window, model, items, view-through and in-store.
2. **One scale:** every network's ROAS rebuilt on the same terms, next to its headline, its break-even and your target, with a grade.
3. **A budget split** from your sales by retailer, with the base, the multiplier, the allowed range and the reason for each number.
4. **Item decisions per network:** push, keep, fix or pause, hold for stock, or add.
5. **Search terms:** winners to copy to other networks, negatives, and a watch list.
6. **The top findings,** ranked by the spend they affect, and what couldn't be compared.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can save the plan to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It never changes anything in Walmart Connect, Instacart Ads, Roundel or any other network: every change is listed for you to make there.

## Tips

- Use a period that ended at least 14 days ago, so late attributed sales are in.
- If a network ran a lift study for you, add its result; it's the only way incremental sales enter the plan.
- Running Amazon Ads too? Add its export and sales the same way, and it joins the split.
