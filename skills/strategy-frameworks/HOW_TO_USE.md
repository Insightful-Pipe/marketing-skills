# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "strategy-frameworks" skill. Run Porter's Five Forces, a PESTLE analysis and an Ansoff matrix on our market, and tell me what it changes in our marketing plan.

## What you need

- **Your market and product:** what you sell, to whom, and in which country.
- **2 to 4 searches that name your category,** such as "appointment scheduling software". If you don't know them, the skill proposes some and asks you to confirm.
- **A few answers about your suppliers:** what your product can't run without, whether it charges per unit, and which channel brings most of your new customers. Supplier power is scored only from these.
- **Growth ideas, if you have them:** countries, customer segments or products you are weighing.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude with DataForSEO, the ads libraries and Search Console. Without it, paste a keyword export and the top results for your main searches, and the skill runs the same rules on what you paste.

## Example requests

**All three frameworks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "strategy-frameworks" skill. Run Five Forces, PESTLE and Ansoff on the US market for our booking app and give me the plan changes.

**Five Forces only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "strategy-frameworks" skill. Do a Porter's Five Forces analysis of the online invoicing software market in the UK. Show me the evidence behind each force.

**PESTLE before a launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "strategy-frameworks" skill. We're about to launch in Canada. Run a PESTLE analysis for us, and mark anything you can't source as an assumption.

**Where to grow next**
Hey Claude, I just added the "strategy-frameworks" skill. Build an Ansoff matrix for us: we're weighing the UK, a gym segment and a point-of-sale add-on. Which one leads?

**No connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "strategy-frameworks" skill. Here is a keyword export and the top 10 results for our three main searches. Run Five Forces on it.

## What you get

1. **In short:** the strongest pressures, the lead growth move and the biggest plan change.
2. **Five Forces:** each force's pressure as "N of M measured signals" (supplier power: "N of M signals, from you"), every signal's value next to its threshold and source, and the plan changes it implies.
3. **PESTLE:** six rows, each with one specific factor, whether it helps or hurts, its evidence, source and date, and whether it is measured, from you or an assumption.
4. **Ansoff matrix:** growth moves in four quadrants, each with demand, competition and reachable demand, and a verdict: lead move, test next or park.
5. **Up to 6 plan changes in one list,** led by the Ansoff lead move, each naming its evidence.
6. **Assumptions and unknowns,** with what would settle each one, and an evidence log of every call or pasted source.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It only reads data and writes a report.

## Tips

- Search data shows how buyers look for a product, not market share or revenue. Use it for marketing decisions, not as a market size.
- If a threshold doesn't fit your business (a local service, a market with a handful of buyers), say so and the skill states the one it used instead.
- Give dated sources for any regulation or economic fact you already know; undated facts are treated as assumptions.
- Rerun it each quarter to see which forces are moving.
