# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-coverage-report" skill. Build our PR report for last month: find the coverage, compare us with our three main competitors and tell us what worked.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, at minimum.** Paste a coverage list or upload an export from your monitoring tool or Google Alerts (date, outlet or URL, headline, passage), plus a GA4 export of sessions and key events by source if you want outcomes.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with DataForSEO, GA4 and Search Console added. The skill then finds the coverage and pulls traffic, branded search, links and AI answer mentions itself. Bing Webmaster is optional, and Google Sheets is only for saving the log.
- **Your brief:** brand and product names, spokespeople, up to three competitors, your key messages and what you pitched this period, each with a few signal phrases. A media list with tiers and audiences makes tiers and reach exact.

## Example requests

**Monthly PR report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-coverage-report" skill. Build our October PR report against Competitor A, B and C. Our messages are "deposits cut no-shows" and "built for small businesses".

**Weekly media monitoring**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-coverage-report" skill. Run this week's media monitoring sweep and tell me if anything needs an answer today.

**Campaign results**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-coverage-report" skill. We put out a data study and a launch release this month. Which one actually did something for the business?

**From an export**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pr-coverage-report" skill. Here's our monitoring export and a GA4 referral export. Turn them into a PR report we can show the leadership team.

## What you get

1. **Alerts first:** a negative story in a top-tier outlet, a false claim (with a correction request drafted), or a week with more than twice the usual mentions, each with the next step.
2. **A scorecard:** stories, tier 1 stories, reach when you supply audiences, sentiment, quote rate, message pull-through, share of voice, AI answer share, referral visits and key events, branded search lift and new links.
3. **A coverage log:** one row per story, with syndicated copies grouped, and the visits, sign-ups and link status that came with it.
4. **Share of voice** against your competitors, counted the same way for every brand.
5. **What worked, what to stop and next month's targets,** each tied to numbers.
6. **What was screened out and what couldn't be measured,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it saves the coverage log to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It never sends anything: replies, statements and correction requests are drafted for you to send.

## Tips

- Keep the same competitors and signal phrases from month to month, so the numbers compare.
- Stories from the last 7 days are logged but judged next month, once the traffic and links have had time to show.
- It won't give you an advertising value equivalent (AVE). If your leadership asks for one, the skill explains why in one or two sentences and offers the outcome measures instead.
