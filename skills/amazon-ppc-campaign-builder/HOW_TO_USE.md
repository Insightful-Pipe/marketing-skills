# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-campaign-builder" skill. Here are my new product's listing, costs, my Brand Analytics search terms and three competitor listings. Build the Amazon PPC launch campaigns and give me the bulk sheet.

## What you need

- **The product:** ASIN, SKU, listing title and bullets, and how many units you'll have in stock at launch.
- **Your costs per unit:** price, cost of goods, referral fee, FBA fee and anything else (returns, storage, inbound shipping). This is how every bid is worked out.
- **Your goal:** a launch (the highest ACOS you'll accept, for how many weeks, and your target after) or straight profit (a target ACOS).
- **Your daily budget** for the whole launch.
- **Keyword sources,** as many as you have: a Brand Analytics Top Search Terms or Search Query Performance export, competitor listings (ASIN, title, price, rating) and your own listing.
- **Suggested bids** you copy from the Amazon Ads console, if you can. Without them each bid starts at its own ceiling.
- **Your conversion rate** (unit session percentage) from the Business Report. For a new product, your other products' rates work.

Paste it, upload CSV or XLSX files, or point to the Google Sheet you keep it in.

## Example requests

**Product launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-campaign-builder" skill. We launch a new kitchen scale on amazon.com next week. We'll accept 45% ACOS for six weeks, then 28%. Budget is $90 a day. Build the campaign structure and the bulk sheet.

**Amazon ads for a book**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-campaign-builder" skill. I'm a KDP author with a new fantasy ebook at $4.99 and a $3.44 royalty. Set up my Amazon ads: keywords, comparable books to target, bids and a $15 daily budget.

**Relaunch with an existing account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-campaign-builder" skill. Here's our current bulk sheet. We're relaunching our yoga mat after a stock-out. Build a clean launch structure for it without duplicating what's already live.

**How should I set this up**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-campaign-builder" skill. How should I set up a Sponsored Products campaign for my first product? Here are my price, costs and the suggested bids Amazon shows me.

## What you get

1. **What an order is worth:** break-even ACOS, the launch cost per ad order, and the highest cost per click each goal allows.
2. **The keyword pool:** every candidate sorted into exact, phrase, broad, brand, wrong fit (which become negatives) or left out, with the reason.
3. **The structure:** exact, phrase, broad and auto Sponsored Products campaigns, competitor ASIN and category targeting, brand defense, and Sponsored Brands when your brand qualifies.
4. **A bid for every target** with the math, the bid it moves to when the launch ends, and a flag where Amazon's suggested range is above what you can afford.
5. **A budget split** that gives every campaign enough clicks to judge in two weeks, with the worst-case launch cost and a stock check.
6. **The negatives** that keep each search in one campaign.
7. **An upload-ready bulk sheet** and a Sponsored Brands setup sheet, plus a dated review plan for day 14, day 30 and the end of the launch.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can write the bulk sheet to a new tab in a Google Sheet, ready to download and upload. It never uploads anything or changes your Amazon Ads account.

## Tips

- Give it your real FBA fee from the fee preview; a guessed fee moves every bid.
- Paste competitor prices and ratings: it only targets competitor pages where your product wins the side-by-side.
- Check the bulk sheet against a freshly downloaded template before you upload, and fill in the category id if it asks for one.
