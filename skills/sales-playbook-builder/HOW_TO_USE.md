# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-playbook-builder" skill. Here's our CRM opportunity export, our outbound sequence report and our call notes. Write the sales playbook our new reps will run from, with weekly activity targets worked back from their quota.

## What you need

- **A CRM opportunity export** for the last 26 weeks (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or any other): created date, source, segment fields such as industry or size, amount, status, the date each deal entered each stage, loss reason, expected close date and next step date. Upload it, paste it, or point to the Google Sheet it lives in.
- **Your outbound sequence report,** if you run outbound: prospects, replies, meetings booked and held, and opportunities by enrollment month, plus sends and replies by step.
- **Call notes** from discovery calls, raw or already coded.
- **The quota,** the number of reps and their start date, selling weeks a quarter, and what a rep can handle in a day.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** Google Sheets, to read the export where it lives.

No history yet? Describe the product, buyers, team and quota. The skill builds the same playbook from your estimates and marks every number as an assumption to test in the first 30 days.

## Example requests

**A full playbook for new hires**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-playbook-builder" skill. Two account executives start next month. Build the playbook from our export: who to target, our stages, weekly activity targets, the cadence, call standards, CRM rules and a 30-60-90 day plan.

**An SDR playbook**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-playbook-builder" skill. Write an SDR playbook from our sequence report: how many new prospects and calls a day, which steps of our cadence to keep, and a 30-60-90 day plan for a new SDR.

**Activity targets from quota**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-playbook-builder" skill. Our reps carry $60,000 a quarter. Work back from our own win rate and reply rates to the number of prospects, replies and meetings each rep needs every week.

**Founder handing off sales**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-playbook-builder" skill. I've sold everything myself so far. Turn what my deals show into a playbook someone else can run, and tell me which of my habits the data actually supports.

## What you get

1. **Who to target and who to disqualify,** ranked by value per opportunity from your own closed deals.
2. **Your stages,** with exit criteria, conversion, typical days and a stall limit for each.
3. **Weekly and daily activity targets per rep,** worked back from quota with your own rates, the math shown, and a check against what a rep can handle.
4. **The cadence,** step by step, with the step that isn't earning its place.
5. **Call and email standards,** only where your notes back them, plus your top objections.
6. **CRM rules,** each with how many deals break it today.
7. **A dated 30-60-90 day ramp plan** with milestones, the first expected win and a quota ramp.
8. **Links to the detailed guides** and a list of every assumption to test.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can add the weekly activity scorecard to a new tab in a Google Sheet you name. It never changes your CRM.

## Tips

- Rerun it each quarter with a fresh export: tiers, rates and targets move as the new reps' deals close.
- Code a few more call notes each month. Behaviours that aren't proven yet become standards once the numbers back them.
- If the capacity check fails, fix the plan (fewer phone steps, SDR time, more inbound) before the reps start, not after.
