# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "integration-marketplace-listing" skill. Our booking plugin, Shopify app and Chrome extension get fewer installs than the big names. Audit all three listings against the rivals, rewrite them, tell me how to get more reviews without breaking any rules, and tell me which marketplace to list in next.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is required.** Paste each listing (or its URL), the last 30 days of numbers from each marketplace dashboard (listing visits, installs, rating, review count, the date of the newest review), and 2 to 4 rival listings per marketplace.
- **The words buyers search for.** Type the job into each marketplace's search box and paste the suggestions it shows. They beat any guess.
- **For the "where next" call:** roughly what share of your customers use each host platform you're considering (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Google Workspace, HubSpot), whether the integration exists, and how many weeks it would take to build.
- **The InsightfulPipe MCP, if you want it to read for you.** The site crawler reads public listing pages where the store allows it (Shopify App Store pages give it the rating and review count; WordPress.org and Atlassian pages blocked it when this skill was built), the screenshot tool captures listings side by side, and GA4 shows the sign-ups each marketplace sends. Google search volume through DataForSEO is optional and was untested when this skill was built.

## Example requests

**Full audit and rewrite**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integration-marketplace-listing" skill. Here are our WordPress.org, Shopify App Store and Chrome Web Store listings and last month's stats. Grade them against the top three rivals in each and rewrite every field to the limit.

**Shopify app store optimization**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integration-marketplace-listing" skill. Our Shopify app has a 4.6 rating but sits under apps with hundreds of reviews. Rewrite the name, introduction, details, features and search terms, and tell me what in the listing breaks Shopify's rules.

**Chrome Web Store SEO**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integration-marketplace-listing" skill. Check our Chrome extension's name, summary and description against the store's limits and its keyword spam policy, and rewrite them.

**WordPress plugin directory SEO**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integration-marketplace-listing" skill. Here is our plugin's readme. Fix the short description and tags for the directory and tell me how to ask for reviews without nagging.

**Which marketplace next**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integration-marketplace-listing" skill. 44% of our customers use Google Workspace, 21% use Wix and 6% use HubSpot. Which marketplace should we list in next?

## What you get

A scorecard per listing (field fit, buyer words, proof, visuals and conversion, each with the number behind it), the fixes in order, every field rewritten with its character count and the marketplace's limit, a rival comparison, a review plan per marketplace with a pace estimate, and a Go now, Plan or Skip verdict for each marketplace you're considering. The skill changes nothing in any marketplace; you paste the copy in.

## Good to know

- Field limits come from each marketplace's own documentation and are checked again when the skill runs, because marketplaces change them.
- A listing page the crawler can't read is marked as such and filled from your paste; it is never graded as empty.
- Paid placements inside marketplaces, general software directories and review sites, and mobile app stores are separate jobs.
