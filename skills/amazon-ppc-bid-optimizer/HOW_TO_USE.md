# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-bid-optimizer" skill. Here are my Sponsored Products targeting and placement reports and my bulk sheet. Set my bids for a 25% target ACOS and give me a bulk file to upload.

## What you need

- **Your Sponsored Products exports for one 30-day window** that ended at least 7 days ago (14 if you're a vendor): the targeting report, the placement report and a fresh bulk sheet. Paste them, upload CSV or XLSX files, or point to the Google Sheet you keep them in.
- **Your goal per product:** a target ACOS, a target TACOS (then add your Business Report for the same dates), or the highest ACOS you'll accept while a product launches.
- **Your costs per product,** if you have them: price, cost of goods, referral fee, FBA fee and other costs per unit. They give each product's break-even ACOS.
- **For a peak plan:** last year's clicks, orders and spend for the same event and for the 30 days before it.

## Example requests

**Weekly bids to a target ACOS**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-bid-optimizer" skill. Set this week's Sponsored Products bids for a 30% target ACOS. Show me the math for every change.

**Bids from a TACOS goal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-bid-optimizer" skill. I want TACOS at 10% on my candles. Here's my Business Report too. Work out the ACOS that allows and set the bids for it.

**Placement bid adjustments**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-bid-optimizer" skill. Here's my placement report. Set my top of search, rest of search and product page adjustments from what each placement actually sells.

**Peak days plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "amazon-ppc-bid-optimizer" skill. Plan my bids and budgets for Black Friday to Cyber Monday from last year's numbers, with the dates to upload the raise and the revert.

## What you get

1. **A target ACOS for every product,** from your target, your TACOS goal or your launch cap, checked against break-even.
2. **A new bid for every keyword and product target,** with the formula's numbers behind it (target CPC = average order value × conversion rate × target ACOS), limited to +20% or −30% per change.
3. **A list of targets held for data,** with how many more clicks each one needs.
4. **Placement adjustments** for top of search, rest of search and product pages, from each placement's own ACOS.
5. **A replay:** what your last 30 days would have cost at the new bids if conversion held, per target, per product and in total.
6. **A peak plan,** if you ask: bid raises and budgets from your own last peak, with upload and revert dates.
7. **A bulk file** with one row per change, for you to upload.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can save the bulk file and the report to a new tab in a Google Sheet. It never changes anything in your Amazon Ads account: you upload the bulk file yourself.

## Tips

- Run it once a week, on a settled window. A bid needs about a week of clicks before its effect shows.
- Download a fresh bulk sheet right before you upload, and copy the IDs and spellings from it.
- A product still above target after one run isn't a mistake: the step limit spreads a big cut over a few weeks.
