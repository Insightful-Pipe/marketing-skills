# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "video-ad-script-writer" skill. Write a 30-second TikTok ad script for my product, starting from the hooks that already work in my ad accounts.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with any of these added:
  - Meta Ads or TikTok Ads: for hook rate, hold rate and the cost of each engaged view on your past video ads
  - Google Ads: for the search terms and ad lines that already convert, and for your YouTube video ads
  - YouTube Analytics: for where viewers leave your longer videos

  The skill pulls the data itself; you don't export anything.
- **A short brief:** what you sell, who it's for, where the ad will run and how long it should be. The skill fills the rest from your data and asks you to confirm.
- **Nothing connected?** Paste past ad results, captions or customer quotes instead. Without them, you still get a script, with every hook marked "untested".

## Example requests

**A script from your best hooks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-ad-script-writer" skill. Find my three best TikTok hooks by 2-second and 6-second view rate, then write two new 20-second scripts in the same style.

**Hook and hold report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-ad-script-writer" skill. Show me hook rate, hold rate and cost per ThruPlay for every Meta video ad in the last 90 days, and tell me which ads have a hook problem and which have a body problem.

**First video ads, built on search data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-ad-script-writer" skill. We've never run video. Write a 30-second YouTube ad and a 15-second Reels cut from what works in our Google Ads.

**Video sales letter or product demo**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-ad-script-writer" skill. Write a 3-minute video sales letter for our course, and put the re-hooks where viewers usually drop off our YouTube videos.

**Review a script**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-ad-script-writer" skill. Here's the script our creator sent. Check it against the checklist and rewrite the hook.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:**
   - your video ads' hook and hold numbers (or a plain "not graded" when there aren't enough)
   - for ads that get views but no conversions, a check of the hook, product timing, benefit and CTA
   - the search themes and ad lines that convert
   - a list of the claims your ads already make, with any conflicts flagged
2. **The angle:** one message, and the data behind it.
3. **A timed script:** voiceover, visuals, on-screen text and direction for every beat. The words per second are counted against a 2.5 limit.
4. **Three hook variants** from three different hook families, each tied to its evidence.
5. **Production notes and a test plan:** what to film, what to run against what, and which metric decides.
6. **A checklist,** each item marked pass or fix.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your ad, search and video data and writes scripts. It never uploads videos or creates ads.

## Tips

- Say which placement comes first (TikTok, Reels, YouTube skippable). The hook window and the length follow from it.
- If your ads quote numbers such as reviews, years or results, check the claims list. The skill won't use a figure that two of your ads state differently.
- For legal, health or finance offers, get the script signed off before you shoot it.
- After launch, run the hook and hold report again once each version has 1,000 impressions or more.
