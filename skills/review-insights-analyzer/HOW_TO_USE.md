# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "review-insights-analyzer" skill. Analyze the last 12 months of Google reviews for all our locations: what drives our 1-star and 5-star reviews, which complaints are getting worse, and what should we fix first?

## What you need

- **Your reviews,** in one of two ways:
  - your Google Business Profile or WooCommerce store connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, so the skill reads every review itself, or
  - a paste or export from Google, Amazon, Yelp, Trustpilot, Tripadvisor or your review app, with the location or product, date, stars and text for each review.
- **Which locations or products to cover,** if you have several.
- **Rivals to compare with,** if you want them. With DataForSEO connected, the skill pulls their rating and review count. To compare what customers say about them, paste some of their reviews.

## Example requests

**Every location, one report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-insights-analyzer" skill. Read the Google reviews for our four clinics and tell me which complaints are local to one clinic and which are everywhere.

**Why is our rating dropping?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-insights-analyzer" skill. Our Riverside location went from 4.5 to 4.1 stars this year. Find out what changed in the reviews and when.

**Amazon review analysis**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-insights-analyzer" skill. Here's the review export for our three best-selling products on Amazon. What do buyers complain about per product, and what should we change on the listing versus the product itself?

**Store product reviews**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-insights-analyzer" skill. Analyze the product reviews in our WooCommerce store. Which products have a sizing or quality problem, and is it getting worse?

**Compare with a rival**
Hey Claude, I just added the "review-insights-analyzer" skill. Here are 40 reviews of the bakery across the street and 40 of ours. Where do they beat us, and where do we beat them?

## What you get

1. **A rating summary per location or product,** with the average in each half of the window and the 1 and 2 star reviews still unanswered.
2. **The codebook:** the themes customers talk about, each with a plain test for what counts.
3. **What drives low ratings:** each complaint with its count, share, the stars it comes with, whether it's rising, where it happens, and how much the average would move if it were fixed, with short quotes as evidence.
4. **What drives five stars:** the praise to protect and to repeat in your ads, listings and pages.
5. **Rivals:** their rating and review count, and a theme-by-theme comparison when you paste their reviews.
6. **Fixes in order,** each with who owns it (operations, the listing, a web page or the product) and the number to check next quarter.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It only reads. To draft replies to the unanswered reviews, use the reviews and comments responder skill if you have it.

## Tips

- Twelve months is the default window, so a trend has two halves to compare. Shorter windows work, but trends need more reviews.
- A location or product with fewer than 20 written reviews is labelled directional. Combine small products into a product line if you want firmer numbers.
- Run it again next quarter: each fix comes with the share to beat.
