# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. Look at my Meta ad account and build me a plan to scale my Facebook Ads.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Meta ad account added.** The skill reads results by ad, 12 weeks of spend history, your pixel, custom audiences, live ads and recent edits itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Where you want to get to:** a target daily or monthly spend, and a timeline. Without them, the skill plans for twice your current spend over 8 weeks.
- **Your target cost per result or ROAS,** and how much it may rise while you scale. Without them, it uses your own 30-day cost per result and allows a 20% rise.
- **How many new ads your team can make a week,** and whether the bigger budget is approved. Without these, those two readiness checks stay unknown.
- **Optional:** customer lifetime value or margin per sale, to see profit per customer at each spend level; a hard budget ceiling the plan must never pass; and business context (seasonal peaks, planned offer changes, competitors' moves), so no budget step lands in the wrong week.

No Meta connection? Paste an ad level export for the last 60 days (and weekly totals for the last 12 weeks if you have them). The skill runs the same checks, without tracking, audiences, recent edits or changes.

## Example requests

**Double the budget**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. I spend about $100 a day on Meta and want to get to $200 a day without the cost per purchase going above $40. Build the plan.

**Which ads to scale**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. List my winning ads that are worth more budget, and tell me for each one whether to raise its ad set or duplicate it.

**Duplicate a winner**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. Duplicate my best ad set with a $150 daily budget into a wider audience. Show me the payload before you create anything.

**Scaling keeps failing**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. Every time I go past $200 a day my cost per lead explodes. Look at my history and tell me what goes wrong and how to do it properly.

**A deadline**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. Black Friday is in 8 weeks and I need to go from $500 to $2,000 a day. Is that realistic, and what's the roadmap?

## What you get

1. **Scaling overview:** where you are, where you're going, the pace, the weeks it takes and the cost per result to expect at the new spend.
2. **Readiness:** six checks (tracking, a proven winner, result volume, creative depth, creative pipeline, budget approval) with a verdict: Ready, Almost ready or Not ready.
3. **Winning ads to scale:** every ad that beats your target on enough results, with a route for each: raise its budget, or duplicate its ad set into the same or a wider audience.
4. **Budget path:** week by week, split between existing winners, new ad sets and a testing reserve.
5. **Audience expansion and creative volume:** the next audiences to build, the new ads you need each week with a production checklist (briefs to write, variations to cut, creators to book), and which ads need a refresh.
6. **Risks and rollback:** the triggers, filled in with your own numbers, and what to do when one hits.
7. **Roadmap by phase:** Foundation, Early, Growth and Stabilise, each with tasks and a success test, plus a weekly check-in.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- raise a winning ad set or campaign budget by one step (at most +20%)
- duplicate a winning ad set with a new budget and its winning ads (Meta creates the copy paused; you switch it on in Ads Manager)
- lower or pause budgets when a rollback trigger hits

## Tips

- If your account has little or no recent spend, the skill says so: the verdict is Not ready, the winner list is empty, and the budget path is printed as a schedule that starts once the foundation work is done.
- Build creative before you add budget. Ads wear out faster at higher spend.
- Run it again at each phase end. The plan is only as good as the last week's numbers.
