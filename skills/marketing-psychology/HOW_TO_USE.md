# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-psychology" skill. Which persuasion angles actually work on my Google Ads audience, and do my landing pages use them?

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with Google Ads and DataForSEO added.** The skill reads your ad lines, their numbers and your landing pages itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Optional:** Meta Ads, to tag your Meta ad text too, and GA4, to pick pages when you don't run Google Ads.
- **The action you want more of** (a call, a form, a purchase, a signup), if it isn't your main conversion.
- **Your brand terms and location words,** if they aren't obvious from the account.

No connection? Paste your best and worst ads with impressions and clicks, or a page's text, and the skill runs the same lever table and ethics check on that.

## Example requests

**What works for my audience**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-psychology" skill. Tag my Google Ads headlines by persuasion lever and tell me which ones my audience clicks and converts on.

**Why a page underperforms**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-psychology" skill. My drain-cleaning landing page converts worse than the others. Read it and tell me which levers are missing or buried.

**Pricing page**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-psychology" skill. Review my pricing page for anchoring, decoy and charm pricing, and tell me what to test first.

**Ethics check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-psychology" skill. Check my ads and landing pages for fake urgency, unbacked claims and other dark patterns.

**Test, then build**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-psychology" skill. Find the levers that work, then draft a paused challenger ad that uses them. Show me the payload before you create anything.

## What you get

1. **A lever scorecard:** each lever (social proof, loss framing, urgency, zero price, risk reversal, payment plans, ease and more) with its CTR index and conversion index against the lines next to it, and a verdict: responds, neutral, weak, too little data or untested. Near-duplicate lines count once, and every cue word it overrules is listed.
2. **Your best and worst ad lines,** by the same index.
3. **A page lever map:** for each landing page, where each lever first appears (top, middle, low or absent), plus calls to action, forms and phone links.
4. **Lever gaps:** what to move up, add, give room, demote or test.
5. **An ethics check:** pre-ticked boxes, real urgency, proof you can back, outcome promises, honest "free", fear in proportion and an easy way out.
6. **The top 5 changes,** ranked by the ad traffic they touch, each with the principle, the evidence, every new line or page block written out, and how to test it.
7. **The principle library** when you bring a specific decision: mental models, decision biases, influence, pricing psychology and experience design, with page, email and ad checklists.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- create a challenger responsive search ad built on the levers that work (always created paused, so you switch it on)
- add callouts for reassurance lines, such as availability or payment plans

It never edits your pages, pauses ads or changes budgets. And it only writes proof, guarantees and deadlines you confirm are true.

## Tips

- Run it after a month of steady ads: a lever needs at least 1,000 impressions across 3 different lines before it gets a verdict.
- Every finding is a test, not a law. Lines in an ad aren't randomised, so run the challenger before rewriting everything.
- Some sites block page readers. If a page comes back blocked, paste its text and the skill carries on.
