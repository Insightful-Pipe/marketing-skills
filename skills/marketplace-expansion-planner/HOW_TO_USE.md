# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketplace-expansion-planner" skill. Should we sell on Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Walmart or TikTok Shop? Tell me the order and which products to list first.

## What you need

- **Your product list:** price, unit cost, the postage you pay per order, and units sold in the last 90 days. Paste it or upload an export. If your Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento store is connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, the skill pulls products, prices and sales itself and asks only for what the store doesn't hold.
- **The fees for each marketplace** you're considering, if you have them. If not, the skill looks them up on each marketplace's fee page (when it can browse) and tells you the page and the date.
- **Your margin floor,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses what a paid sale earns on your own store today, from your ad spend and orders.
- **Any MAP policy,** trademark status and approvals, so it can catch conflicts before you list.
- **Optional:** DataForSEO connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, so it can measure search demand for your category on each channel (at most 4 paid calls per run).

## Example requests

**Should I sell on Amazon?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketplace-expansion-planner" skill. Here's my product export. Should I sell on Amazon with FBA, and which products would still make money after the fees?

**Pick the best marketplaces**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketplace-expansion-planner" skill. We're a US home goods brand on our own store. Rank Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Etsy and TikTok Shop for us and give me a rollout order.

**Check a pricing plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketplace-expansion-planner" skill. We want to list on eBay 10% under our store price. Check it against our MAP policy and our margins.

**European marketplaces**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketplace-expansion-planner" skill. We sell in Germany and the Netherlands. Compare Amazon.de, Zalando and bol using the fee tables I'm pasting.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for every channel:** Open now, Test, Not yet or Skip, with the share of your sales volume that would clear your floor there.
2. **Margin after fees for every product on every channel,** with the break-even price, the price needed to reach your floor, and how much each sale can spend on marketplace ads.
3. **The findings that change the plan:** products that would lose money, products a channel won't accept, discounts that break your MAP, commissions that sink cheap products, and shelves priced far below you.
4. **A rollout order** with the first three products for each channel and what to check after 30 days.
5. **A list of unknowns,** and what each would change.

In the example, an invented ceramics store learns that Amazon and Etsy are worth opening now, its 14 in floor planter falls under its floor with Amazon fulfilment, its resold plant care kit clears on none of the five channels, and its plan to list on eBay 10% under its own price would break MAP on six products: five under its own policy and one, the resold kit, under its maker's.

## What it can change for you

Nothing in your store or on any marketplace. With your yes, it can save the plan to a new tab in a Google Sheet you choose.

## Tips

- Give real unit costs. The plan is only as good as the cost figures; a missing cost is left as unknown, never guessed.
- Check the fee pages before you list. Marketplaces change fees, and the plan names the source and date of every rate it used.
- Run it again 30 days after each launch with the real fees per order, to see whether the channel is earning what the plan said.
