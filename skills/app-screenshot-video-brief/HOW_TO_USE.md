# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "app-screenshot-video-brief" skill. Write the screenshot and preview video brief for my app on the App Store and Google Play, and tell me what the first three frames should say.

## What you need

- **Your app's App Store ID or Google Play package name.** One store is enough.
- **Your current screenshots,** uploaded in order, plus the icon and any preview video stills. Without uploads, the skill reads the store's screenshot links where your client can open them.
- **Which features are paid,** so frames that show them carry a tag.
- **The keywords your listing targets,** including the App Store keyword field if you use one (the store hides it, so only you can supply it).
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with the App Store and Google Play sources, so the skill reads your listing, your reviews and your rivals' first frames itself. Without it, paste 30 or more reviews from each store or describe what users praise and complain about.

## Example requests

**Full brief for both stores**
Hey Claude, I just added the "app-screenshot-video-brief" skill. Here are our 6 App Store screenshots and our Play package name. Write the full brief: frame order, captions, preview video and feature graphic.

**App store screenshot ideas from reviews**
Hey Claude, I just added the "app-screenshot-video-brief" skill. Read our recent App Store reviews and tell me which benefits our screenshots should lead with, and which complaints they should answer.

**Beat the rivals in search results**
Hey Claude, I just added the "app-screenshot-video-brief" skill. Search "habit tracker" on both stores, read what the top apps say in their first three screenshots, and plan ours so we say something they don't.

**Preview video plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "app-screenshot-video-brief" skill. We have no app preview yet. Storyboard a 15 to 30 second preview that works on the App Store and as our Google Play YouTube video.

**From uploads only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "app-screenshot-video-brief" skill. No connection, just these screenshots and our app description. Check them against the store rules and rewrite the captions.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:** the benefits your 4 and 5 star reviewers name, ranked, the complaints split into expectation gaps and product problems, and the claims your rivals already make in their first frames.
2. **The first three frames,** with the rule and the numbers behind each one.
3. **The full set per store:** the screen to capture, headline, subline, paid tags and Google Play alt text for every frame.
4. **A preview video storyboard** with timings, the share of real app footage and the poster frame.
5. **Icon and feature graphic notes,** and a specs sheet with the sizes and counts each store accepts.
6. **Copy checks** on every caption: length and the words Google Play doesn't allow.
7. **One store test** to run on the new set, with its settings and the rule for applying the result.

## What it won't do

- It doesn't generate images or video. Hand the brief to a designer or your own design tool.
- It doesn't upload or change anything in App Store Connect or Play Console.

## Tips

- Upload the screens you actually have. The brief names real screens to capture, never mock-ups.
- Run it again after the store test ends, with the new reviews, to plan the next round.
- If you ship on iPad or Android tablets, say so; the skill adds those sets.
