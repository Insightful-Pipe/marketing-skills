# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ugc-brief-generator" skill. Find my best video hooks in Meta Ads and write a UGC creator brief and script built on them.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Meta ad account added.** The skill pulls your ad results, video watch data and ad copy itself; you don't need to export anything. TikTok Ads is optional.
- **Your product facts and offer,** and the claims your brand can back. The brief never invents these.
- **Two or three competitors,** if you'd like market references from the Meta Ad Library. It asks for them when your account has no proven winner yet.

## Example requests

**Brief from your winners**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ugc-brief-generator" skill. Look at my Meta ads from the last 90 days, find the hooks that stop people and the ads that convert, and write a brief for two new creators.

**Scripts from the best hooks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ugc-brief-generator" skill. Write three UGC video ad scripts that reuse my best-performing hook types, with timings and on-screen text.

**New account, no winners yet**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ugc-brief-generator" skill. We've barely run ads. Check my account, then use these two competitors' video ads in the Meta Ad Library to brief our first UGC test.

**Refresh a tired winner**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ugc-brief-generator" skill. My best UGC ad is wearing out. Brief new creators on the same angle with fresh hooks.

## What you get

1. **What worked:** your best hooks (by 3-second view rate) and best converters (by cost per result), each next to your account median, plus the ads that had too little data to judge.
2. **Market references** (optional): competitors' video ads from the Meta Ad Library, ranked by how long they've run, with the creator-style ones marked and the pattern to borrow.
3. **Up to three creator briefs,** each with seven parts: overview, hook, body, CTA, technical specs (Meta Reels, Stories and Feed, TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Pinterest), do's and don'ts with compliance, and references.
4. **A timed script for each brief,** a one-screen card for the creator, a pre-send checklist (including the due date and payment terms), and a review rubric for when the video comes back.
5. **A launch measurement plan:** what to compare against your benchmark, and when to judge it.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It only reads your data and writes briefs. When the videos come back, the `facebook-ads-creative-tester` skill (if installed) can upload them and set up a paused test after you confirm.

## Tips

- Nothing is called a winner below 1,000 impressions, and a converter also needs 3 or more results. A small account may get "no proven winner"; that's a real answer, and the brief then gives you hooks to test.
- The ad copy doesn't always show a spoken hook. When the skill marks a hook "watch to confirm", tell it what the first three seconds of that video show.
- Give it your list of approved claims. Creators may say only those.
