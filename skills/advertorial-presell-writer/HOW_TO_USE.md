# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "advertorial-presell-writer" skill. Write an advertorial presell page for my product and the ads that lead into it, and tell me which angle to lead with and why.

## What you need

- **Your product, price, offer terms and product page URL.**
- **Your proof list:** ratings with their review count, test reports, sales figures, your guarantee, and any real story you can tell (your own, or a customer's review they gave you permission to quote). The skill only writes claims from this list.
- **Customer words:** at least 30 reviews, support emails or survey answers, pasted or exported.
- **Where the traffic comes from:** Meta or TikTok feed ads, native placements like Taboola or Outbrain, Google Demand Gen, or email.
- **Any rules on what you can say,** for example no health claims for a supplement or sleep product.

No connection? Paste the brief, the proof list, your reviews and your ad results, and the skill writes the same page, ads and checks.

With the InsightfulPipe MCP connected, it reads your own winning ads (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads), rivals' long-running ads in the public ad libraries and the pages those ads send people to, product reviews from WooCommerce, and for a presell that is already live, GA4 and Shopify or WooCommerce orders.

## Example requests

**A new presell**
Hey Claude, I just added the "advertorial-presell-writer" skill. Write a story advertorial for our cooling pillow for cold Meta traffic. Here are our reviews, our proof list and the product page.

**Pick the angle from evidence**
Hey Claude, I just added the "advertorial-presell-writer" skill. Look at my winning Meta ads and my three competitors' longest-running ads, then tell me which angle our presell should lead with.

**Fix a presell that isn't converting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "advertorial-presell-writer" skill. Our listicle presell gets traffic but few sales. Read GA4 and Shopify for the last 30 days, tell me where readers drop, and rewrite the part that's losing them.

**Native ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "advertorial-presell-writer" skill. Write a listicle presell and the Outbrain headlines for our washable rug, and check the page against the FTC's advertising label rules.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:** your winning ads, rivals' long-running ads with days running, the formats rivals use, and what your reviews talk about most, including reviews that can't be used as claims.
2. **An angle score** for each candidate angle, with the lead angle and a test angle.
3. **The format** (story, listicle or short reasons-why) and the rule that chose it.
4. **For a live presell,** the read, click and buy rates against targets, and which part to rewrite first.
5. **The full page:** advertising label, three headline options, byline, every section tagged with its proof, calls to action and a footer disclosure.
6. **The lead-in ads** for each channel, with character counts.
7. **A compliance check** against the FTC's native advertising guidance and review rules, plus the claims for you to confirm.
8. **A test plan:** headline, then angle, then format.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can create the page in Shopify as a hidden page, so nothing is public until you publish it. It never publishes pages, changes ads or edits your product page.

## Tips

- The more real reviews you give it, the better the angle choice. Include the bad ones; the page answers them.
- Get written permission before quoting a customer, and keep the email.
- Test the headline first: it decides how many people read the rest.
