# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "inventory-planner" skill. Here are my stock and sales exports. Tell me what to stop advertising, what is about to run out, and how to sell my excess inventory.

## What you need

- **A stock export** with units available, unit cost and price per variant, plus any incoming stock and its arrival date. Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or any sheet works.
- **Sales for the last 56 days** per variant (weekly or daily), and the days each variant had nothing available, if you have them.
- **Per supplier:** lead time, how late the last three deliveries were, and how often you reorder.
- **Your payment fee and pick-and-pack cost per unit,** so markdowns never go below what an item costs you to sell.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with your store, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Merchant Center or Klaviyo connected, so the skill pulls the data and sees where each item is advertised.

## Example requests

**Full plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "inventory-planner" skill. Run the full stock plan on my Shopify store: days of cover, reorder-by dates, what to hold back from ads and what to move.

**How to prevent stockouts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "inventory-planner" skill. Which products will sell out before my next delivery, and what should I stop advertising or emailing until they are back?

**How to sell excess inventory**
Hey Claude, I just added the "inventory-planner" skill. I have too much teaware left after summer. Find the dead stock and overstock, tell me which items are worth pushing with ads, and give me a markdown plan that never sells below cost.

**Ads on items that cannot ship**
Hey Claude, I just added the "inventory-planner" skill. Check my Google Shopping and Meta catalog ads for products that are out of stock or about to be, and show me what that spend cost last week.

**Plan, then apply**
Hey Claude, I just added the "inventory-planner" skill. Build the plan, then exclude the out-of-stock items from my Performance Max asset group and Meta product set. Show me the changes first.

## What you get

1. **A ranked plan,** with the money at stake on each line: contribution lost while an item is out, or cash tied up in excess stock.
2. **A stock table:** every variant's pace, days of cover, sell-out date, reorder-by date and class (out of stock, sells out within 7 days, reorder now, healthy, above ceiling, overstock, dead stock).
3. **A hold-back list:** items to keep out of Shopping, Performance Max, Meta catalog ads and scheduled emails, with the ad spend on items that cannot ship and the catalogs that still say in stock.
4. **A plan to move excess stock:** which items are worth advertising at their margin, the email and placement for each, and a dated markdown ladder down to a clearance floor built from cost.
5. **Ready payloads** for each change, marked not run until you confirm.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- exclude items from a Performance Max asset group or a Meta product set, and build a product set for items to push
- set a markdown price and compare-at price on Shopify, a sale price on WooCommerce or a special price on Magento
- create a clearance collection on Shopify
- create a Klaviyo email campaign as a draft (it never sends it)

It never changes stock counts.

## Tips

- Run it weekly in busy seasons and every two weeks otherwise; the classes move as stock sells.
- Give it the real lateness of your last deliveries. The buffer comes from them, and a guessed buffer moves every reorder date.
- Before a big sale, run it first so the sale features items with stock to spare.
