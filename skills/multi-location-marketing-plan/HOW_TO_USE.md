# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-location-marketing-plan" skill. Build our franchise marketing plan for the next 90 days from every location's Business Profile numbers.

## What you need

- **Your location list,** one row per location: city, corporate or franchise, and the opening date. If the InsightfulPipe MCP is connected with your Google Business Profile locations, the skill reads the rest itself. If not, paste each location's Performance numbers (views, calls, directions, website clicks, bookings) for the last 28 days and the 28 before, plus review count, rating, reviews in the last 90 days and the reply rate.
- **The money per month:** the brand fund, shared costs paid from it, each franchisee's local budget, the co-op match rate and a launch amount for new locations.
- **Your brand standard:** the name on the signs, the primary category every location should use and the location page pattern on your website.
- **Your fund rules,** so the plan never moves money the franchise agreement doesn't allow.

## Example requests

**Franchise marketing plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-location-marketing-plan" skill. Build a 90-day franchise marketing plan for our 14 locations. The brand fund is $45,000 a month and co-op matches 50% of approved local ads.

**Working from a paste, nothing connected**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-location-marketing-plan" skill. Here is a CSV of our 8 clinics with their Business Profile views, calls, direction requests, reviews and ratings. Tier them and tell me where the local budget should go.

**Co-op budget split**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-location-marketing-plan" skill. Split next month's local marketing fund across our stores by demand and results, and show what each franchisee has to spend to draw its full co-op match.

**Rules and governance**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-location-marketing-plan" skill. Write the Business Profile rules, local ad templates and approval rules our franchisees should follow, and list which locations break them today.

**Monthly scorecard**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-location-marketing-plan" skill. Re-run the location scorecard for this month, re-tier every location and tell me which ones get reserve money released.

## What you get

1. **A profile rules check** for every location: verification, name, primary category, website link, tracked link, low ratings answered, reply rate and posting.
2. **Location tiers** (Grow, Convert, Seed, Fix, New) from each location's own views and actions per 1,000 views against your network's median.
3. **The money:** the brand layer, launches, each location's share and co-op draw, what each franchisee must spend to draw it, cost caps and a reserve, adding up to the fund to the dollar.
4. **Business Profile rules,** location pages and a review target per location.
5. **Local ad templates** with locked and local fields, and approval rules for franchisees.
6. **A 90-day rollout** with dated tasks, owners and three check-ins.
7. **A location scorecard** with the same columns as the multi-location report in `local-business-marketing-report`, plus tier, draw and review target.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the exact content, it can:
- put the scorecard and money table in a new tab of a Google Sheet you name
- publish one brand library post on each location's Business Profile

Both need a Read & Write connection. It never edits profiles, budgets or campaigns.
