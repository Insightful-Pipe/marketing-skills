# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "lead-magnet-writer" skill. Write a lead magnet on the topic my buyers search most, finished and ready to hand out, with the opt-in copy and the email that delivers it.

## What you need

- **A short brief:** what you sell, who buys it, and the topic (or let the data pick it).
- **Your own notes,** if you have them: your process, policy wording, numbers or examples. The asset builds on these first.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** Search Console (the questions your site already shows for), GA4 (the pages that bring the right visitors), DataForSEO (what the market searches, 2 to 4 paid calls), and Klaviyo or Mailchimp (where the opt-ins land). With none of them, paste your customers' questions or a query export instead.

## Example requests

**Let the data pick the angle**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lead-magnet-writer" skill. We sell booking software to salons and clinics. Find the no-show topic people search most and write the lead magnet for it.

**A set topic and format**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lead-magnet-writer" skill. Write a 5-lesson email course on pricing your services for freelance designers. Here are my notes.

**From a free tool plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lead-magnet-writer" skill. The free-tool-strategy run picked a client onboarding checklist. Write the checklist, the opt-in block for our onboarding blog post and the delivery email.

**Write it and save the email**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lead-magnet-writer" skill. Write a quick-reference guide on GDPR consent for small shops, then save the delivery email as a Klaviyo template. Show me the template first.

## What you get

1. **Why this angle and format:** the angles found, their search demand, your own impressions and traffic, and the format the searches favour.
2. **The finished asset** in one of four formats: a checklist, a fill-in template or spreadsheet layout, a one-page quick-reference guide, or a 3 to 5 lesson email course.
3. **Opt-in page copy:** headline, subhead, what's inside, preview, form, button and consent line, for the pages that already bring the right visitors.
4. **The delivery email:** two subject lines, preview text, body and a compliant footer.
5. **Opt-in tracking:** the GA4 `generate_lead` event code and whether it already exists.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- save the delivery email (and course lessons) as a Klaviyo or Mailchimp template
- mark `generate_lead` as a key event in GA4

It never sends an email and never publishes a page.

## Tips

- Give it your own rules and examples. A lead magnet built on what you actually do beats general advice.
- Ebooks, scored quizzes and calculators are other jobs; it will point you to the right skill if you have it installed.
- Run it again a few months after launch, with the opt-in numbers, to pick the next angle.
