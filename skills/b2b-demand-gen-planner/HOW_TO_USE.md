# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "b2b-demand-gen-planner" skill. We need $560k of new ARR closing next quarter. Work back from that: how many deals, opportunities, SQLs, MQLs and leads we need, which channels can supply them at our own costs, how much outbound we need, and give me a 90-day plan.

## What you need

- **The target:** new revenue and the window it must close in, plus your gross margin if you want payback.
- **CRM numbers** (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or any CRM), pasted, uploaded or in a Google Sheet: won and lost opportunities in the last 12 months with won value and cycle length (opportunity to won, and lead to won); open opportunities expected to close in the window; and leads by source with how many became MQL, SQL and opportunity.
- **Outbound numbers,** if you do outbound: contacts, positive replies, meetings booked and held, and opportunities in the last 90 days, plus SDR count, pace, capacity and cost.
- **Costs for channels with no connector,** such as review site profiles, content or events.
- **Your assets per stage:** demo and pricing pages, case studies, calculators, guides, webinars, outbound triggers.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console, DataForSEO and Google Sheets.

## Example requests

**Just the lead math**
Hey Claude, I just added the "b2b-demand-gen-planner" skill. Our average deal is $18k, we win 22% of opportunities, and the cycle is 75 days. About 40% of leads become MQLs, 45% of MQLs become SQLs and 35% of SQLs become opportunities. How many leads do we need to add $400k of new ARR next quarter?

**A full demand gen plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "b2b-demand-gen-planner" skill. Here's our CRM export and outbound numbers; the ad accounts are connected. Build our B2B demand generation strategy for next quarter from our revenue target.

**Can search carry it?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "b2b-demand-gen-planner" skill. Can paid and organic search alone supply the pipeline for our target, or do we need outbound and LinkedIn too?

**How many SDRs**
Hey Claude, I just added the "b2b-demand-gen-planner" skill. How many SDRs, mailboxes and target accounts do we need for outbound to deliver 30 opportunities next quarter?

## What you get

1. **The backward calculator:** deals, opportunities, SQLs, MQLs and leads, each with its formula, and the plan window shifted by your sales cycle.
2. **Rates and costs by source:** stage rates and lag from your own CRM, cost per lead and per opportunity per channel.
3. **The in-market ceiling:** how much pipeline buyers already searching can supply at your run rates and paid search headroom, with a warning if the target needs more, and search demand from Search Console and keyword volumes alongside it.
4. **Outbound math:** contacts, meetings, SDRs, mailboxes and how much of your target account list the quarter uses.
5. **The allocation:** every added budget or hire, cheapest opportunity first, until the target is covered, or the revenue the window can reach if it can't be.
6. **Offers per stage,** from your own assets.
7. **A 90-day plan** by channel and by month, week 1 to 2 measurement fixes (including GA4 against CRM by channel group), day 30 and day 60 checkpoints with lead floors and cost per opportunity ceilings, CAC and payback.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can create a new tab in your Google Sheet and write the plan into it. It never changes budgets, campaigns or your CRM.

## Tips

- Give it at least 12 months of closed opportunities; fewer make the win rate noisy.
- Keep lead source filled in on every lead. Leads with no source can't be planned for.
- Rerun it at day 30 with fresh numbers and compare against the checkpoints.
