# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-claims-checker" skill. Check every claim in our live ads, landing page and latest email, tell me what proof each one needs, and rewrite anything we can't back.

## What you need

- **The copy.** Paste it, upload an export, give a URL, or connect your ad accounts, email tool or store through the InsightfulPipe MCP so the skill reads the live copy itself.
- **Where it runs.** The markets (US, UK, EU countries) and the channels. The rules differ by market.
- **Your proof.** Study results, review platform stats, customer counts, price lists, guarantee terms, certificates, and whether any reviewer or creator was paid or given something. A claim with no proof is removed from the clean copy until you supply it.

## Example requests

**Before a launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-claims-checker" skill. We launch this landing page and three Meta ads on Monday in the US and UK. Here's the copy and our customer study. Check every claim.

**Live ad accounts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-claims-checker" skill. Read the live ads in our Google Ads and Microsoft Ads accounts and build a claims ledger. Our review stats and price list are attached.

**Testimonials and reviews**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-claims-checker" skill. Our emails quote customer results and some of those customers got a discount for the review. Check the testimonials against the FTC and UK rules and fix the wording.

**Green claims in the EU**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-claims-checker" skill. We say "carbon neutral" on our product pages and in our ads in Ireland and Germany. Does that still work, and what can we say instead?

**Product listings**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-claims-checker" skill. Check the descriptions of our active Shopify products for health and "Made in USA" claims.

## What you get

1. **A claims ledger:** every claim, every place it appears, the proof it needs, the proof you gave, the rule at stake, and a keep, fix or remove verdict.
2. **A fix list,** highest risk first, with replacement text for every placement and character counts for ad limits.
3. **The clean copy,** each piece rewritten with every verdict applied.
4. **The proof to collect** to bring a removed claim back.
5. **Questions for a lawyer,** one line per claim that needs one.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- pause a Google Ads or Meta ad that carries a claim to remove
- add the clean version as a new Google Ads responsive search ad, created paused

It never edits live ad text in place, and never publishes a page, sends an email or changes a product listing. You paste the clean copy.

## Tips

- Run it before every launch and whenever a number in your copy changes (new review count, new study, new price).
- Send the proof with the request. Without it, most numbers come back as "remove until proven".
- It is a claims review, not legal advice. Take the lawyer questions it writes to your counsel.
