# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-media-lift-analyst" skill. We ran TV in three states last month. Tell me how many extra orders it brought in, with a range.

## What you need

- **The flight:** media, first and last day, and the markets or regions it ran in.
- **Your outcome by week and region** for about 16 weeks before the flight through 2 weeks after it. Paste or upload it, or connect the InsightfulPipe MCP with Shopify, WooCommerce, GA4, Google Ads, Search Console, BigQuery or Google Sheets and the skill pulls it.
- **Spend and what one extra order or lead is worth,** if you want a payback answer.
- **Promo codes or vanity URLs** used only in the ads, and anything that happened in only some regions (a local sale, a pop-up, a store opening).

## Example requests

**Regional TV, radio or CTV flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-media-lift-analyst" skill. We ran connected TV and radio in Ohio, Michigan and Indiana from September 7 to October 4 and spent $50,000. Did it lift Shopify orders, and did it pay back at $410 margin an order?

**Billboards or an event, from a pasted export**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-media-lift-analyst" skill. Here is a CSV of weekly leads by city for the last six months. Our billboards ran in Denver for 8 weeks starting July 1. Did they do anything?

**National flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-media-lift-analyst" skill. Our TV ran nationally in the US for 6 weeks. We also sell in Canada and the UK. Measure the lift in branded search and sign-ups.

**Geo test readout**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-media-lift-analyst" skill. Our geo holdout from the incrementality test plan finished last Sunday. Read it against the decision rule we agreed before launch.

**Spot response**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-media-lift-analyst" skill. Here's our TV airing log for October. Which networks and dayparts brought the biggest jump in site visits?

## What you get

1. **A grade first:** readable, read with caution, or no readout, with the reason. When the data can't support a read, it says so and stops.
2. **The lift** for the flight, the carry-over weeks and both together: extra outcomes, lift %, a 95% interval and the chance the lift is above zero.
3. **The comparison regions** it kept and dropped, with each one's pre-flight correlation and why.
4. **A placebo back-test** on the weeks before the flight, so you can see the method doesn't find lifts where there were none.
5. **Support checks:** brand search lift, any shift in paid search spend, and the promo-code and vanity-URL floor.
6. **Cost per extra outcome** with a range, break-even, and a paid back, did not pay back or inconclusive call.

## What it changes

Nothing. It only reads data.

## Tips

- Give it at least 8 weeks before the flight, and 16 if you can. Fewer weeks means a wider range or no readout.
- Tell it about anything local: a sale in one state can look exactly like a TV lift.
- If the answer comes back inconclusive, plan the next flight with a few markets held dark, using the incrementality-test-designer skill if you have it.
