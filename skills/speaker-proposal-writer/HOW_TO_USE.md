# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "speaker-proposal-writer" skill. Find conferences with open calls for speakers where my buyers will be in the room, tell me which are worth it, and write the proposals.

## What you need

- **Who is speaking:** name, role, company and any talks given before.
- **The topic and your proof:** what you can teach and the numbers behind it, with dates. The skill never invents a result.
- **Who must be in the room,** the window you want to speak in, where you'll travel, and how many talks you can give.
- **What a sign-up costs you today** from other channels, if you know it. It's the bar each event has to clear.
- **Events you already have in mind,** as names, links or pasted call pages.

No connection? It works from what you paste: it gives you the searches to run, and you paste back the results and the call pages. With the InsightfulPipe MCP connected, it searches for open calls and reads the call pages through DataForSEO, pulls the questions your audience searches from DataForSEO and Search Console, and reads what past talks produced in GA4.

There is no events database behind it, so the list is only as good as the searches and what you paste. Every deadline is checked on the event's own page.

## Example requests

**Find and pick events**
Hey Claude, I just added the "speaker-proposal-writer" skill. I want three talks between January and June next year in front of clinic and salon owners. Find open calls in the US and Canada, plus virtual events, and rank them. A sign-up costs us about $85 from Google Ads.

**Write a proposal for one event**
Hey Claude, I just added the "speaker-proposal-writer" skill. Here's the call for speakers page for a conference I want to speak at. Write the title, abstract, learning outcomes and speaker bio to its limits.

**Shape the talk around demand**
Hey Claude, I just added the "speaker-proposal-writer" skill. I'm pitching a talk on cutting no-shows. Find the questions our audience actually searches for and build the abstract and learning outcomes around them.

**Plan the leads from a talk**
Hey Claude, I just added the "speaker-proposal-writer" skill. I'm speaking at a conference on March 10. Make the tracking link and a plan for turning the talk into content and sign-ups, then tell me in 30 days how it did.

**How to get speaking engagements**
Hey Claude, I just added the "speaker-proposal-writer" skill. I've never spoken at a conference. Where should a first-time speaker in my field start, and which calls are open now?

## What you get

1. **An event list:** every event found, with its source, dates, closing date, days left and status (open, tight, rush or closed), and the reason for each one dropped (pay to speak, paper calls with fees, listing pages, old editions, outside your window or travel).
2. **A ranking:** buyers in the room, expected sign-ups from your own past-talk rate, cash cost, cost per expected sign-up against your benchmark, a verdict for each event, and where your talk and budget limits fall if every event says yes.
3. **The questions to answer:** themes from search questions, your site's Search Console queries and your customers' own questions, ranked, with the off-topic ones listed.
4. **Your proof:** each figure computed from your counts with its source, and any small-sample figure held back.
5. **One proposal per event:** title, abstract, learning outcomes and speaker bio in that event's format, each field shown as used / limit.
6. **A content and leads plan:** a tracking link per event and a dated plan from the announcement to the 30-day readout.
7. **A past-talk readout,** a watch list of closed calls for next year, and next steps.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It finds, ranks and writes; you submit each proposal on the event's own form. It never submits, posts or emails anything.

## Tips

- Track every talk with its own link. After one tracked talk, the ranking uses your real sign-ups per buyer instead of falling back to cost per buyer in the room.
- Apply to every event that passes. Not all of them will say yes.
- If a call page won't load for the skill, paste the page text. Its fields and limits come from the page.
