# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "developer-oss-growth" skill. Build a 90-day growth plan for our open-source project, with monthly targets and the route from free users to our paid team plan.

## What you need

- **A short description of the project:** what it is (library, CLI, API or self-hostable app), the licence, what is open and what is paid, and the step that shows a developer got it working.
- **Your GitHub numbers, pasted:** stars gained per month, new contributors per month and the median time to the first reply on new issues. GitHub has no connector, and it keeps repository traffic for only 14 days, so paste any traffic snapshots you saved.
- **A signup cohort export** from your product: signups per month, how many reached the activation step within 7 days, and how many paid within 30 days.
- **Optional, with the InsightfulPipe MCP connected:** GA4 for docs sessions and signups by source, Search Console for the queries your docs rank for, and DataForSEO for developer search demand. Without them, paste the same numbers from exports.
- **A goal,** if you have one, such as a number of new paying teams a month.

## Example requests

**Full growth plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "developer-oss-growth" skill. Here are six months of stars, contributors and our signup cohorts. GA4 and Search Console are connected. Build the 90-day plan with monthly targets.

**Stars without users**
Hey Claude, I just added the "developer-oss-growth" skill. We get lots of GitHub stars but few signups to the hosted version. Find where the route breaks.

**Developer marketing strategy for an API**
Hey Claude, I just added the "developer-oss-growth" skill. We sell an API with a free tier. Plan our developer marketing: docs, communities and launches, with targets from our own numbers.

**Check a goal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "developer-oss-growth" skill. We want 100 new paying teams a month by January. Tell me how many signups that takes and whether our plan gets there.

## What you get

1. **A route table** for six months, from search and the repository to docs, signup, activation and paid, and the one step that holds growth back.
2. **A docs review:** their share of signups, queries by intent, docs pages one push from the top 3 with the clicks at stake, and searches your docs miss.
3. **The repository and contributors:** launch spikes judged by the signups they brought, GitHub-referred signups against organic search, and contributor health against your best months.
4. **Communities and launch sites** ranked by signups per 100 sessions, with the next launches and the rules to respect.
5. **The route to paid:** cohort rates and how much more often activated signups pay.
6. **Monthly targets** for the next three months, set from your own trend and best months, and a check against your goal.
7. **A 90-day plan** in which every action names the target it moves.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your data and writes a plan.

## Tips

- Save the GitHub traffic page every two weeks; it is the only way to get monthly repository visitors.
- Tag the links you post in your Discord or forum, or their signups hide inside direct traffic.
- Run it again each month with the new cohort to see which targets moved.
