# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-proposal-writer" skill. Here's an RFP and our answer library. Tell me if we should bid, and if yes, draft the answers.

## What you need

- **For a proposal:** your discovery notes or a call transcript, what you sell, and your price list.
- **For an RFP:** the full RFP, what you sell, your price list, and any past proposals or answer library you have.
- **Proof you can use:** case studies, references and certifications, and which customers you may name.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** a Google Sheet that holds your past answers, and your website so it can find your customer, security and integrations pages.

Nothing has to be connected. Paste or upload what you have; anything missing shows up as a gap instead of a guess.

## Example requests

**Proposal from discovery notes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-proposal-writer" skill. Here are my notes from two calls with a logistics company and our price list. Write the proposal with three options.

**Bid or no bid**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-proposal-writer" skill. Read this RFP and build the compliance table. Should we bid?

**Answers from our library**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-proposal-writer" skill. We decided to bid. Draft every answer from the answer library in my Google Sheet and keep each one under the RFP's word limit.

**Tighten a draft**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-proposal-writer" skill. Check this proposal draft and tell me which claims have no source.

## What you get

**Proposal mode**
1. The buyer's problem in their own words, the outcomes they want and how each will be measured.
2. Scope in and out, and a timeline checked against their go-live date.
3. Two or three priced options built from your price list, with the math shown and one recommended.
4. Proof matched to the buyer, and dated next steps.
5. A list of claims to confirm and gaps to fill.

**RFP mode**
1. A compliance table: every requirement with its section number, mandatory or desirable, and met, partly met, not met or unknown, with the source behind each status and what is missing on each partly met row.
2. A bid score out of 100 with the arithmetic, mandatory knockouts, and a bid or no-bid call.
3. Then, and only then, draft answers in the RFP's numbering, each opening with Yes, Partly or No, with its word count and its sources.
4. An executive summary in the buyer's words, pricing on the RFP's terms, questions to send the buyer, gaps for your team and a submission checklist.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can put the compliance table in a new tab of your Google Sheet. It never sends a proposal or submits a response.

## Tips

- Give it your price list and discount limit up front; it never invents a price or a discount.
- Mark roadmap features as roadmap. They count as not met, however close they are.
- Keep an answer library with a last-checked date on each answer; answers older than 12 months get flagged to re-confirm.
- Send the buyer the clarifying questions before the RFP's question deadline.
