# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "original-research-report" skill. Turn our product data and last month's customer survey into an industry report with headline stats, charts, a methods section and a stat sheet for press.

## What you need

- **Your data, or a plan to get it.** A survey export (CSV from Google Forms, Typeform, SurveyMonkey or any tool), a table of aggregates from your product, or a description of what you could pull.
- **Permission to use it.** If the report uses customer data, your terms or privacy policy must allow aggregated, de-identified use in published research. The skill asks before it touches customer data.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP,** with BigQuery or Google Sheets for the data, Search Console for the searches people run for statistics on your site, and DataForSEO for what already ranks.

## Example requests

**Build the report from your data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "original-research-report" skill. Write our 2026 benchmark report from the booking data in BigQuery and the owner survey in this CSV. Show me the query before you run it.

**Plan a study before you have data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "original-research-report" skill. We want to publish an industry report next spring. Pick the question worth answering, tell me what data we need and how many survey answers it takes.

**Year in review from usage data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "original-research-report" skill. Write a year-in-review report from our platform usage data, comparing this year with last year for the same customers.

**Check a survey report before it goes out**
Hey Claude, I just added the "original-research-report" skill. Here is our draft survey report and the results export. Tell me which stats hold up, which to drop, and rewrite the rest so journalists can quote them correctly.

## What you get

1. **A study card:** the question and why it beat the others, the sources, the unit of analysis and the precision rules.
2. **A findings ledger:** every candidate stat with its base, 95% range, test and whether it is new compared with what already ranks.
3. **5 to 10 headline findings** in wording that is safe to quote, plus the supporting findings and what the data could not say.
4. **Chart specs** for each finding.
5. **A report outline and a methods section** ready to publish.
6. **A one-page stat sheet** with the citation line, a press angle, three social posts and a sales line.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can put the chart data into a new Google Sheets tab and draw the chart there. It never edits an existing tab, never changes your data and never publishes or pitches anything.

## Tips

- Name the comparisons you care about before you look at the results. The skill tests only those, with a threshold adjusted for how many there are.
- A finding the data can't carry is listed, not hidden. Publishing that list makes the rest of the report more credible.
- Run it again with next year's data and the same definitions to get a year-on-year report.
