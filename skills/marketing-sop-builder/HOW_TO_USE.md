# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-sop-builder" skill. Turn my notes on how we launch a paid campaign into an SOP with a checklist, and check it against our Google Ads and Meta accounts.

## What you need

- **How the process runs today:** a description, meeting notes, a transcript of a screen recording (with timestamps if you have them) or the old SOP. Nothing else is required.
- **Who does, checks and approves it,** if the source doesn't say. The skill won't invent owners; gaps become open questions.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected,** with Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4 or Google Tag Manager. Then every step that names a conversion action, campaign name, tag, key event, pixel or budget is checked against the live account.
- **Optional: your naming convention and monthly budget plan,** for the naming and budget headroom checks.

## Example requests

**Campaign launch SOP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-sop-builder" skill. Here's the transcript of Leo building our last launch and our call notes. Write the launch SOP and check every step against our Google Ads, Meta and GTM.

**Monthly reporting SOP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-sop-builder" skill. Write an SOP for our monthly marketing report from these notes, and mark which steps a skill can run on a schedule.

**Refresh an old SOP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-sop-builder" skill. Our tracking QA SOP in Notion is a year old. Read it, check it against our GA4 key events and GTM tags, and tell me what's out of date.

**SOP library**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-sop-builder" skill. We run launches, a weekly pacing check, monthly reporting, blog publishing and a quarterly tracking audit. Which SOPs should we write first, and what's our operating rhythm?

## What you get

1. **What the live checks found:** account issues that block the process, cleanup items, SOP fixes (before and after) and open questions.
2. **The SOP:** purpose and done-means, trigger and frequency, who does what, tools and access, numbered steps with owner, timing, minutes and a "done when" check, a quality gate, what to do when something breaks, common mistakes, open questions and a change log.
3. **A one-page checklist,** one short line per step.
4. **Automation marks:** each step is Manual, Assist or Schedule, with the skill that can take it if you have it installed.
5. **In library mode:** your processes ranked by hours a year and risk, and the next run date of each.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can save the SOP as a Notion page under a parent page you pick. It never changes an ad account, a tag container or a GA4 property: account issues are reported with who should fix them.

## Tips

- A recording with timestamps gives real step times. Without one, the skill uses the times you state and marks the rest "ask the owner".
- Run it again after a process changes or something breaks, and bump the version in the change log.
- Write one process per SOP. If a source mixes two, the skill splits them.
