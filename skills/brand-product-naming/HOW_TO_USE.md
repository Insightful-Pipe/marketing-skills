# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-product-naming" skill. Help me name our new product and check which names are already taken.

## What you need

- **A few lines about what you're naming:** what it does, who buys it, three traits the name should carry, the countries you sell in, and the competitors a buyer would compare you with. The skill asks for anything missing and shows the defaults it used.
- **Your must-haves,** if any: a .com, an App Store or Google Play listing, an X handle.
- **Names you already like.** They go into the list and get the same test as the rest.

**Optional, for live clash checks:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude. DataForSEO shows who already uses each name on Google, how many people search the word each month, and, for the top 3 names, which domains with the name are registered. The domain check is built in. App Store and Google Play search ran on live data when this skill was built, though the InsightfulPipe site still lists both as coming soon; if your connection doesn't offer them, the skill gives you those two searches as a checklist. X shows whether the handle is taken; that call is billed to your own X developer account, so the skill asks first.

No connection? The skill runs the brief, the long list and the scores the same way, then gives you a short checklist for each shortlisted name and scores what you paste back.

## Example requests

**A new product**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-product-naming" skill. We're launching an assistant that answers booking texts and fills cancellations from a waitlist. Help me name it and check the names aren't already taken.

**A new business**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-product-naming" skill. I'm starting a dog grooming business in Austin and Toronto. Give me name ideas that work in English and French, and check the domains.

**A rename**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-product-naming" skill. Our app's name clashes with a bigger app. Find us a new one that's easy to spell after hearing it on a sales call.

**Test my favourites**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-product-naming" skill. Here are my five favourite names. Score them and check each one on Google, the app stores and X before I pick.

**A feature or a plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-product-naming" skill. Name our new reporting feature. It sits after our brand name, so it doesn't need its own domain.

## What you get

1. **A naming brief** to approve before any names.
2. **The criteria and their points:** fit, how it sounds, whether people can spell it after hearing it, length, meaning in each of your languages, and distance from competitor names.
3. **A long list of 30 names** in five styles, each scored out of 12 with the facts the score rests on, and the knockouts with their reason.
4. **A shortlist of 6** checked for clashes: Google results, monthly searches for the word, App Store and Google Play, domain status for all 6 and registered domains for the top 3, and X handles, each marked red, amber or green with what was found.
5. **Three finalists** with why each works and what each would cost you.
6. **Your next checks:** the trademark registers to search, the registrar check, and the native-speaker check. The skill never says a name is legally clear.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads public results only, and never registers a domain, claims a handle or posts anything.

## Tips

- Write the positioning first if you can. A name is judged against what the brand stands for.
- Say which criterion matters most to you (for example spelling, if most customers find you by phone). The skill doubles its points.
- Before you fall for a finalist, say it out loud to three people and ask them to write it down.
