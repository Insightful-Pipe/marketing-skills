# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Plan our holiday season from last year's data and tell me if we're ready for Black Friday.

## What you need

- **A year of history, connected or pasted.** With the InsightfulPipe MCP connected (GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, Klaviyo, Shopify, or Meta Ads for the Meta mode), the skill pulls the history itself. With no connection, paste or upload daily exports and it runs the same steps on them.
- **Your business model,** if it isn't obvious: online store, lead generation, B2B or SaaS, or local services.
- **Your own dates,** if you have them: last shipping day, sale dates, office closures, or an event that matters to you (Diwali, Lunar New Year, a launch).
- **Your limits,** for example "no discounts deeper than 20%". Promotion ideas stay inside them.
- **For the year-round calendar:** a list of last year's sales (dates, offer, depth, scope), the countries you sell to, and any product drops or anniversaries.
- **For the Meta Ads plan:** last year's fourth quarter from Ads Manager by week (or the Meta connection), your offer dates, the last shipping day, and a target cost per purchase or ROAS if you have one.

## Example requests

**Black Friday plan from history**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Build our Black Friday and December plan from what happened last year, with budget by phase.

**Seasonality forecast**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Forecast our Google Ads leads week by week for the next 90 days, based on last year's pattern.

**Readiness check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Check whether we're ready for Black Friday: tracking, page speed, email flows, budget, and stock if the store is connected.

**Promotion calendar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Give me a dated promotion calendar from now to New Year, with ideas that fit a SaaS business.

**Year-round retail promotion calendar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Here are last year's sales. Build our ecommerce marketing calendar for the next twelve months, with how deep and how often we should discount and which sales to drop.

**Meta Ads Black Friday plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Read last year's fourth quarter in our Meta ad account week by week and plan this year's Black Friday Facebook ads: budget by week, when to raise it, creative and offer dates, and the January wind-down.

**Agency, several clients**
Hey Claude, I just added the "holiday-campaign-planner" skill. Plan the holiday season for each of my clients separately. Don't mix their accounts.

## What you get

1. **This year's calendar,** with each date worked out by rule and matched to last year's date for the same event.
2. **Last season, window by window:** BFCM, the December run-up and the Christmas lull, each measured against a baseline and adjusted for growth, then graded peak, flat or dip. With two seasons of history, it says whether the pattern held both years.
3. **A 13-week outlook,** week by week, with a flag on weeks where the last two years disagree.
4. **A readiness check:** tracking, promotion page speed, email, budget headroom, and store stock and discounts when Shopify is connected.
5. **The plan:** phases with dates, a budget factor and daily budget for each, bid notes for short events, a promotion calendar with lead times, and promotion ideas tied to your data.
6. **What couldn't be checked,** and why.

**Year-round promotion calendar mode:** every past sale measured from your daily orders (lift, the wait before it, the dip after it, net extra revenue, how much was pulled forward), then a depth cap, a minimum gap between sales and a full-price floor set from that history, and a twelve-month calendar of sales, full-price gifting pushes, product drops and clearances, with dates worked out by rule for the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Sales that lost money last year are left out, with the reason.

**Meta Ads holiday plan mode:** last year's weeks around Black Friday with CPM and cost per purchase against a baseline, the split by prospecting, retargeting and offer campaigns, last year's budget edits and winning ads, then this year's expected CPM and cost by week, a daily budget path raised in steps, a creative and offer calendar, a learning-phase check per ad set and the January wind-down.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, and one yes per change, it can:
- draft Klaviyo email campaigns for dates on the calendar. Klaviyo creates them as drafts; the skill never schedules or sends them;
- save the promotion calendar as a new tab in a Google Sheet you name;
- apply one Meta budget step on its date (a new daily budget on an ad set or campaign).

It never changes bids, discounts, stock or Google Ads budgets, and it never pauses or switches on an ad. It recommends those with the numbers behind them.

## Tips

- Run it 8 to 10 weeks before Black Friday, so there is time to fix what the readiness check finds.
- A site under a year old can't show a season yet. The skill says so instead of guessing, and still gives you the calendar and the readiness check.
- Run it again next autumn. It will have one more season of history, so more windows can be graded and checked across two seasons.
