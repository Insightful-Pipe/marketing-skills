# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-response-kit" skill. Something went wrong today and people are talking about it. Tell me how serious it is and write what we need to say.

## What you need

- **What happened,** when it started, and what your team has confirmed so far. Say who confirmed each part.
- **How many customers are affected,** if you know, and how many active customers you have.
- **Links** to the posts, threads or articles people are sharing.
- **Who approves statements** and who speaks for the business.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** connected to X, Facebook Pages, Instagram, DataForSEO, Search Console, the Social Media Scheduler, Klaviyo or Mailchimp, Meta Ads and Google Ads. The skill then reads mention counts, comments, news coverage, branded search and everything scheduled to go out itself. Without it, paste what you have and it works from that.

## Example requests

**Live incident**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-response-kit" skill. Our checkout has been down for 50 minutes and customers are posting about it. Rate how bad it is and write the holding statement first.

**One negative article**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-response-kit" skill. A news site published a story about us this morning with two numbers that are wrong. Here's the link. How should we respond to the negative press?

**One viral complaint**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-response-kit" skill. A customer's post about a late order has 3,000 reposts. Write the private message to them first, then our public reply.

**Pause what's scheduled**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-response-kit" skill. We're in the middle of a PR crisis. List every post, email and ad going out in the next three days and tell me which to pause. Pause them one at a time after I say yes.

**Update and review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "crisis-response-kit" skill. It's two hours since our holding statement. Re-read the mentions, re-rate the level and write the next update. Then start the post-incident review.

## What you get

1. **A severity level** (Watch, Incident or Crisis) set by numbers: mention rate against the same hours on earlier days, comments on your own posts, news and blog coverage, the share of customers affected, downtime and branded search, with hard triggers such as personal data or safety going straight to Crisis.
2. **A facts sheet** splitting what is confirmed from what is still being checked. Public pieces use confirmed facts only; the rest become fill-in fields.
3. **The pieces, in the order they go out:** the holding statement, the staff note, the customer email, social replies by comment type, a reactive Q&A built from the questions people are actually asking, correction requests for inaccurate articles, and the full statement and apology once the facts are in.
4. **A pause list** of the real scheduled posts, emails, texts, flows and ads, each with the exact change and the daily ad budget it stops.
5. **Update times** and a **post-incident review** with every promise, its owner and its due date.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on each single item, it can pause or move scheduled posts, stop a scheduled Klaviyo or Mailchimp campaign, hold a Klaviyo flow, pause a Meta or Google Ads campaign, post the statement or replies on X and Facebook, reply to Instagram comments, and send the staff note to Slack. It never deletes posts or comments, never hides criticism, and never sends the customer email itself.

## Tips

- Run it as soon as you know something is wrong, even with half the facts. The holding statement only needs what is confirmed.
- Run it again at every update time. The level can go down as well as up.
- It is not legal advice. When personal data, safety, a regulator, a lawsuit or an allegation against a leader is involved, your lawyer clears each public piece first.
