# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "web-to-app-funnel" skill. Should we sell our app subscription on the web before the install? Model it with our numbers and plan the funnel.

## What you need

- **Your plans, prices and plan mix,** and how many payments a monthly payer makes in the first year.
- **The store fee you pay today** on each store, and the share of payers on iOS and Android.
- **Your current app funnel per paid click:** click to install, install to trial, trial to paid.
- **Your web numbers,** measured from a pilot or estimated step by step. Estimates are labelled as estimates in the report.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads or Stripe connected. The skill then reads your web step counts, cost per click, pixels and conversion actions itself.

## Example requests

**Should we do it at all**
Hey Claude, I just added the "web-to-app-funnel" skill. We pay Apple 30% and Google 15%. Our yearly plan is $49.99 and 3% of ad clicks become payers in the app. Is a web funnel worth it, and what conversion rate would it need?

**Judge a pilot**
Hey Claude, I just added the "web-to-app-funnel" skill. We ran a two-week web quiz pilot on Meta next to our app-install campaign. Should we move budget to the web funnel, and what do we have to fix first?

**Plan the funnel and the tracking**
Hey Claude, I just added the "web-to-app-funnel" skill. We've decided to build a web2app funnel. Design the quiz, the web paywall, checkout and the handoff into the app, with the GA4, Meta and TikTok events for each step.

**Link out from the app**
Hey Claude, I just added the "web-to-app-funnel" skill. Can our US iOS app link from the paywall to web checkout now, and what would we keep per subscriber if Apple charges a commission on it?

## What you get

1. **The store rules as of today,** read from Apple's and Google's own pages, each with its link and the date it was read.
2. **The money on both paths** per 1,000 ad clicks: payers, net revenue and profit, the fee saved per payer, and the web conversion rate you need to break even.
3. **A recommendation:** build, test or not yet, with the one number that decides it.
4. **The funnel blueprint,** step by step from the ad to the first app login.
5. **The tracking plan:** the event for each step on GA4, Meta, Google Ads and TikTok, how to deduplicate them, and how to tie the web purchase to the app login.
6. **Findings, a test plan when one is needed, and every assumption** with the result it moves.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- mark a funnel event as a GA4 key event
- add a website subscription conversion action in Google Ads
- add a Meta custom conversion on your pixel

It never changes budgets, campaigns, prices, store listings or checkout settings, and never installs code.

## Tips

- Run a small pilot before you rebuild anything. A measured web conversion rate beats any estimate.
- Keep selling the subscription in the app. Apple's rules require it for anything bought on the web that the app unlocks.
- Measure how many web payers log into the app. A payer who never opens it is a refund waiting to happen.
