# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "in-app-events-featuring-planner" skill. Here is our release roadmap for the next six months. Plan our App Store in-app events, tell us which ones to nominate for featuring and by when, and tell us what we can do on Google Play.

## What you need

- **Your roadmap and promotions** with dates: releases, new content, seasons, challenges, sales. Say which ones have an end date and how many weeks each one can move.
- **The app:** App Store, Google Play or both; game or app; category; main countries and listing languages.
- **3 to 5 search phrases** for what your app does, or let the skill build them from your listing.
- **Optional, for past results:** a daily downloads export from App Store Connect or Play Console covering each past event and the 28 days before it, the events' dates, and any ad budget or listing change in those weeks.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With DataForSEO it reads 12 months of search demand for your category itself. With the App Store and Google Play servers it reads the leading rival listings, so the featuring pitch only claims what is really different.

## Example requests

**Plan the calendar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "in-app-events-featuring-planner" skill. We ship a new feature on January 12 and a spring content pack in March, and we run a sale on Black Friday. Build our App Store in-app events calendar with every deadline.

**Get featured**
Hey Claude, I just added the "in-app-events-featuring-planner" skill. How do we get featured on the App Store for our March update? Tell us if it's worth a nomination, draft it, and give us the date to submit by.

**Google Play promotional content**
Hey Claude, I just added the "in-app-events-featuring-planner" skill. Can our app use Google Play promotional content? If it can, write the items for our next three moments; if not, tell us what to do on Play instead.

**Did our last events work**
Hey Claude, I just added the "in-app-events-featuring-planner" skill. Here is our App Store Connect daily downloads export and the dates of our last three in-app events. Which ones really lifted downloads?

## What you get

1. **The plan in three lines:** the nominations and their submit-by dates, the events, and what happens on Google Play.
2. **Demand by month** for your category, so events and nominations land in the months people search most.
3. **Each moment checked:** an App Store event or not (and the badge), a Play promotional content type or not, nominate or skip, with the reason.
4. **A dated calendar** of every task: review submissions, publish dates, nomination deadlines, listing changes and reverts.
5. **Event cards** written inside Apple's and Google's limits, with character counts.
6. **Featuring nomination drafts** in the App Store Connect format.
7. **Past events measured** against a clean baseline, with any window an ad budget change spoils marked as such.

## What it changes

Nothing. You submit the events, nominations and listing changes in App Store Connect and Play Console.

## Tips

- Give the flexible dates. A content pack that can move three weeks is often worth moving into a busier month.
- Tell the skill about ad budget changes around past events. Otherwise an ad push can look like an event that worked.
- Rerun it each quarter with the new roadmap and the latest export.
