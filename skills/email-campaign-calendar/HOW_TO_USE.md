# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "email-campaign-calendar" skill. Plan next quarter's email calendar: how often each part of my list should get mail, what goes out when, and a preference center.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is fine.** Describe the business and paste a campaign export for the last 12 months: per send, the name, date, audience, recipients, clicks, unsubscribes, spam complaints and revenue.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** with Klaviyo or Mailchimp. The skill then reads your segments, their sizes and every sent campaign's results itself. With GA4 and WordPress added, it fills the content slots with your best posts and the ones you have scheduled.
- **Your offers and launches for the quarter, with dates.** Promotional sends are planned only for real offers.
- **Stories and product angles** you want to tell. Without them, the calendar shows open slots instead of made-up subjects.

## Example requests

**A quarter's calendar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-campaign-calendar" skill. Plan our Q1 email calendar from our Klaviyo history. Our offers are a winter clearance on January 12 to 17 and a long weekend sale on February 12 to 15.

**How often to send**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-campaign-calendar" skill. How often should we send marketing emails? Use our own unsubscribes and revenue, not an industry rule.

**From an export**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-campaign-calendar" skill. Here is our Mailchimp campaign export for the last year. Set a send frequency per engagement tier and build the next 13 weeks.

**Preference center**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-campaign-calendar" skill. Design an email preference center so people can choose fewer emails instead of unsubscribing, and tell me which sends each choice gets.

## What you get

1. **Engagement tiers** (active, warm, cooling, dormant) with how many people are in each now.
2. **The fatigue curve:** for each sends-per-week level your list has run, the unsubscribe and complaint rate per send, revenue per recipient and the subscribers lost, with the pick and why.
3. **A frequency per tier,** and lower tiers getting a subset of the same sends, never extra mail.
4. **The mix** of promotional, product, story and content sends, from each type's own results.
5. **A dated calendar** for the quarter: one row per send with the date, type, tiers, a one-line brief, the call to action and where the subject came from.
6. **One frequency test,** with the arms, the split, the dates and the sample size from your own order rate.
7. **A preference center:** the choices, how many sends each gets in the quarter, how to wire them in Klaviyo or Mailchimp, and the copy.

## What it can save for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- write the calendar to a new tab in a Google Sheets spreadsheet you name
- save draft campaigns in Klaviyo or Mailchimp for the sends you pick

It never schedules or sends anything. You schedule each send in your email platform.

## Tips

- Name your sale months (default November and December). They are set aside when reading the curve, because people tolerate more mail then.
- If your engaged segment counts opens, the skill flags it: Apple Mail privacy opens make almost everyone look engaged.
- Run it again each quarter. The test result and a new quarter of sends move the curve.
