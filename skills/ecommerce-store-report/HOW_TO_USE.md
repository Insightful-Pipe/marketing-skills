# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-store-report" skill. Build last month's store report from my orders and tell me the three biggest movements.

## What you need

- **Your orders.** Either an orders export (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or any cart, as CSV) or your store connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP. With no connection, the export is enough.
- **Enough history.** The export should cover the month you want, the month before and the same month last year. For new and returning buyers, add each customer's first order date (a customers export), or export orders from 12 months before the earliest month.
- **A refunds export with dates,** if you have one. It lets the report show refunds the way your store's own sales report books them.
- **GA4 and Klaviyo, if connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP.** They add sessions, the online conversion rate and email-attributed revenue next to the store's numbers.

## Example requests

**Monthly report from an export**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-store-report" skill. Here is my Shopify orders export. Build my September sales report against August and last September.

**Weekly trading update**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-store-report" skill. Give me last week's store report against the week before and the same week last year, in a short format I can send to the team.

**Why did sales drop?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-store-report" skill. Our store sales fell this month. Tell me which channel, country or product caused it.

**Month to date**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-store-report" skill. How is this month going so far compared with the same days last month?

**Report, then save it**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-store-report" skill. Build the monthly report and save the KPI table to a new tab in my reporting spreadsheet. Show me the table first.

## What you get

1. **The three biggest movements,** each explained in plain words with the numbers behind it.
2. **A KPI table:** net sales, gross sales, discounts, refunds, orders, AOV, buyers, new and returning buyers, discount and refund share, each against the prior period and the same period last year.
3. **Breakdowns** by channel (online, point of sale, wholesale, marketplace apps), country, payment method and buyer type, plus the rising and falling products.
4. **A refunds section** that shows refunds on the month's own orders and refunds processed during the month, and explains the gap between the two net sales figures.
5. **Flags** for a partial period and for refunds that can still arrive.
6. **Traffic and email lines** when GA4 or Klaviyo is connected.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the KPI table to a new tab in a Google Sheets spreadsheet. It never changes orders, refunds, products or discounts in your store.

## Tips

- Run it on the 2nd or 3rd of the month, so the last day's orders are in.
- If your return window is longer than 30 days, say so: the report then knows how long refunds can keep arriving.
- Use the same export columns every month, so the numbers compare like for like.
