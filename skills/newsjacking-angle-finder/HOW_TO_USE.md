# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "newsjacking-angle-finder" skill. What's in the news today that we can comment on? Draft the comments, don't send anything.

## What you need

- **Your topics and spokespeople.** A few themes you can speak on, and who can speak on each.
- **One or two numbers you can share,** such as a rate from your own product or customer data, with the period and source. Every comment carries one, because that is what makes a reporter quote you.
- **Your no-go list,** and whether you ever comment on politics or policy.
- **Nothing else is required.** Paste links or headlines and the skill checks them. For a story watch that finds stories itself, connect X through the InsightfulPipe MCP; DataForSEO, Search Console and GA4 add momentum, topic ranking and quotable facts, but they don't find stories.

## Example requests

**Daily check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsjacking-angle-finder" skill. Check today's X news and X trends for anything in our field we could comment on, and draft the comments. (Needs X connected.)

**Is this one worth it?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsjacking-angle-finder" skill. Here's a headline that's going around. Should we comment on it, and if so, what do we say and to whom?

**No connections**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsjacking-angle-finder" skill. Here are five links from this morning, our topics and our spokesperson. Tell me which ones we should jump on.

**Build the topic map first**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsjacking-angle-finder" skill. Use our Search Console queries to work out which topics we should watch for reactive PR.

## What you get

1. **A topic map:** your themes, which are core (by your own search impressions) and who covers each.
2. **Today's list:** every story that matched, with its decision (Send now, Send today, Prepare, Needs a fact, Skip or Ruled out), fit, timing, momentum and send-by time.
3. **Drafts** for up to 5 stories: the angle, a two or three sentence comment with one of your own numbers, who to send it to, and a risk check.
4. **What it ruled out and why,** so you can see it never suggests commenting on a disaster, an election or a lawsuit.
5. **What would unlock a story** that needs a number you haven't shared yet.

## What it never does

It never posts, replies, sends a message or emails anyone. Every comment is a draft for your spokesperson to approve and send. X calls are billed to your own X developer account, so it keeps pages small and tells you the estimated cost.

## Tips

- Run it early in the working day. Most stories are worth commenting on for about a day.
- Keep a short list of approved numbers ready. A story without one goes to "Needs a fact".
- If a reporter answers, reply within the hour. A late reply loses the story.
