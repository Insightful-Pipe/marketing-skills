# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. Find every review and comment we haven't answered and draft replies in our voice. Don't post anything yet.

## What you need

- **Nothing, to start.** Paste reviews, comments or mentions and the skill ranks them and drafts the replies.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with any of these added: Google Business Profile, a Facebook Page, an Instagram business account, a Meta ad account, a TikTok Ads account, X, YouTube or a LinkedIn Page. Then the skill reads reviews, comments and mentions itself, so there's nothing to export. TikTok ad comments are the exception: you paste those in (see below). App Store and Google Play reviews need only your app's store id.
- **Which pages and locations are yours,** if your workspace also holds clients' accounts. The skill only reads the ones you pick.
- **Optional:** three words for your voice, a contact route for complaints (email, phone or form), a sign-off, anything a reply must never mention, and the facts replies may quote (plans, features, policies). Without them it matches your Page's tone, points people to your website (a link in comment replies; named in words in review replies, which carry no links) and signs as "The {business} team". Without the facts, any answer that needs one gets a [fact needed] gap for you to fill.

## Example requests

**Unanswered reviews queue**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. List every Google review we haven't replied to, worst first, with a draft reply for each.

**Reply to comments**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. Check our Facebook Page and Instagram for comments that still need an answer and draft the replies. Our voice is friendly, direct and a bit nerdy.

**Ad comment sentiment**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. How do people feel about our Facebook and TikTok ads? Show me which ads get complaints and what they're about.

**X, YouTube and LinkedIn reply queue**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. Go through our X mentions, YouTube comments and LinkedIn Page comments, show me what still needs a reply, worst first, and tell me which YouTube comments to pin or heart.

**App Store and Google Play review replies**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. Find our unanswered 1 to 3 star reviews on Google Play and the App Store, draft replies within each store's rules, and group the bug reports by app version for the product team.

**Meta ad comments moderator and buyer doubts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. Read the comments under our running Meta ads, list the scams and spam to hide, and tell me what buyers doubt, starting with our most expensive ad, with one fix for each doubt.

**Draft, then post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "reviews-and-comments-responder" skill. Draft replies to our unanswered reviews, then post the ones I approve.

## What you get

1. **Coverage:** for each source, how many items it read, how many were answered and your response rate. Facebook comments that have replies from someone the skill can't identify are listed for you to check, and the rate shows as a range until you do. App Store reply status shows as unknown unless you paste it.
2. **A reply queue:** every review, comment or mention that needs an answer, ranked P1 to P3 and oldest first. Each one shows its age, whether it's late, its sentiment and a short draft reply.
3. **Ad comments and buyer doubts:** per ad, highest spend first, what to hide and what buyers doubt (will it work for me, is it worth the price, can I trust you, how does it work), with one fix per recurring doubt: an opening line, a landing page answer or a reply template.
4. **An app review bug report:** low-star themes by store and app version, with no reviewer names or quotes, ready for the product team.
5. **YouTube pin and heart picks.**
6. **A hide list** for spam, scams and abuse only. Criticism gets an answer, not hidden.
7. **What it needs from you:** missing facts or contact details the drafts wait on.
8. **Unknowns:** sources that aren't connected.

## What it can change for you

Only after you approve the exact reply, one at a time, it can:
- reply to a Google review
- reply to a Facebook Page or Meta ad comment, or hide spam, scams and abuse (one at a time or as one approved batch per post)
- reply to or hide an Instagram comment (untested until an Instagram account is connected)
- reply to an X mention, or hide a spam reply under your own X post (untested: the X calls haven't been run live yet)
- reply to a YouTube comment

It never deletes anything. You post these yourself, from the drafts: TikTok replies (TikTok doesn't share comment text through the API, so you paste those comments in), LinkedIn replies, Google Play and App Store replies, and YouTube pins and hearts.

## Tips

- Run it weekly. A reply within a day or two counts for much more than a perfect one a month later.
- Give it your contact route once. Every complaint reply needs one.
- Google checks review replies before they appear, so a reply you post may take a while to show up.
- X bills every call to your own X developer app. The skill reads 30 days of mentions and shows the estimated cost; replies without a link cost less.
- Google Play replies are capped at 350 characters, and the reviewer is notified the first time you reply.
