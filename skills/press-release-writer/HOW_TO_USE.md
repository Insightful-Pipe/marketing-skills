# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "press-release-writer" skill. Write a press release for our new feature launch next Tuesday, and check our numbers before it goes out.

## What you need

- **The facts:** what's happening, when, where, who it's for, and why it matters.
- **Where each number comes from:** analytics, billing, a pilot, a survey. Numbers without a source are cut.
- **Who can be quoted,** with their titles, and any draft quotes.
- **Your boilerplate (or your last release) and media contact,** if you have them.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** with GA4, Shopify, Google Sheets, DataForSEO or WordPress connected. Nothing is required; each connection lets the skill check more itself.

## Example requests

**Product launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "press-release-writer" skill. We launch Waitlist Autofill on Nov. 10. Here are the pilot results, a quote from our CEO and our boilerplate. Write the release and the embargo plan.

**Funding round**
Hey Claude, I just added the "press-release-writer" skill. We closed a $6 million seed round led by two investors. Write the release, and tell me whether a wire is worth it.

**Data story**
Hey Claude, I just added the "press-release-writer" skill. We surveyed 800 salon owners about cancellations. Turn the findings into a release, and check the percentages against the sheet.

**Event**
Hey Claude, I just added the "press-release-writer" skill. We're opening a new office in Toronto with a ribbon cutting on March 3. Write the media advisory.

**Is this news?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "press-release-writer" skill. We hired a CFO, refreshed our logo and passed 12,000 customers. Which of these deserves a release?

## What you get

1. **A decision:** release, media advisory, not a release, or hold, and where each other item in your brief should go.
2. **A claim check:** every number and superlative with its source, what was measured and a verdict (supported, adjust, from you, unsupported).
3. **Coverage research:** which outlets covered similar news in your category in the last year, and the angle the release takes because of it.
4. **The release:** three headlines, two subheads, dateline, a 40 to 60 word lead, a 400 to 600 word body with two quotes that say something new, boilerplate, contact and the end mark, with the counts shown.
5. **A reactive Q&A** for the questions reporters will ask.
6. **An embargo and distribution plan:** exclusive, direct pitch or wire, with dates.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact post, it can save the release as a WordPress draft in your press category. It always saves a draft, never publishes, and never sends a pitch or buys a wire.

## Tips

- Give the source and date for every number, even the ones you're sure of. Reporters ask.
- Quotes come back marked as drafts. Send them to the speaker for approval before the release goes anywhere.
- One release, one news item. If you have three announcements, the skill tells you which one leads and where the others belong.
- The sample run uses an invented booking software company, Example Booking Software, so you can see the full output before you run it on your own news.
