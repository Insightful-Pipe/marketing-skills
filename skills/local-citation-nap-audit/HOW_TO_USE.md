# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "local-citation-nap-audit" skill. Check that my business name, address, phone, website and hours match everywhere I'm listed, and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Paste your business details and any listing URLs or listing text, and the skill audits what you give it.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With Google Business Profile added, your live profile becomes the source of truth. The Web Crawler reads the business markup on your site, Facebook Pages reads your page details, and DataForSEO finds your Maps entries and directory listings by name and phone.
- **Your old details,** if you have any: a previous name, address or phone number. These find the stale listings that do the most harm.
- **Any call-tracking numbers you use on purpose,** so they aren't flagged as wrong.

## Example requests

**Full NAP check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-citation-nap-audit" skill. Run a NAP consistency check for my business and rank the fixes.

**After a move**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-citation-nap-audit" skill. We moved in 2024 from 22 Mill Street. Find every listing that still shows the old address or the old phone.

**Duplicate listings**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-citation-nap-audit" skill. Customers keep finding a second Google Maps listing for us. Find duplicates and tell me how to deal with each one.

**Local citations list**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-citation-nap-audit" skill. Which directories should my dental practice be listed on next? Only the ones that matter for dentists in my city.

## What you get

1. **The source of truth** the audit compares against.
2. **A consistency summary:** listings checked, how many match, how many have a wrong field, duplicates, and anything left unread.
3. **A ranked fix list:** duplicates and wrong phone numbers first, then wrong addresses, then website, hours and name, with where to make each change.
4. **A grade for every field on every listing:** match, style only, wrong, missing or unread.
5. **Where to list next:** core platforms you're missing, then directories that rank for your category and city where you aren't listed yet.
6. **Unknowns,** such as a platform it couldn't check.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It's read only, and every fix comes with where to make it. If your site's business markup is wrong, it writes the corrected JSON-LD for you or your developer to paste.

## Tips

- Run it after a move, a new phone number or a rebrand, and then every quarter.
- Fix the duplicates and the wrong phone numbers before you add any new listing.
- Paste your Apple Maps and Bing Places details: there's no connector for them, so the skill can only check them through search results otherwise.
