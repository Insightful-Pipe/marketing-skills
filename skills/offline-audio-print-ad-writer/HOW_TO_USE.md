# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Read how our last radio flight and postcard drop did, then write a 30-second radio spot and a postcard for our next offer, each with its own promo code.

## What you need

- **A short brief:** what you're promoting, the offer (or two to choose between), the formats with their length or size, the dates, and the claims you can stand behind.
- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** (optional), with any of these added:
  - CallRail: calls to each tracking number during each flight or drop
  - Shopify or WooCommerce: orders that used each promo code
  - Google Analytics 4: visits from your short URLs and QR codes
  - Google Search Console and Google Business Profile: brand searches, direction requests and call clicks during each window

  The skill pulls these itself.
- **Nothing connected?** Paste your call counts, an order export with the discount code column, or just a list of past pieces with dates and costs. With no history at all, you still get finished copy, with the offer marked untested.

## Example requests

**Which offline ad worked**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Compare our two radio flights and the A/B postcard drop from this fall by cost per response, and tell me which offer and headline to repeat.

**Radio and TV scripts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Write a 30-second and a 15-second radio spot for our spring sale, and a 30-second TV version with the audio and picture side by side.

**Podcast host read or newsletter sponsorship**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Write talking points and the required lines for a 60-second podcast host read, and a sponsored slot for a local newsletter, each with its own code.

**Direct mail**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Write an EDDM postcard and a one-page sales letter for our open house, and check that the postcard size qualifies.

**Billboard, transit and print**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Write a roadside billboard in 7 words or fewer, a bus shelter poster and a half-page newspaper ad for the same offer.

**Review a draft**
Hey Claude, I just added the "offline-audio-print-ad-writer" skill. Here is our radio script. Is it too long for 30 seconds, and how do we track it?

## What you get

1. **The past push read:** responses and cost per response for each piece, a verdict on any split test, the brand-search and Business Profile lift during each window, and the offer and line to repeat.
2. **The angle:** the offer and the line, with the evidence that chose them.
3. **Finished pieces:** every word counted against the format's limit (2.5 spoken words a second, 7 words on a roadside board, postcard copy by side), with the key built in.
4. **A response tracking plan:** a code, tracking number, short URL or QR for every piece, its UTM tags, and when and how to read it.
5. **Offered setup:** the coupon codes and the CallRail tracking number as exact payloads, run only if you say yes.
6. **A checklist,** each rule marked pass or fix.

## What it changes

Nothing unless you confirm. It can create the WooCommerce coupons and a CallRail tracking number for you, one at a time, after you approve each.

## Tips

- Give every piece its own code, even two halves of one mail drop. A piece without a key can't be judged next time.
- Send the cost of each piece. Without it you get responses but no cost per response.
- For a mail drop through EDDM, give the size first: some common sizes don't qualify.
- For legal, health or finance offers, get the copy signed off before it airs or prints.
