# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-roadmap-planner" skill. Build me a 12-month SEO roadmap from my Search Console and GA4 data, with goals for month 3, 6 and 12.

## What you need

- **Best: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with Google Search Console added.** GA4 adds the lead goal, DataForSEO adds competitors and links, Bing Webmaster adds Bing clicks, and the crawler reads robots.txt. The skill pulls the data itself.
- **No connection? Paste exports instead:** the Search Console Performance report (dates, queries, pages) and GA4 traffic acquisition by month. A competitor keyword export from any SEO tool is optional.
- **No exports either? Answer eight short questions** about the site, its traffic, leads, competitors and team. The roadmap marks every number as "from you" or "assumption".
- **Your hours a month for SEO** and who does what. Without them, the skill plans 40 hours with you as the owner.

## Example requests

**Plateaued site**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-roadmap-planner" skill. Our organic traffic has been flat for a year. Build a 12-month SEO strategy and tell me what to work on first. We have 20 hours a month.

**New website**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-roadmap-planner" skill. We launched our site three months ago. Write an SEO plan for a new website: the first 3, 6 and 12 months.

**Traffic is falling**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-roadmap-planner" skill. Organic clicks are down since last year. Build a roadmap that stops the loss first, then grows again.

**No connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-roadmap-planner" skill. I don't have anything connected. Ask me your questions and build a rough SEO roadmap template I can fill in with real numbers later.

**Save it**
Hey Claude, I just added the "seo-roadmap-planner" skill. Build the roadmap, then put it and the monthly scorecard in a new tab of my planning spreadsheet.

## What you get

1. **A measurement check:** whether GA4 sees your search visits and your leads, before any goal rests on them.
2. **Where you are:** brand and non-brand clicks against last year, leads per click, Bing and AI assistant visits, and your links against your competitors.
3. **Your stage:** new, growing, plateaued or declining, with the math.
4. **Five workstreams sized:** technical, existing pages, new pages, links and AI search, each with the evidence, the upside in clicks a month, the hours, and the skill that does the work.
5. **The roadmap:** months 1 to 3, 4 to 6 and 7 to 12, each item with an owner, hours and what "done" means, and never more hours than you have.
6. **Goals and a monthly scorecard:** clicks and leads for month 3, 6 and 12, items shipped and referring domains, plus a rule for when to re-plan.
7. **Later, assumptions and unknowns:** what didn't fit, every assumed number, and what couldn't be checked.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the roadmap and the scorecard to a new tab in a Google Sheet you choose. It never edits your site, robots.txt, sitemaps or Search Console.

## Tips

- Give real hours. A smaller plan you finish beats a big one you don't.
- Name the searches you can't serve (a product you don't sell, a country you don't ship to), so the new-page list leaves them out.
- Rerun it every quarter, or when the scorecard misses its path two months in a row.
