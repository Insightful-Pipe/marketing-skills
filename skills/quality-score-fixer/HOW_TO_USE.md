# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "quality-score-fixer" skill. Find out why my Google Ads Quality Scores are low and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill pulls keywords, ads and landing pages itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **PageSpeed Insights and SEO Crawler servers,** if you want your landing pages checked live. Without them it still reads Google's own landing page ratings and ad approvals.
- **Anything you changed in the last two weeks,** so it doesn't grade ads or pages that are too new.

## Example requests

**Quality Score breakdown**
Hey Claude, I just added the "quality-score-fixer" skill. Break down Quality Score by expected CTR, ad relevance and landing page for my search campaigns, weighted by spend.

**Ad strength and pinning**
Hey Claude, I just added the "quality-score-fixer" skill. Review the ad strength of my responsive search ads and tell me which pinned headlines are holding them back.

**Landing pages**
Hey Claude, I just added the "quality-score-fixer" skill. Check every final URL in my account: is any broken, slow or rated below average for landing page experience?

**One campaign, then fix**
Hey Claude, I just added the "quality-score-fixer" skill. Fix Quality Score in my DUI campaign. Draft a new ad for the weakest ad group and show it to me before you add it.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for five areas:** Quality Score by component, keyword-to-ad match, ad strength, pinning, and landing pages.
2. **The top fixes,** ranked by the spend each one touches, with the keywords and numbers behind it.
3. **Tables by ad group, ad and final URL,** so you can see where each weak part comes from.
4. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.
5. **A date to run it again,** because Quality Score needs a few weeks of traffic to move.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- add a new responsive search ad (created paused, so nothing spends until you turn it on)
- replace an ad's headlines, descriptions or final URL
- turn the new ad on, or pause the old one

It never changes bids or budgets, moves keywords or edits your website.

## Tips

- Fix ad relevance first. An ad that uses the searcher's words usually lifts expected CTR as well.
- Give changes 2 to 4 weeks before judging them.
- If you manage several accounts under one manager account, say which client account to check.
