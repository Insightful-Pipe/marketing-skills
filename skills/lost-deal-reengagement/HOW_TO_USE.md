# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "lost-deal-reengagement" skill. Here is our closed lost export from the last 18 months. Tell me which deals are worth another try, when, and how.

## What you need

- **Your closed lost deals,** as a CSV, a spreadsheet, a paste or a Google Sheet: company, amount, date lost, the lost reason, the notes and the contacts. No CRM connection needed.
- **What changed since:** features you shipped and price changes, with their dates.
- **Your fit rules,** three to five lines on what a good customer looks like.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP, so the skill can read the sheet, check each account's careers page, ad libraries, news and app, and build a Google Ads audience after you confirm.

## Example requests

**Rank the closed lost pile**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lost-deal-reengagement" skill. Rank our closed lost deals by what they're worth and how well they fit, and give each one a retry date.

**A feature just shipped**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lost-deal-reengagement" skill. We shipped offline mode on August 12. Find every lost deal that needed it and draft the notes from each deal owner.

**Competitor renewals**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lost-deal-reengagement" skill. Which deals did we lose to a competitor whose contract renews in the next six months, and what should we send before it does?

**Closed lost email template and nurture**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lost-deal-reengagement" skill. Write a closed lost email for each loss reason and a three-email nurture for deals that aren't ready yet.

**Ad audience**
Hey Claude, I just added the "lost-deal-reengagement" skill. Build a Google Ads Customer Match audience from our lost deals that can still come back. Show me the list and the checks first.

## What you get

1. **The pipeline at stake,** by loss cause and by status: ready now, warm up, hold, no play, ask the owner.
2. **A ranked retry list:** each deal's cause (and the logged reason when the notes say otherwise), fit, weighted value, retry date with how it was set, evidence of change at the account, and its play.
3. **This week's personal notes,** drafted for each owner within their weekly limit, with the evidence behind each one.
4. **A nurture track per cause group,** three emails each, with the first written in full.
5. **A Customer Match audience plan** with the size, policy and eligibility checks, and the payloads marked "not run".
6. **Questions for owners** about deals with no clear cause.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can create a Google Ads Customer Match list, upload the qualifying contacts to it, and write retry dates and plays into empty columns of your Google Sheet. It never sends an email, never changes a CRM and never reopens a deal.

## Tips

- Paste the rep's notes, not just the lost reason dropdown. The notes usually tell a different story.
- List every shipped feature and price change with its date: each one can reopen a group of deals.
- Run it again each month. Warm-up deals move to a note on their retry date, and new losses join the list.
