# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-architecture" skill. Here are our brands and products. Tell me how they should relate, what to name each one, and what it means for our website, ads and social accounts.

## What you need

- **Your brand and product list:** each product's name, what it does, where it lives online, and whether it's live or planned.
- **Who buys each one:** your customer mix by segment (business type or customer type), as counts or rough shares.
- **What each costs a customer per month,** or your plan prices and plan mix.
- **Three yes or no answers per product** about risk, which the skill asks for.
- **Optional, for measured equity:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with Search Console, DataForSEO, GA4, Google Ads or Meta Ads connected. The skill then pulls name searches, co-searches, traffic, key events and conversion rates itself. Without them, paste what you have and it marks the rest unknown.

## Example requests

**Whole portfolio**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-architecture" skill. We have a core app, a payments add-on with its own name and a company we acquired. Should we be a branded house or a house of brands?

**A new product**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-architecture" skill. We're launching a premium product for enterprise clients at about ten times our current price. Should it carry our name?

**An acquisition**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-architecture" skill. We bought a competitor with a well-known name. Should we merge it into our brand, endorse it or keep it separate?

**Naming tiers and features**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-architecture" skill. Check our plan names and feature names and tell me which ones to rename.

## What you get

1. **A portfolio table:** for each product, the recommended relationship (master brand, sub-brand, endorsed or separate), its new name if any, the rule that decided, and the four measures behind it: name demand, co-search share, audience overlap and price ratio.
2. **The evidence per product,** in a few lines, and what changes now.
3. **A naming system** for products, tiers and features, with every rename and every name that already means something else in search.
4. **A channel plan:** what each choice means for the website, ad campaigns and social accounts.
5. **SEO hand-offs** for any URL or domain move, with the search and traffic numbers SEO needs.
6. **Unknowns:** what couldn't be measured, and why.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It only reads data and returns a plan. You carry out the renames and moves.

## Tips

- Include planned products. The skill tells you before launch whether they should carry your name.
- Give honest risk answers. A product that rates or competes with your own customers is the clearest case for a separate brand.
- Run it again after an acquisition or a big price change.
- Clear every name you keep or adopt with a trademark search. The skill flags names that already mean something else in search, but it never clears a name.
