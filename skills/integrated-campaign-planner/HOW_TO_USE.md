# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "integrated-campaign-planner" skill. Plan our next campaign across ads, email, social, press and creators, and tell me what our budget can actually buy.

## What you need

- **The brief,** in a sentence or two: what you are promoting, who it is for, the dates, the total budget (and how much of it goes to creators, press or production), and the goal, such as sign-ups, sales, leads or people reached.
- **Optional but much better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** with the channels you use. Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads and Pinterest Ads give your real costs; Klaviyo or Mailchimp give your recipients per send and click rates; Instagram, Facebook Pages and LinkedIn Pages give your reach per post; GA4 gives sign-ups per channel. Then the budget and the forecast come from your own numbers.
- **Nothing connected?** Paste your last 90 days per channel (spend, impressions, clicks and results; recipients per send and click rate; reach per post). The skill labels them as yours and sizes the plan the same way.

## Example requests

**Product push**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integrated-campaign-planner" skill. Plan a 6-week push for our reminders feature starting January 11. $30,000 total, $4,000 of it creators and press. We want 900 new sign-ups.

**Seasonal campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integrated-campaign-planner" skill. Plan our spring campaign for April across Meta, Google, email and Instagram. Budget $12,000. Show me the calendar and the asset list.

**Brand campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integrated-campaign-planner" skill. We want more people in our city to know us. Plan a brand campaign for the next two months on $8,000 and tell me how many people it can reach on each platform.

**Always-on theme**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integrated-campaign-planner" skill. Turn "client stories" into an always-on theme with a monthly rhythm across email, LinkedIn and our blog.

**Read-out**
Hey Claude, I just added the "integrated-campaign-planner" skill. The noshow27 campaign ended last week. Read the results back and fill in the read-out.

## What you get

1. **The brief on one page:** goal, audience, offer, dates, budget, a campaign tag, two central ideas with one recommended, the message line and its proof points.
2. **What the budget buys:** per ad line, the budget, expected impressions, clicks and results, and the same "if costs hold", each from that line's own history; email and organic forecasts from your own rates; the gap to your goal, and what would close it.
3. **The budget:** non-media costs, a test reserve, warm-up reach money and results money, each line with its cap; lines left out and why.
4. **The channel plan by phase:** prep, open, build, last call and read-out, with what each channel does in each.
5. **The asset list** with formats, specs, counts, owners and due dates.
6. **A dated calendar** of ads, emails, posts, press and creator dates.
7. **KPIs and a read-out template,** plus a mid-flight check to move money to what works.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- write the calendar and asset list to a Google Sheet
- draft the posts in the Social Media Scheduler
- draft the emails in Klaviyo
- create the ad campaigns paused, on Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and Pinterest

Nothing goes live or spends money. These need a Read & Write connection, and an owner, admin or operator can switch them off.

## Tips

- Give the real split between media and non-media costs; creator fees and production change what is left for ads.
- Use the campaign tag in every campaign name and link. The read-out finds the campaign by it.
- If the plan says the budget can't reach your goal, believe it: lengthen the flight, add budget, or lower the goal.
- Launching something brand new? A launch plan fits better; use launch-strategy if you have it.
