# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "cross-border-store-expansion" skill. We've decided to sell into Canada, the UK, Germany and Australia. Set the store up and tell me if each market is ready.

## What you need

- **The countries you've chosen.** The skill sets the store up for them; it doesn't pick them.
- **Your products:** price, unit cost and weight, plus the HS code and country of origin if you have them.
- **Carrier quotes and duty rates** for each market, if you have them. Without them the skill still shows the method and marks those numbers as estimates to confirm.
- **Optional: your store and analytics connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP.** With Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento, GA4 and Search Console connected, the skill reads your orders, shipping zones, tax countries, policies and traffic by country itself. DataForSEO adds search volumes in each market's language.

## Example requests

**Shopify Markets setup**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cross-border-store-expansion" skill. Help me set up Shopify Markets for the UK and the EU: local prices, rounding, shipping and duties.

**No store connected**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cross-border-store-expansion" skill. I sell candles from the US at $28, cost $7, 0.5 kg each. What should I charge in Canada and Australia, and what should shipping cost?

**Is this market ready?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cross-border-store-expansion" skill. We get a lot of German visitors but almost no German orders. Check Germany against our own data and tell me what's blocking it.

**Pricing in a new currency**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cross-border-store-expansion" skill. Price my catalogue in GBP so we keep our US margin after VAT, duty and currency fees.

## What you get

1. **A verdict per market** from your own orders, conversion and search clicks there: blocked, held back by setup, ready after setup, or no evidence yet.
2. **Local prices on landed cost,** next to what plain currency conversion would give and the margin each earns.
3. **Shipping rates and free-shipping thresholds** worked out from your own order values and your home shipping policy.
4. **Duties and taxes:** how prices should show tax, who pays duty, each market's duty-free line, missing HS codes, and the registrations to confirm.
5. **Payment methods and what to translate first** in each market.
6. **A launch checklist per market,** and the fixes ranked by the orders at stake.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact text, it can draft a hidden "International shipping, duties and returns" page in Shopify for you to review and publish. It never changes prices, shipping zones, market settings or payment methods; it gives you the exact values to set.

## Tips

- Give your broker's duty rates and each market's duty-free line if you can. Under the line, duty may not touch your prices at all; above it, it can move them more than anything else.
- Low-value import rules change often. Confirm each market's current rule before launch.
- Run it again 30 days after a market goes live to see whether conversion there moved.
