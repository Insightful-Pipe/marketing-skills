# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-max-search-reviewer" skill. AI Max has been on in my main Search campaign for a month. Is it worth keeping, and what should I exclude?

## What you need

- **Either** a search terms export for the last 30 days that shows whether a keyword or AI Max matched each term, **or** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with your Google Ads account, so the skill pulls everything itself.
- **Your target CPA or ROAS,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses the CPA your own keywords got in the same campaign and window.
- **Your brand terms,** if they aren't obvious from the account name.
- **The facts your ads may claim:** prices, plans, guarantees, hours, compliance. The skill checks Google's generated headlines against them.
- **The date you turned AI Max on,** for a before and after comparison.

## Example requests

**Review a campaign running AI Max**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-max-search-reviewer" skill. Review AI Max on my Online Booking campaign. My target CPA is $60, and we turned AI Max on four weeks ago.

**Should I turn it on?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-max-search-reviewer" skill. Should I use AI Max in my Clinic Scheduling campaign? Tell me what has to be true first.

**AI Max vs broad match**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-max-search-reviewer" skill. Compare what AI Max broad match and keywordless matching bring next to my own keywords, from this export.

**Moving Dynamic Search Ads across**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-max-search-reviewer" skill. Plan the move of my DSA campaign to AI Max: which pages and terms to keep and what to exclude.

## What you get

1. **A verdict per campaign:** go, go with controls or not yet before launch; win, on par, loss, losing early or too early once it runs, never on fewer than 15 AI Max conversions.
2. **A match source table:** your keywords next to AI Max broad match and keywordless matches, with cost, conversions and CPA.
3. **The terms AI Max added,** sorted into winners to promote, negatives, brand leaks, overlap with your other campaigns, competitor terms to decide on, and a watch list.
4. **The landing pages AI Max chose,** with the ones to exclude and the ones to keep.
5. **Every generated headline that makes a claim,** marked supported, not found or contradicted.
6. **An after-controls estimate** and a **switch-off plan** with the trigger number written in advance.
7. **A DSA move plan,** when you have Dynamic Search Ads.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- add negative keywords taken from your own AI Max search terms
- add winning AI Max terms as exact keywords

AI Max switches, brand exclusions, URL exclusions and generated text are changed by you in Google Ads; the skill writes the steps.

## Tips

- Wait at least 14 days after turning AI Max on before the first review, and rerun it every month.
- Promote the winners before you switch anything off, so the traffic that worked stays.
- A generated line marked "not found" may still be true. Tell the skill the fact and it will mark it supported.
