# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "digital-pr-link-campaign" skill. We ran a customer survey. Plan a digital PR campaign that gets us links from news sites.

## What you need

- **Data you own:** survey results (each question with the count for each answer, how many people answered and when), a spreadsheet export, or your Shopify orders or GA4 data. The skill finds the story in it; with no data it tells you what data would make one.
- **The page the study will live on,** and the pages you want the links to help.
- **Your beat:** the two to four words a writer would use for your topic.
- **Nothing connected is fine.** Paste the data and any outlets you know ran similar stories. With the InsightfulPipe MCP connected, it reads Shopify, GA4 or a Google Sheet itself, finds the outlets through DataForSEO, checks with the crawler whether each outlet linked its sources, and runs the results check from DataForSEO, GA4 and Search Console.

## Example requests

**Campaign plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "digital-pr-link-campaign" skill. Here are our survey results. Find the strongest story, outline the study page, build the media list and write the pitches. We launch on March 3.

**Digital PR campaign ideas from store data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "digital-pr-link-campaign" skill. Look at our Shopify orders for the last two years and tell me which findings could earn links from news sites, and which ones won't hold up.

**Reply to a reporter request**
Hey Claude, I just added the "digital-pr-link-campaign" skill. A journalist posted this request. Should we answer it, and if so, draft the reply from our study.

**Results check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "digital-pr-link-campaign" skill. Our study went live 30 days ago at /research/remote-work-2026. What did it earn: new referring domains, mentions without a link, referral visits and brand searches?

## What you get

1. **Every candidate finding tested** on base size, effect size, freshness, press appetite and privacy, with the lead and the supporting findings picked and the rest held back with the reason.
2. **An outline of the study page:** headline, findings with their bases, charts, a method box with the margin of error, a quote to approve, embed code with a credit link, and internal links to the pages you want to help.
3. **A media list built from past coverage:** outlets that ran the same kind of story in the last 12 months, with syndicated press releases removed, each with the article that earned its place and whether it linked its source.
4. **The pitch kit:** an exclusive offer, a wide pitch per outlet that names the story they ran, one follow-up with something new, and a link request for coverage without a link.
5. **Reporter request replies,** or a plain reason to skip one.
6. **A tracker and a results check** that rates the campaign against your site's own normal month of new referring domains.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can save the tracker to a new tab in a Google Sheet you name. It never sends a pitch or a reply: you send them from your own inbox.

## Tips

- Bring raw counts, not only percentages. The checks need the number of people or orders behind every share.
- Run the results check at 14, 30 and 60 days after launch, and send the link request to every outlet that mentions you without a link.
- A finding that fails a check is still useful on the study page. It just shouldn't carry the pitch.
