# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "internal-linking-planner" skill. Plan the internal links that will help my pages ranking on page two, and check my anchor text.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with Google Search Console added** for your site. The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **The Web Crawler server** to read the links on your pages. It's free.
- **DataForSEO (optional),** used only if your site blocks the crawler. It reads up to 10 pages and bills your DataForSEO account.
- **WordPress (optional),** if you want the skill to add the links for you.

## Example requests

**Link plan from search data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "internal-linking-planner" skill. Which of my pages should link to my posts ranking 4 to 20, and with what anchor?

**Anchor text report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "internal-linking-planner" skill. Check the anchor text of the internal links on my blog posts and tell me which ones to rewrite.

**Click depth**
Hey Claude, I just added the "internal-linking-planner" skill. Crawl my site from the homepage and show me the pages with search traffic that are more than 3 clicks deep.

**Plan, then add the links on WordPress**
Hey Claude, I just added the "internal-linking-planner" skill. Plan internal links for my WordPress posts, then add the first one. Show me the paragraph before you change it.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown.
2. **A scorecard for five areas:** link opportunities, anchor text, link targets, related-posts blocks, and click depth.
3. **A link plan:** which page links to which, with the anchor and the search data behind each one.
4. **Anchor fixes,** each with a better anchor.
5. **A topic-hub note:** the hub page for each topic and the pages missing from it.
6. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

On WordPress, with your yes on each exact change, it can:
- add a planned link to a post or page
- rewrite a weak anchor

It never changes a page you haven't approved. On other sites, it gives you the plan to apply yourself.

## Tips

- Run it monthly, and after you publish a batch of new posts.
- Tell it your money pages, so they get links first.
- If it says the crawler was blocked, allow the crawler in your firewall or bot settings, and run it again for the depth check.
