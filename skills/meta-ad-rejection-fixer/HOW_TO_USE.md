# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-rejection-fixer" skill. My Facebook ad was rejected. Here's the notice and the ad: tell me why, fix it, and draft the review request.

## What you need

- **The notice from Meta,** pasted or as a screenshot: the rejection email, the text Ads Manager shows on the ad, or your Account Quality screen. Meta only gives the reason there, so the skill asks for it even when your account is connected.
- **The ad:** its text, headline, link, and the image or video (upload it or describe it).
- **What you're advertising** and where, plus the ad set targeting if you have it.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with your Meta ad account.** The skill then finds every blocked ad, your rejection history, your account status and your targeting by itself.

## Example requests

**One rejected ad**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-rejection-fixer" skill. Meta rejected my ad for "personal attributes". Here's the copy and the image. Rewrite it so it passes without losing the offer.

**Ad not approved, no idea why**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-rejection-fixer" skill. My Facebook ad was not approved and the email doesn't say much. It promotes our trainer job openings in Dallas. What did it hit?

**Disabled ad account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-rejection-fixer" skill. Our Facebook ad account was disabled yesterday. Here's the Account Quality screen. What happened, what do we fix first, and can you write the appeal?

**Whole account sweep**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-rejection-fixer" skill. Check my connected Meta ad account for rejected, stuck or broken ads and any account restrictions, and fix what you can.

**Special ad category check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-rejection-fixer" skill. We advertise home loans. Do we need a special ad category, and what happens to our targeting if we add one?

## What you get

1. **Your account standing:** active or restricted, in plain words, and how many ads were rejected in the last 30 days.
2. **A table of every blocked ad:** status, since when, spend at stake, and the policy it most likely hit, marked as taken from your notice or inferred.
3. **A fix for each ad:** the exact words or visual that tripped review, the rewritten text (old and new lines), what to change in the image, and any targeting or category fix.
4. **A special ad category check,** listing each targeting setting that a category would remove.
5. **Review request and appeal drafts,** short and factual, with where to send them.
6. **The exact changes it can make for you,** marked "not run".

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- create the replacement creative with the rewritten text
- swap it into the rejected ad, which sends the ad back to review
- or add it as a new paused ad in the same ad set

It never sends a review request or appeal for you, never changes targeting or categories, and never switches an ad on.

## Tips

- Paste Meta's exact wording. "Likely" reasons are educated guesses; the notice is the real one.
- Fix the pattern before resubmitting. Several rejections in a month count against your account.
- Don't open a new ad account to get around a restriction. Meta treats that as evasion.
- No one can promise Meta will approve an ad or restore an account. The skill gives you the strongest honest case.
