# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "content-distribution-planner" skill. We publish a new blog post on Tuesday. Plan how it reaches our readers over the first 30 days, based on what worked for our past posts.

## What you need

- **The piece:** its URL or text, the publish date and what it should drive (sign-ups, demo requests, sales).
- **Your channels:** newsletter and its send day, social accounts, communities someone on your team is active in, partners, places you can republish, and paid platforms.
- **Past results,** one of these:
  - the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with GA4 (plus Klaviyo or Mailchimp, LinkedIn Pages, Facebook Pages, Meta Ads or LinkedIn Ads, and the Social Media Scheduler if you use them), so the skill pulls them itself
  - a GA4 landing page export by source / medium
  - a short table of recent posts with the visits and sign-ups each channel sent
- **A boost cap,** if you're open to paying for reach.

## Example requests

**Full 30-day plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-distribution-planner" skill. Our deposits study goes live on Tuesday. Plan its first 30 days across our newsletter, LinkedIn, Facebook, the forum we post in and our payments partner. Boost cap $400.

**How to promote a blog post, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-distribution-planner" skill. Here's my post and a table of how my last six posts did on each channel. Tell me where to share this one, in what order, and what to say in each place.

**Is it worth a boost?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-distribution-planner" skill. Check our GA4 and ad accounts and tell me whether this guide needs a paid boost to reach 300 engaged visits in a month, and on which platform.

**Plan, then book the posts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-distribution-planner" skill. Plan the promotion for this case study, then put the LinkedIn and Facebook slots in the scheduler as drafts. Show me the list first.

**Day 7 check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-distribution-planner" skill. It's day 7 for the post we planned last week. Compare each channel with what you expected and tell me which slots to keep or cancel.

## What you get

1. **The piece in a nutshell:** who it's for, the points each slot can lead with, the businesses it names and the pages that should link to it.
2. **A channel ranking** from your own past pieces: engaged visits and key events per piece, one value score, and how strong the evidence is (proven, thin or untried).
3. **The sizing:** expected visits from your newsletter and each account, and a boost budget worked out from your own cost per engaged visit, with a day 3 gate and a stop rule.
4. **A dated 30-day calendar,** with a short brief and a tagged link for every slot, and clashes with your existing queue moved.
5. **Day 7 and day 30 checks,** with the numbers to beat and what to do when a channel falls short or overshoots.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact list, it can book the LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Dev.to, Hashnode, Slack and Discord slots in the InsightfulPipe Social Media Scheduler, as drafts unless you say otherwise. It never sends your newsletter, posts in communities, emails partners or starts a paid boost. Those stay with you.

## Tips

- Tag every link the way your past ones were tagged, so the day 7 check compares like with like. The skill does this for you.
- A channel with no past data gets one test slot, not a big push. Run the day 30 check and the next plan will know more.
- Run it again on day 7 and day 30 with the same piece.
