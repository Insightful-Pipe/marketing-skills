# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-gmv-max-planner" skill. Set an ROI target for each of my TikTok Shop products from its margin, tell me how to split my GMV Max campaigns and budget, and review last week.

## What you need

- **Your margins per product:** price, unit cost, the shipping you pay, your platform fee rate, the affiliate commission rate and the refund rate. A pasted table is fine.
- **Your Seller Center product export** for the last 30 days, and for the 28 days before your products joined GMV Max if they already run there.
- **Your GMV Max results,** either from the InsightfulPipe MCP with your TikTok Ads account connected (the skill reads stores, campaigns, settings and the GMV Max report itself), or as a GMV Max report export from TikTok Ads Manager by product, video and LIVE session.
- **Your profit goal** (the share of revenue to keep after ads; 10% if you don't say) and **a daily cap** across all GMV Max campaigns.

## Example requests

**Launch plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-gmv-max-planner" skill. We're starting TikTok Shop ads. Here are our 12 products with costs and fees. Which ones go into GMV Max, at what ROI target and budget?

**Weekly review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-gmv-max-planner" skill. Review last week's GMV Max results by product, video and LIVE session, and tell me what to scale, hold, fix or pull.

**Why are we losing money**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-gmv-max-planner" skill. GMV Max shows ROI 5 but our TikTok Shop profit is flat. Find out which products and sessions lose money.

**Plan, then change it**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-gmv-max-planner" skill. Split my GMV Max campaign by margin, then create the new campaign and change the budgets. Show me the payloads first.

## What you get

1. **A margin table:** each product's break-even ROI and target ROI, the products ads can't make profitable, and the products whose break-even sits above your current campaign's target.
2. **A campaign plan:** products grouped by target (the ROI target is set per campaign), each group's target and starting budget, inside your cap.
3. **The ramp:** when to raise budgets or lower targets, checked in a fixed order with break-even first (no new money while a campaign holds a product it can't pay for), and the first review date.
4. **A weekly read by product:** ROI against break-even and target, a lift check against sales before GMV Max, and a verdict: scale, hold, fix, pull, check lift or too few orders.
5. **Videos and LIVE:** products with too few videos, videos that carry a product, videos that spend with no orders, and LIVE sessions by length against your blended break-even.
6. **Top actions** with the money at stake, and the drafted changes.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- create a new GMV Max campaign for a product group (switched off, so you finish it and turn it on in TikTok Ads Manager)
- change a GMV Max campaign's daily budget

ROI targets, products, videos and LIVE schedules are changed by you in TikTok Ads Manager and Seller Center.

## Tips

- Run it weekly, on Monday, so the review covers a full week.
- Change each campaign at most once every 3 full days; GMV Max re-learns after every change.
- Keep your margin table up to date: a new supplier price or fee changes every target.
