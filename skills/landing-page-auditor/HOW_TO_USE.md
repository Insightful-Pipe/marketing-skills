# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Audit my top landing page and tell me why it isn't converting.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with Google Analytics 4 and DataForSEO added. Search Console, Google Ads, Screenshot and PageSpeed Insights make the audit fuller. The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **The page,** if you have one in mind. Without one, the skill picks your landing page with the most sessions.
- **The one action the page is for** (sign up, buy, book a demo, call). Without it, the skill uses the first button in the hero.

No connections? Share the URL, a desktop and a mobile screenshot, and your numbers, and the skill grades what it can see.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Audit example.com/demo for the last 28 days. It's a paid landing page and the goal is demo bookings.

**Message match with the ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Check whether the page my Google Ads send people to matches the ads, and what Google rates its landing page experience.

**Message match for a Facebook ad or an email**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Here's the Facebook ad that sends people to example.com/offer: [paste the headline, text and CTA]. Does the page deliver what the ad promises?

**Hero and CTA only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Just review the first screen and the main button of my homepage. Is it clear what we do and what to click?

**Sales page review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Review our long-form sales page for the course: structure, proof, objections and the offer.

**Website content review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Review the content of our five busiest landing pages and tell me which one to fix first.

**Mobile rendering check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Check how our key pages render on a phone: first screen, tap targets, text size and speed.

**Structure for a new page**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. We're launching a webinar page next month. Give me the section order and what each section should say.

**Compare with a competitor**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. Compare my pricing page with competitor.com/pricing and tell me what they do better.

**Form friction**
Hey Claude, I just added the "landing-page-auditor" skill. My lead form has 8 fields. Which ones should go?

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with every deduction shown.
2. **A measurement check:** whether the page's conversion is counted at all, and which proxies stand in if it isn't.
3. **A scorecard for seven pillars:** clarity, relevance, value, trust, urgency, friction, and design and mobile. Each one shows the evidence behind it.
4. **The top 5 fixes,** ranked by impact, confidence and ease, split into quick wins and bigger bets.
5. **Rewrites** for the headline, subheadline and main button, built from the words your visitors search and click on.
6. **A page structure** for the page type, set against the current one.
7. **Test ideas,** sized against your traffic, so you know whether an A/B test can work at all.
8. **A roadmap** (this week, this month, this quarter), and a list of what couldn't be checked and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can mark your page's conversion event as a GA4 key event, once the event is firing. It doesn't edit your page, your tags or your ads. It gives you the copy, the structure and the tracking spec to hand over.

## Tips

- Say what traffic the page gets. A paid landing page and a homepage are judged differently.
- If your GA4 tag changed recently, the skill uses only the days after the change and labels the rates directional.
- Run it again four weeks after the changes, on the same proxies, to see what moved.
