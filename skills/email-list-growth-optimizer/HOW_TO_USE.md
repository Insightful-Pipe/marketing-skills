# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "email-list-growth-optimizer" skill. Our email sign-ups feel flat. Check our popups and sign-up forms and give me a 90-day plan to grow the list.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Paste or screenshot each form and its settings (type, device, trigger, pages, fields, offer, consent text, the list it feeds), plus a form performance export and your sessions by page and device.
- **Or the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** with Klaviyo or Mailchimp, GA4 and, for a store, Shopify. The skill then pulls form results, list growth, welcome flows, sessions and checkout consent itself. You still share the form settings, because no report holds them.
- **The lift worth testing for,** if you have one. Without it, the skill sizes tests for a 20% lift in submit rate.

## Example requests

**Grow the list**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-list-growth-optimizer" skill. How do we grow our email list? Read our Klaviyo forms, GA4 and Shopify for the last 30 days and give me the plan.

**Is this popup working?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-list-growth-optimizer" skill. Here are screenshots of our welcome popup on desktop and mobile and its numbers. Is it any good, and what should we test?

**Mailchimp audience**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-list-growth-optimizer" skill. Our Mailchimp audience grew last quarter, but I think it was one import. Show me real growth per visitor and where sign-ups come from.

**Signup form ideas**
Hey Claude, I just added the "email-list-growth-optimizer" skill. Write three new signup form variants for our blog and product pages, ready to paste into Klaviyo.

## What you get

1. **Growth over 90 days** in three 30-day blocks: added, removed, net, and sign-ups per 1,000 sessions with imports kept out.
2. **Sources:** how many sign-ups each form, checkout opt-in and everything else brought in.
3. **A scorecard for every form version:** reach, submit rate, close rate, sign-ups per 1,000 sessions on its pages, and grades for offer, trigger and timing, fields, mobile behaviour, consent wording and welcome path.
4. **Pages without a real chance to sign up,** by page group and device.
5. **Top fixes,** compliance and welcome path first, then the forms missing the most sign-ups.
6. **Paste-ready variants:** headline, body, button, decline, consent block, trigger and targeting.
7. **A 90-day plan** with every test sized to the form's own traffic, and the tests that would take too long swapped for a bigger change.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can set a welcome flow you have reviewed live in Klaviyo. It never edits forms, consent text or checkout settings; it writes those for you to paste.

## Tips

- Share both the desktop and mobile settings of a form; they are graded separately.
- Tell the skill about any list you imported. Imports are kept out of the growth rate, and their consent is checked.
- The consent check reads what is on the form. It isn't legal advice; confirm SMS and EU or UK wording with your counsel.
- Run it again 30 days after the fixes to see sign-ups per 1,000 sessions move.
