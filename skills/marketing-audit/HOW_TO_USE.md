# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-audit" skill. Audit our whole marketing and tell me the ten fixes worth the most.

## What you need

- **Nothing to start.** With no connection, the skill asks six short questions and works from what you paste: exports, numbers from your dashboards, a strategy document.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** Each source you connect turns its areas from "not measured" into measured, and the skill pulls the numbers itself. GA4 and Search Console matter most; ad platforms, Klaviyo or Mailchimp, social pages, Google Business Profile and PageSpeed fill in the rest.
- **Your answers to six questions:** your main customer, why customers pick you, what you sell and at what price, your main competitors, the one result that matters and its target cost, and your brand terms.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-audit" skill. Run a full marketing audit on our business for the last 28 days. Our target cost per sign-up is $60.

**Check a plan against the data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-audit" skill. Here is our Q4 marketing strategy. Audit our marketing, then tell me which parts of this plan the data supports, which it contradicts and what the plan leaves out.

**No connection yet**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-audit" skill. I'll paste our GA4 channel report, our Search Console queries and our ad spend. Give me a marketing scorecard from that.

**Rank the fixes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-audit" skill. Audit our marketing and rank the ten fixes worth the most, so I can hand them to marketing-plan-builder for a dated plan.

## What you get

1. **A score out of 100,** with the math shown and the share of areas it could measure.
2. **A scorecard for ten areas:** positioning and customer, offer and pricing, measurement, channel mix, website conversion, search and content, paid media, email and retention, social, and brand and reputation. Each has a result, a key number and the evidence.
3. **The ten fixes worth the most,** tracking problems first, then the ones with a monthly figure, each with how the figure was worked out.
4. **A plan check,** when you paste a strategy or plan: each claim marked supported, contradicted or untested, plus what the plan misses.
5. **A list of what it couldn't measure,** and what would measure it.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- mark an event that is a real result (for example a demo request) as a GA4 key event
- save the scorecard and fixes to a new tab in a Google Sheet

It never changes budgets, bids, campaigns, emails or posts.

## Tips

- Connect GA4 first. Most areas lean on it, and the measurement area tells you whether the rest can be trusted.
- Answer the six questions honestly. "We don't have one" is a useful answer.
- Run it each quarter, and again after the fixes, to see the score and coverage move.
- When the audit is done, say "turn these fixes into a plan" to hand them to the marketing plan builder.
