# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Build our brand positioning against our three main competitors and tell me what only we can say.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with DataForSEO, Crawler and your Google Search Console property added. Without Search Console, the skill reads the keywords your site ranks for from DataForSEO instead (estimates, US only). Notion is needed only if you want the result saved there.
- **Your domain and one to four competitor domains.** Pick the ones buyers really compare you with.
- **Optional:** founder facts (how it started, the first customer), customer quotes you're allowed to use, and your brand terms if they aren't obvious from your name.
- **For the elevator pitch and the business fact brief, no connection is needed:** paste your positioning, notes or last brief. Search Console, GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Business Profile, Google Play, App Store and LinkedIn Pages each replace a guess with a measured line when connected.

## Example requests

**Positioning statement and differentiation**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Read our site and these three competitors, show me what's table stakes and what's ours alone, and write our positioning statement.

**Personal monopoly**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Find the one position no competitor can claim, and prove it against their pages.

**Brand story and founder story**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Write our brand story and founder story. Here's how the company started: ...

**Mission, vision and a 5-year brand strategy**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Draft mission and vision options and a 5-year brand strategy with baselines from our real search data.

**Elevator pitch and startup one-liner**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Write our elevator pitch: a one-liner, 30-second versions for a buyer, an investor and a partner, a two-minute version, a short company description and my founder introduction. Use the words our customers search and write in reviews.

**Business fact brief (brand brief and company fact sheet)**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-positioning-builder" skill. Build our business fact brief from our site, Search Console, GA4, Google Ads, Meta and reviews, ask me only about the gaps, and next month show what changed since this version.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:**
   - every site's claims grouped into themes, quoted from its own page
   - your proof points, each marked yours alone or shared
   - the words buyers search to find you, and which ones your home page never uses
   - demand and search results for candidate category names
   - each site's colours and fonts
2. **A positioning canvas and statement,** with every competitor described only in its own words.
3. **A differentiation strategy:** what to own, what to match quietly, what to answer and what to avoid.
4. **A personal monopoly line,** checked against every competitor page.
5. **Stories:**
   - positioning stories for each best-fit segment
   - the brand story and brand world
   - a founder story from your facts, or the questions to answer if you gave none
6. **Mission and vision options.**
7. **A brand strategy:** pillars and a 5-year horizon plan, each metric with its baseline measured in this run.
8. **In elevator pitch mode:** a one-liner with two alternates, a 30-second pitch per audience, a two-minute pitch, a 50-word company description and a founder introduction. Each line shows its word count and speaking time, the buyer words it uses and the source of every claim, plus a five-second clarity test to run with five real people.
9. **In business fact brief mode:** one dated file with your offers, buyers and their problems, the alternatives they weigh, proof, claims you may and may not make, buyer words, key numbers, past results and which account is which. Every line is tagged measured, from you or assumed, the gaps become a short interview, and a refresh lists each changed line with its old and new value.

## What it can change for you

With your yes, it can save the brand book, the pitches or the fact brief as a new page in Notion. It never edits your website or any ad account.

## Tips

- A run uses 8 to 10 DataForSEO calls with three competitors: one more with four, and one more without Search Console.
- Prices and trial terms are never quoted in public lines (statements, stories, pitches), yours or theirs. The private fact brief records your own plan prices as your page states them, dated.
- The elevator pitch never invents traction: give it the numbers you're happy to say out loud, or it leaves a marked slot.
- Re-run it before you publish any line that says "the only": competitor pages change.
