# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. Find our USP: what can we say that the businesses around us can't, and that people actually respond to?

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill reads your ad lines, their results and your search terms itself; you don't export anything.
- **DataForSEO in the same workspace,** to read your pages and your competitors' pages and run the market search (about 5 to 8 paid calls per run).
- **Optional:** Google Search Console (the words buyers use to find you) and Google Business Profile (review text).
- **Your competitors,** if you know who you lose deals to. Without them, the skill takes the first 3 businesses in the search for your main service.
- **For message testing alone, nothing connected:** paste the lines to test and what you know about your reach (list size, ad clicks, page traffic), then paste the answers or an export when the survey closes.
- **Optional: reviews of your competitors,** especially complaints. The skill can't fetch them itself; paste them and it counts what their customers complain about by theme, so the "unlike" lines can answer it. Without them, competitor weaknesses are left out rather than guessed.

## Example requests

**Find the USP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. We sell project management software for marketing teams. What's our USP against the tools buyers compare us with?

**Against named competitors**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. I know we're better than these two competitors but I can't say why in one line. Here are their sites. Find our angle and show me their own words next to ours.

**Turn features into a USP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. Look at our landing page and our ads. Which of the things we already say could become a USP, and which ones does everyone say?

**Our ads sound like everyone else's**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. Our tagline and headlines feel generic. Check which selling points our Google Ads audience responds to and rewrite the messaging around the strongest one.

**Pick a niche**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. We serve lots of client types. Use our campaigns and conversions to show which buyer we serve best and what USP we could own there.

**A small player against a big one**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. We compete with a much bigger brand. What can a smaller business like ours credibly claim that they can't?

**Find it, then test it**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. Find our USP, then set up the test: show me the callouts and the challenger ad before you add anything.

**Message testing before a rewrite**
Hey Claude, I just added the "usp-extractor" skill. Here are four value propositions for our homepage hero. Plan a message test: pick the cheapest way to tell which one works, tell me how many answers or how much budget it needs, write the survey, and read out the winner when I paste the answers.

## What you get

1. **A USP score out of 100** and the recommended USP, with how sure the skill is and why.
2. **The intersection:** what you do well, what buyers care about (with numbers), and what competitors don't say.
3. **The evidence:** which selling points buyers respond to in your ads, what they search for, what your customers mention, and a table of every theme showing whether it's yours alone, contested or table stakes, with each competitor's own words.
4. **Proof points,** and the ones told differently in different places.
5. **3 to 5 candidates** scored on credible, relevant, unique, sustainable and memorable, with the reason behind every score.
6. **The primary USP and two alternatives,** each written four ways ("only", for-who-that, "unlike", what-how-why).
7. **Messaging:** landing page hero, Google Ads headlines, descriptions and callouts with character counts, a Meta ad, an elevator pitch and taglines.
8. **"Unlike" lines** for each competitor and against doing nothing.
9. **Claims to avoid,** with a replacement for each.
10. **A test plan** and a validation checklist.

In message testing mode you get the method chosen (five-second test, preference or single-variant survey, ad or page split) with the answers, days or spend each one would need, the survey script and invitation, and a readout with a confidence interval for every message and a verdict: decided, leaning, not decided or too few answers. It needs no connection; with Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, GA4, Klaviyo, Mailchimp or Google Sheets connected, it reads the CTR, CPC, traffic, segment size or answers itself.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- add the USP callouts to a campaign
- create a challenger responsive search ad with the USP lines, always paused, so you choose when it runs
- for a message test that runs as a live ad split, create one paused test ad per message in Google Ads, Meta Ads or LinkedIn Ads

It never changes budgets, bids, live ads or your website, and never sends a survey or an email.

## Tips

- Tell it who you really lose deals to. Competitors who rank in search are a good default, not always the right set.
- Share proof you're allowed to use (customer counts, ratings, guarantees). The skill never invents a number.
- If you sell several things, run it once per offer. A USP is for one buyer.
- In a regulated field (law, health, finance), have someone check the final claims against your advertising rules. The skill flags the risky ones.
- Run the challenger for at least 3 weeks, then ask the skill to read the result.
