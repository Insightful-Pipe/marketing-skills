# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-seo-auditor" skill. Audit my online store's SEO and rank the fixes by the revenue at stake.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is fine.** Paste or upload a product export from your store and a Search Console Pages export for the last 28 days. Add a GA4 landing page report for Organic Search with revenue if you want the fixes ranked by money.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with your store (Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento), Search Console and GA4 added. The skill then reads the catalogue, your search data and your organic revenue itself. Merchant Center and the web crawler make it fuller.
- **Your answers to two questions,** if you know them: which filter pages you want to rank, and which retired products have a replacement.

## Example requests

**Shopify SEO audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-seo-auditor" skill. Run a Shopify SEO audit on my store and tell me what to fix first.

**Faceted navigation**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-seo-auditor" skill. Google is showing my filter URLs. Check my faceted navigation SEO and tell me which filters deserve their own page.

**From exports, nothing connected**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-seo-auditor" skill. Here are my WooCommerce product export and my Search Console pages export. Audit the store's SEO.

**Discontinued products**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-seo-auditor" skill. We retired a lot of products this season. Find the old product URLs that still get search traffic and draft the redirects.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for eight areas:** store map and crawl rules, duplicate product paths and variants, filters, sorting and pagination, internal search pages, discontinued and out-of-stock products, category pages, product and merchant listing markup, and product images.
2. **The fixes, ranked:** failures first, then warnings, each ordered by the organic revenue that runs through the URLs it touches, with the numbers behind it.
3. **Drafts to review:** category intros written only from catalogue facts about the products in stock (count, names, prices and options), and a redirect list (a CSV ready for Shopify's URL redirect import).
4. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can add the drafted intro to a Shopify collection or a WooCommerce product category. It never creates redirects, edits your theme or changes robots.txt; those come as instructions and files for you to apply.

## Tips

- Run it after a season change, a big catalogue cleanup or a theme switch.
- If the crawler can't read your store's pages (many stores block bots), the skill says so and asks for the page source instead of guessing.
- Run it again a month after the fixes and compare the duplicate share and the search pages left in Google.
