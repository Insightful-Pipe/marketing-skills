# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-answer-source-placement" skill. Find the pages AI answers cite for my category that name my competitors but not us, and plan how to get on them.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Paste answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot or a Google AI Overview with their links, or a list of URLs, and the skill opens and checks each page.
- **Or the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with DataForSEO added. The skill then finds the cited pages itself: the pages AI answers cite for your category, AI Overview references, the organic top 10, ChatGPT's sources, and each site's domain rank and spam score. The crawler reads each page's links, and Search Console (optional) supplies the buying questions you already show up for.
- **Your brand, domain, category and 2 to 5 rivals.**
- **What you can offer an editor:** a demo login, data you can share, a customer who will be quoted. Pitches only promise what's on this list.
- **Whether paid placements are allowed.** If you don't say, they aren't.

## Example requests

**LLM seeding plan (connected)**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-answer-source-placement" skill. We sell salon booking software. Our rivals are Competitor A, B and C. Find the pages ChatGPT and AI Overviews cite for our buying questions and tell me which ones we can realistically get on.

**From pasted answers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-answer-source-placement" skill. Here are five ChatGPT and Perplexity answers to "best salon booking app" with their sources. Check every cited page for us and our rivals and give me a placement plan.

**How to get recommended by ChatGPT**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-answer-source-placement" skill. ChatGPT recommends our competitors and never us. Which sources is it using, and which of them can we get into?

**Fix what the answers say about us**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ai-answer-source-placement" skill. Check the cited pages that already list us and tell me which have our pricing or features wrong, with the corrections to send.

## What you get

1. **The buying questions used,** and the ones left out with the reason.
2. **Which answers name you** and which don't.
3. **Where the answers lean:** the most-cited sites, and the page types that carry the most citations and the most gaps.
4. **A target table:** every outside page, who it names, its way in, how fresh it is, domain rank, spam score, how often it was cited, and its tier (Act now, Pitch, Long shot or Skip) with the rule that set it.
5. **A plan by tier,** with one next step per page.
6. **Drafts:** pitches to list editors and creators, and corrections for listings with your old facts.
7. **A paid lane** (only if you allow it) and a **refused list** for tactics that break platform or search rules.
8. **A rerun date** and what to measure then.

## What it changes for you

Nothing. It sends no email, posts nothing and edits no listing. Every draft is yours to send.

## Tips

- Use the words a buyer types for your category, not your product's name for it.
- Rerun it after 30 days with the same questions. The measure is how many gap pages now name you.
- A small blog that the answers cite beats a famous site they ignore. Trust the hits column over the domain rank.
