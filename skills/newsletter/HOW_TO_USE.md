# How to use: Newsletter

Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Write this month's newsletter from what we published last month, and tell me why you picked each post.

## What it needs

The InsightfulPipe MCP. It works with whatever is connected and says what it couldn't check:
- **GA4 and Search Console** to rank your posts by what people read and searched for.
- **Klaviyo or Mailchimp** to learn from past issues (clicks, subject styles, unsubscribes, send slot) and to save the finished issue as a draft.
- **No analytics connected?** With DataForSEO added, it works on any public blog: it reads the posts and ranks them on public search estimates (DataForSEO bills per call; a run uses up to about 12 calls). Or paste your post list and any numbers you have.

It asks only for what the data can't tell it: the newsletter's name, who reads it, what a reader should do next, and how often you send. If you don't know, it uses sensible defaults and says so.

## Example prompts

**This month's issue**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Write October's issue from our September blog posts. Our readers are small business owners, and the goal is clicks to the blog.

**Pick a format**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. We're starting a weekly newsletter. Look at what we publish and what gets read, and recommend a format we can keep up.

**Ideas**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Give me 10 newsletter ideas backed by what our readers search for and click.

**Review a sent issue**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Review last week's newsletter in Klaviyo: what worked, what didn't, and three changes for the next one.

**Grow the list**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Plan cross-promotion for our newsletter: where on our site to ask for signups, and which newsletters in our niche we could swap mentions with.

**Email course**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Turn our posts on one topic into a 5-day email course.

**Start and monetize a newsletter**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter" skill. Help me start a newsletter for our blog readers: the promise, format, cadence and platform, and when we could sell sponsor slots. Price a rate card from our last issues' clicks in Mailchimp and write the media kit.

## What you get

1. **A ranking of the month's posts:** each with its score, its role in the issue (lead, supporting, fresh pick, quick link, course, left out) and the reason.
2. **The issue:** three subject lines in different styles, preview text, the body in one of nine formats (curator, teacher, storyteller, roundup, analyst, behind the scenes, interview, contrarian, product update), tagged links, the images and fields still needed, send timing and one A/B test.
3. **Checks:** subject and preview length, facts traced to your posts, no invented numbers, link count and tagging, length, voice and mobile.
4. **Extras when you ask:** an email course outline, issue ideas with the evidence behind each, a review of a sent issue, a cross-promotion plan, or a launch and sponsorship plan: the topic angle from search demand, a cadence you can keep, a sponsor rate card priced from your own delivered and unique clicks, a media kit, and the audience a sponsor slot, a paid tier or affiliate links each need.
5. **What it couldn't check,** and why.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the final copy and the list, it can save the issue as a **draft** campaign in Klaviyo or Mailchimp. It never sends, schedules or sends a test; you do that in your email platform.

## Tips

- Keep one format across issues; readers learn the shape.
- Give it two past issues you like. Voice matches much better.
- Judge issues on clicks and replies, not opens.
- Run it the first working day of each month, so the window is a full month.
