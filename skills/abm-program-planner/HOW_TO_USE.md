# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "abm-program-planner" skill. Plan an ABM program for next quarter from our target account list and our closed deals.

## What you need

- **Your target account list:** company, domain, industry, employees, region, and the LinkedIn company page link if you have it.
- **A CRM export of closed opportunities** from the last 12 to 24 months, won and lost. Add the titles of the people in each deal and the activity before each opportunity (meetings, replies, events, page visits, ad impressions) if your CRM has them: they let the skill learn your buying committee and test the engagement score.
- **Your open pipeline** on the target accounts.
- **The basics:** average first-year contract value, sales cycle, how many AEs and SDRs will work the list, and the monthly budget.
- **Two LinkedIn numbers per tier,** once the skill drafts the targeting and you set it up in Campaign Manager: the forecast audience size, and the suggested CPM bid it shows (the bid comes live instead if LinkedIn Ads is connected).
- **Optional connections through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** LinkedIn Ads (which target companies already see your ads, and live suggested bids), Google Ads (whether Customer Match can run) and GA4 (visits to your account pages). Without them, the skill works from what you paste.

## Example requests

**A full ABM plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "abm-program-planner" skill. Here are our 60 target accounts and two years of closed deals. We have 3 AEs, 2 SDRs and $5k a month. Build the ABM plan.

**Which accounts to target**
Hey Claude, I just added the "abm-program-planner" skill. Score this account list against the deals we actually won and tell me which accounts belong in tier 1.

**Pricing the LinkedIn part**
Hey Claude, I just added the "abm-program-planner" skill. Price LinkedIn for each ABM tier. Here are the audience forecasts and suggested CPM bids from Campaign Manager.

**Expansion**
Hey Claude, I just added the "abm-program-planner" skill. Plan an expansion program for our existing customers. Here is the customer export with renewal dates and products owned.

## What you get

1. **A go or no-go** on ABM from your own numbers.
2. **A scored, tiered account list** (one-to-one, one-to-few, one-to-many) with the weights shown, and the accounts left out with reasons.
3. **The buying committee per tier,** from the roles in the deals you won.
4. **A 90-day play sequence per tier:** ads, outbound, direct mail, events, personalised pages and sales touches.
5. **A budget per tier,** capped by what a won account is worth, with LinkedIn priced from your audience forecasts and suggested CPM bids (live bids when connected).
6. **An engagement score and sales trigger,** tested on your past deals.
7. **Quarter goals and a comparison group,** so you can tell whether the program worked.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can create a Google Ads Customer Match list and upload your consented contacts at tier 1 and 2 accounts. It never builds LinkedIn campaigns or changes your CRM; it hands those steps to you or to the LinkedIn skills.

## Tips

- Give LinkedIn company page links with the number in them (`linkedin.com/company/12345`): LinkedIn reports companies by that number, not by name.
- Paste the Campaign Manager audience forecast for each tier's targeting, and the suggested CPM bid if LinkedIn Ads isn't connected. The skill never guesses an audience size or a price: without them, the LinkedIn line says "forecast needed" or "CPM needed".
- Rerun it each quarter with the new closed deals: the fit scores and the engagement weights learn from them.
