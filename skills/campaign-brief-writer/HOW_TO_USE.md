# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "campaign-brief-writer" skill. Turn the request below into a one-page marketing brief for our agency, with baselines from our past campaigns.

## What you need

- **The request:** paste the email, the meeting notes or a few lines on what's wanted.
- **Who's involved:** who asked, who owns the brief, who approves it, and the team or agency it goes to.
- **The result that counts and the number wanted,** for example 250 paid sign-ups.
- **The budget,** split into media and agency or production costs.
- **Dates:** launch, end, when the brief is due, and any company days off.
- **Facts you can stand behind:** product claims, customer results with their source, the offer and its terms, legal lines and brand rules. The message, proof and offer use only these.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads and Klaviyo for past campaign results, GA4 for your landing page and key events, and Google Sheets for a campaign log. Without them, paste your past campaigns as a table.

## Example requests

**Write a brief from an email**
Hey Claude, I just added the "campaign-brief-writer" skill. Here's the email from our Head of Marketing about a New Year promo. Write the brief for the agency, fill the baselines from Google Ads, Meta and Klaviyo, and tell me if the target fits the budget.

**Brief a freelancer on a webinar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "campaign-brief-writer" skill. We're running a webinar for clinic managers on 12 March. Write a brief I can send to our freelance designer and email writer.

**Check someone else's brief**
Hey Claude, I just added the "campaign-brief-writer" skill. Score this brief from the sales team, tell me what's missing before the agency starts, and write the questions to send back.

**Make an intake form**
Hey Claude, I just added the "campaign-brief-writer" skill. Turn your brief format into a request form for our marketing team and add it as a new tab in our planning sheet.

## What you get

1. **A one-page brief:** business goal, one measurable objective, audience, single message and proof, offer, mandatories and no-gos, budget, deliverables, KPIs with baselines and targets, tracking, dated milestones with owners, and sign-off.
2. **Baselines from your own campaigns:** the comparable past campaigns, the ones left out and why, and the pooled cost per result, email conversion and landing page conversion rate.
3. **A budget check:** how many results the budget can reach, whether the requested target fits, and if not, the budget it needs and the choice for the approver.
4. **A tracking check:** whether the conversions your KPIs rely on exist and are counted in GA4, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and Klaviyo, whether they still fire in GA4 and Meta, and what to fix before launch.
5. **For a brief someone else wrote:** a score out of 100 across 13 parts, the blockers, a verdict, and one question per gap.
6. **An intake form** with the same parts, ready to paste or add to Google Sheets.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can add the intake form or the brief as a new tab in a Google Sheets spreadsheet you name. It never edits an existing tab, and it never changes an ad account, GA4 property, tag or email list.

## Tips

- Paste the request as it was written. The skill keeps the requester's number visible even when it sets a lower reachable target.
- If you count more than one conversion in Google Ads, say which one the campaign is judged on.
- Run the check mode on every brief that arrives from outside the marketing team before work starts.
