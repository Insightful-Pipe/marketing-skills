# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "event-sponsorship-roi" skill. Here is the prospectus for a trade show we've been offered. Should we buy it, and at what price?

## What you need

- **The package:** the prospectus or the organiser's email, with the fee, expected attendance, who attends, and what is included.
- **Your other costs:** booth or display, printing, travel, prizes, and staff hours with a rough hourly cost.
- **Your own funnel for the last 12 months:** a CRM export of leads (source, created date, qualified or not, won, lost or open, won date and amount), spend by channel, and your gross margin. If you have no CRM, give the counts by hand.
- **For a new event:** your low, expected and high guess for how many of the people there will become qualified leads. The skill never invents this rate; without it, it shows the rate you would need to break even.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** GA4 for UTM and QR traffic, CallRail for calls on the event number, Shopify or WooCommerce for promo-code orders, BigQuery or Google Sheets for your CRM data. Without them, paste the exports.

## Example requests

**Should we sponsor?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-sponsorship-roi" skill. A regional conference wants $12,000 for a booth and a panel slot. Here's the prospectus and our CRM export. Is it worth it, and what should we push back on?

**Compare options**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-sponsorship-roi" skill. Compare these three sponsorships for next year (a trade show, a local sports team and a charity gala) and tell me which to buy with a $15,000 budget.

**Plan the activation**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-sponsorship-roi" skill. We signed the home show. Give me sponsorship activation ideas and set up the tracking so we can measure it after.

**Measure an event**
Hey Claude, I just added the "event-sponsorship-roi" skill. How do we measure sponsorship ROI for the show we did in March? Pull the calls from the show number in CallRail, the SHOW26 orders and the QR traffic in GA4, and tell me whether to renew.

## What you get

1. **Your own numbers:** lead to qualified rate, qualified to won rate, gross profit per deal and per qualified lead, and what you already pay for a qualified lead through paid channels.
2. **For each package:** total cost, reachable buyers, expected qualified leads (low, expected, high), cost per qualified lead against your benchmark, ROI, break-even and a walk-away fee, with a Buy, Negotiate or Pass verdict and a list of what to ask the organiser for.
3. **A side-by-side table** of every option, ranked, fitted to your budget.
4. **An activation plan** for the one you pick: a tracking kit (UTM links, QR codes, a tracking number, a promo code, a CRM source value), plus before, on site, content and follow-up.
5. **After the event:** leads (including those your CRM logged under other sources), qualified leads, won revenue, store orders, cost per outcome, measured ROI, and a renew, renew at a lower fee, or drop call.

## What it changes

Nothing. It only reads. You set up the links, numbers and codes it writes out.

## Tips

- Give it last year's results for a repeat event: it then forecasts from what that event really produced.
- Set up the tracking kit before anything is printed. An event with no tracking number, code or UTM links can only be measured from the CRM.
- Run the measurement after the capture window closes (30 days after the event), and again once enough time has passed for your typical deal to close.
