# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-campaign-planner" skill. Plan a Google Ads campaign on my competitors' brand names and tell me which rivals are worth bidding on.

## What you need

- **Your rivals,** two to six, each with its website and one true thing you do better than it, with where that can be checked. A rival you can't beat on anything gets skipped.
- **A daily budget** for this campaign, and the CPA you'll accept from these searches (your target CPA if you don't know).
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** with your Google Ads account, and DataForSEO for the search results check. Then the skill pulls Keyword Planner volumes and bids, your own search terms on each rival's name and who already advertises there by itself.
- **No connection?** Paste a Keyword Planner export for the rival names and a search terms export, or just give the list of rivals. The skill marks anything it can't see as unknown.

## Example requests

**Plan the campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-campaign-planner" skill. Plan a competitor campaign for my Google Ads account against Competitor A and Competitor B, $40 a day, and we'll accept a $150 CPA from these clicks.

**Is it allowed?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-campaign-planner" skill. Can I bid on competitor brand names in Google Ads, and can I put their names in my ads?

**Which rivals are worth it**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-campaign-planner" skill. Here are five competitors and what we do better than each. Rate which ones are worth bidding on before we spend anything.

**From a list alone**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-keyword-campaign-planner" skill. I have no account connected. These are our three biggest rivals and our offer: draft the competitor campaign, the negatives, the ads and the comparison page brief.

## What you get

1. **The rule, stated straight:** bidding on a rival's name is allowed in Google Ads; using it in ad text can be restricted after a complaint.
2. **A go, test or skip rating for each rival,** with its searches, expected CPC, the CPC you can afford, who already advertises on its name and how often its searches converted in your account.
3. **The campaign, drafted paused:** one ad group per rival and intent, phrase and exact keywords, and negatives that keep out the rival's login, support and job searches.
4. **Comparison ads without rival names,** every line with its character count and the fact it rests on, plus a named version only if you ask.
5. **A comparison landing page brief** for each rival you go after.
6. **A budget forecast** with every assumption shown, honest Quality Score, CPC and conversion rate expectations, and the review gates for day 14 and day 30.
7. **The exact create payloads,** marked not run.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact plan, it can create the campaign, ad groups, keywords, negatives and ads, all paused. Turning them on is a second, separate yes. It never changes your live campaigns as part of the plan.

## Tips

- Give it the facts behind each angle, with the date you checked the rival's page. Comparison claims go stale quickly.
- Build the comparison pages before launch; the ads stay paused until they're live.
- Run it again after 30 days with the campaign's real conversion rate in place of the planning assumption.
