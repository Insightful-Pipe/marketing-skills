# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-email-teardown" skill. Here are 8 weeks of emails from my three biggest competitors and from my own seed inbox. Compare their email programs with mine and tell me what to adopt, what to avoid and which three tests to run.

## What you need

- **Rival emails with dates.** One research inbox per rival, on a domain you own, signed up on the same day. Paste the emails or upload a mailbox export (received time, sender, subject, preview and body). 28 days is the minimum; 56 days with one sale in it is better. If you haven't started yet, the skill gives you the setup and waits.
- **Your own program,** either from a seed inbox you signed up the same way, or from Klaviyo or Mailchimp connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP. With a connection it reads your flows, your welcome and campaign results and your signup volume itself.
- **Your discount ceiling,** the deepest discount you'd ever give. Without it, the skill uses the deepest discount in your own emails.

## Example requests

**Full teardown**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-email-teardown" skill. Tear down the email programs of these three rivals from the attached inbox exports and compare them with my Klaviyo account.

**Welcome series only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-email-teardown" skill. How fast do my competitors send their welcome email, how many emails follow in the first two weeks, and what do they offer at signup? Compare it with my welcome flow.

**Sale timing**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-email-teardown" skill. Here are my rivals' Black Friday emails and mine. When did each of us start, how many emails did we send and how deep did the discounts go?

**Getting set up**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-email-teardown" skill. I want to track competitor emails. How do I set up research inboxes the right way?

## What you get

1. **A side-by-side table** of every program: minutes to the first email, emails in the first 14 days, regular sends a week, signup offer, deepest discount, subject line patterns, the promotional, product and content mix, and sale timing, with the rival median.
2. **A profile of each rival,** including the offer on its signup page and how many of its Meta ads push that offer.
3. **What to adopt,** only changes that cost no margin and add no regular sends.
4. **What to avoid,** such as deceptive subject lines, deadlines that move and discounts above your ceiling.
5. **Three tests,** each with a baseline from your own data, the sample size per arm and how many weeks it takes on your volume.
6. **What couldn't be seen,** stated plainly.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It never signs up for a rival's list, never edits your flows or campaigns, and never sends anything.

## Tips

- Sign every inbox up on the same day, and note the exact time of each signup.
- Use addresses on your own domain. Never use a person's private address or someone else's details.
- Run it again after a big sale season to see how each program changed.
