# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ideas-generator" skill. Give me marketing ideas for my business and tell me which ten to try first.

## What you need

- **A line about your business:** what you sell, to whom and where. That is enough to start.
- **Your budget and hours** for new ideas each month. Without them, the skill assumes $0 and 5 hours a week and says so.
- **What you've tried** and what is off-limits, so it doesn't suggest them again.
- **Optional, for sharper scores:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with GA4, Search Console and DataForSEO, plus up to three competitor names for the public ad libraries. Without them, the skill scores from what you tell it and labels its assumptions.

## Example requests

**Small business**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ideas-generator" skill. I run a small online tea shop. Give me marketing ideas for my small business. We have $500 a month and 8 hours a week.

**Creative ideas only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ideas-generator" skill. I need creative marketing ideas for my dog grooming van in Denver. Nothing that needs me on camera.

**From your own data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ideas-generator" skill. Use my GA4 and Search Console to find marketing ideas for my B2B bookkeeping service, and check what my two main competitors advertise.

**One channel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ideas-generator" skill. Give me email marketing ideas for my yoga studio's list of 900 members.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:** which of your channels already convert, what people search for, which offers competitors run on Meta and for how long, and whether they advertise on Google.
2. **The ten ideas to try first,** ranked with Impact, Confidence and Ease scores shown, each with a first step, cost, hours, an estimate and a stop rule.
3. **Up to three to start now** that fit one month of your budget and hours together.
4. **The long list** of every idea that passed, with scores.
5. **What was cut and why:** ideas that don't fit your model, budget, hours, past results or off-limits.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the shortlist to a new tab in a Google Sheet you choose. It never launches ads, posts, sends emails or spends money.

## Tips

- Tell it your current monthly orders, leads or bookings if GA4 isn't connected. Impact scores compare each idea against that number.
- Name real competitors. An offer they have kept running on Meta for 60 days or more is some of the best proof it pays. The Google library only shows whether they advertise there, not what or for how long.
- Run it again each quarter, and tell it which ideas worked. They raise Confidence for similar ideas next time.
