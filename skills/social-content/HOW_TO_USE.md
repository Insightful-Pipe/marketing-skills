# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Look at what worked on our social accounts and plan and write our next two weeks of posts.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with at least one social account added:** a Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok, a LinkedIn Page, Threads or X. The skill reads your past posts and their views itself; you don't export anything.
- **Your goal,** if it isn't reach: engagement, or clicks to your site.
- **Who you're writing for,** in one line, and the one thing you want them to do. Without it, the skill infers the audience from your top posts and says so.
- **How many posts a week your team can really make,** if you know.
- **Optional:** two or three posts whose voice you like, the markets to adapt captions for, and a current offer.

No social account connected? Paste 10 to 20 recent posts with their dates and views, and the skill works from those.

## Example requests

**Strategy and plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Read our Facebook Page and TikTok, tell me which topics and formats beat our usual, and give me pillars, a content mix and a posting cadence we can keep up.

**Captions in our voice**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Write Instagram captions for next week in the voice of our best posts, with hashtags that have worked for us.

**Reel and Story scripts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Turn our three best-performing topics into Reel scripts and one Story sequence with a poll.

**LinkedIn and X**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Write a LinkedIn post and an X thread from our top topic this quarter, and a building-in-public update about our launch.

**Personal brand**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. I'm a founder posting as myself on Instagram and TikTok. Read my posts and plan a content calendar for the next two weeks in my own voice.

**Hashtag campaign and other markets**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Plan a branded hashtag campaign for our customers, and adapt the launch caption for the UK and for Mexico.

**Hashtag research**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Grade the hashtags we've used on Instagram and TikTok, tell me how many hashtags to use on Instagram now, and build three rotating sets per topic with one new tag to test in each.

**Check a post before it goes out**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Here's a LinkedIn post and a tweet we're about to publish. Check them against our best posts on each network and the current limits, and rewrite only what fails.

**Bluesky and Mastodon**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Should we be on Bluesky? If yes, tell me how to set it up, borrow what works on our other networks, and write our first two weeks of Bluesky posts.

**Publish one**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-content" skill. Write this week's Facebook post from our best topic, show it to me, and publish it to our Page once I say yes.

## What you get

1. **What your history says:** each network's posts, views and gaps, and which pillars, formats, hook styles, hashtag habits, video lengths and weekdays scored better or worse than your usual, with the post counts behind each verdict.
2. **A strategy:** which network leads, your pillars with a content mix (promotion kept to 20% or less), a cadence you can keep, the days to post (only when your data shows one), and posts worth boosting.
3. **A two-week plan:** every slot with its network, pillar, format, hook and the evidence for it, plus one test a week.
4. **Ready-to-edit drafts:** Facebook and LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions with hashtags, Reel and TikTok scripts, Story sequences, carousels, X threads and Threads posts, each checked against the network's limits and pointing to your one action. Facts only you know, such as the time a task used to take, stay in [brackets] for you to fill.
5. **On request:** a branded hashtag campaign, captions adapted for other markets, and building-in-public posts.
6. **Other modes:** hashtag research (your tags graded on your own posts, candidates sized, how many per network, rotating sets), a pre-publish check of a draft against your top 10% of posts and the network's limits with a rewrite of what fails, and a Bluesky and Mastodon plan with a keep-or-drop test after 8 weeks.
7. **Habits:** a daily engagement routine, a batching day and a weekly review, so the next run has better data.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact post, one at a time, it can publish a text, link or image post to a Facebook Page, Instagram, a LinkedIn Page, Threads or X (X bills each post to your own X developer account, and the skill states the cost first). Videos need the file you film, so you post Reels and TikToks yourself. Publishing needs a Read & Write connection, and an owner, admin or operator can switch publishing off. It never schedules, edits or deletes posts, and Bluesky and Mastodon posts are drafts you post yourself.

## Tips

- Rerun it monthly: every post you publish gives the next plan better evidence.
- A small or new account gets "too few to judge" on many labels. That's honest, and the plan turns those into tests.
- Keep the test slots: changing one thing at a time is how you learn what your audience wants.
