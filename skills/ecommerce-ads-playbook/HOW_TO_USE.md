# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-ads-playbook" skill. Audit my online store and tell me what to fix first, then build the ads plan around it.

## Two modes

- **Mode 1: Ads playbook.** A full-funnel advertising strategy for your store: Google Shopping and Performance Max, Meta catalog and Advantage+ sales campaigns, TikTok, budgets by spend level, and ROAS targets checked against your margin.
- **Mode 2: Store growth audit.** An audit of the store itself on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or any cart that exports orders. It scores ten areas and ranks the fixes by the money at stake.

## What you need

**For Mode 1:** your AOV, target ROAS or CPA, monthly budget, the channels you run today and your margin. If Google Ads or Meta Ads is connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, it reads the last 30 days itself.

**For Mode 2:** no connection is needed. Paste or upload:
- your orders export for the audit window (90 days by default), plus the 12 months before it (only customer and date are needed for those)
- your products export
- optionally: a GA4 export of sessions and purchases by channel and device, PageSpeed results for your home, a collection and a product page, your email tool's flow list, your review app's export, and links to your shipping and returns policies

If Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, GA4, PageSpeed, the crawler, Merchant Center or Klaviyo is connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, it reads those instead of the exports.

## Example requests

**Ads strategy for a store**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-ads-playbook" skill. We sell skincare on Shopify, AOV $45, $20K a month for ads. Build our Google and Meta plan.

**Shopify store audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-ads-playbook" skill. Run a Shopify store audit on our store for the last 90 days and rank the fixes by money.

**Audit from exports, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-ads-playbook" skill. Here are my WooCommerce orders and products exports and a GA4 export by channel and device. How do I audit my online store from these?

**Ecommerce store audit checklist**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-ads-playbook" skill. Use your ecommerce store audit checklist on our Magento store: where is it leaking money?

**Audit, then plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ecommerce-ads-playbook" skill. Audit the store first, then use the numbers to build next quarter's ads plan.

## What you get

**Mode 1:** a strategy document with the budget split by channel and funnel stage, campaign structures for Google and Meta, a 12-week roadmap and success metrics.

**Mode 2:**
1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown.
2. **A scorecard for ten areas:** sales base and refunds, conversion by channel, mobile vs desktop, first vs returning buyers, top and dead products, discount dependence, template speed, tracking health, email capture and core flows, and reviews and policies.
3. **The top fixes, ranked by the money at stake per 30 days,** each with its formula, then the fixes that can't be priced honestly.
4. **What couldn't be checked,** and the input that would fill it.
5. **Your store's numbers, ready for Mode 1,** so the ads plan starts from your real AOV, conversion rate and discount share.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. Both modes are read-only: they recommend, and you or the matching skill make the change.

## Tips

- Run the audit before a big ads push, and again 60 to 90 days after the fixes.
- Tell it if you sell apparel or footwear: refunds are graded on higher lines for those.
- If you know your gross margin, say so, and the money at stake is easier to weigh.
