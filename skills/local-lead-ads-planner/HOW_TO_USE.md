# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "local-lead-ads-planner" skill. Are Yelp ads worth it for my business, and should I keep Angi, Thumbtack and Nextdoor? Judge them on booked jobs.

## What you need

- **Your lead lists.** Exports from Angi, Thumbtack and Yelp (Request a Quote messages), or your own lead log, with the outcome of each lead.
- **Your invoices or job values,** so each booked job has a dollar figure.
- **What you spent** on each platform in the last 90 days (lead charges, ad spend, any monthly fee).
- **Your gross margin on a job,** and how much of a job's gross profit you're willing to spend to win it. Without the second number, the skill uses 40%.
- **Optional: CallRail and Google Business Profile connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP.** The skill then reads calls, call length, missed calls and form leads per tracking number, and your Business Profile call clicks, reviews and photos itself.

## Example requests

**Is it worth it?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-lead-ads-planner" skill. Are Yelp ads worth it for my plumbing company? Here's my Yelp invoice and my jobs from the last three months.

**Two platforms head to head**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-lead-ads-planner" skill. Thumbtack vs Angi leads: which one gets me more booked jobs per dollar? Both exports are attached.

**Starting on Nextdoor**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-lead-ads-planner" skill. I'm thinking about Nextdoor ads for my small cleaning business. Work out what I can afford to pay per lead before I start.

**Getting money back**
Hey Claude, I just added the "local-lead-ads-planner" skill. Go through my Angi leads from the last month and draft credit requests for the ones that qualify.

## What you get

1. **A verdict per platform:** scale, keep, fix first, cut or not graded, with the numbers behind it, and your Google Business Profile calls as the no-cost benchmark.
2. **The most a lead and a booked job can cost you,** worked out from your own margin, job values and close rate.
3. **A scorecard:** leads, valid leads, booked jobs, close rate, average job value, cost per lead and per booked job, and gross profit after lead cost.
4. **Which services and ZIP codes to keep paying for,** and which to drop.
5. **How your profile compares** with the businesses shown beside you, on rating, reviews, photos and recent reviews.
6. **A response-time target** taken from how your own leads booked at different reply speeds, plus missed calls per tracking number.
7. **Credit and dispute drafts** for leads that meet a platform's rules, and a list of the ones that don't qualify and why. The platform decides every request.
8. **The top 5 actions,** in order.

## What it can change for you

With CallRail connected and your yes on the exact change, it can mark junk calls and forms as "not a lead" with a note, and tag leads you've asked a platform to credit. It can't change anything on Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack or Nextdoor; the report tells you where to make each change.

## Tips

- Give each platform its own CallRail tracking number. Without one, calls from Yelp and Nextdoor can't be told apart.
- Record why every lead didn't book. "Outside our area" and "number not in service" are what make credit requests possible.
- Run it every month, and send credit requests weekly: Angi only takes them on leads under 45 days old.
