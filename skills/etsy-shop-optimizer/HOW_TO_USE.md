# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "etsy-shop-optimizer" skill. Audit my Etsy shop and tell me which listings to fix first. My listings CSV and last month's Stats are below.

## What you need

- **Your listings CSV.** In Shop Manager: Settings > Options > Download Data > "Currently for sale listings".
- **Your Stats for one period** (the last 30 days by default), pasted or as a screenshot: the shop totals, the listing table (views, favorites, orders, revenue) and "How shoppers found you".
- **Optional, and it makes the audit sharper:** the Search visibility page, your shop sections, About and policies, and listings that went live during the period. After the first pass the skill asks for a few details on the listings worth fixing only: category, attributes, US shipping price and 3 to 5 similar listings.
- **Optional upgrade:** the DataForSEO MCP server connected through InsightfulPipe, to measure search demand for your tags. The DataForSEO account needs a balance; this path is untested through InsightfulPipe so far, because the connected test logins had none. Without it, the audit still runs and every tag says "demand not measured".

## Example requests

**Full shop audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "etsy-shop-optimizer" skill. Run an Etsy shop audit on my listings CSV and the last 30 days of Stats, and rank the listings by what they cost me.

**Listings not getting views**
Hey Claude, I just added the "etsy-shop-optimizer" skill. Half my Etsy listings get almost no views. Find out which ones and why.

**Views but no sales**
Hey Claude, I just added the "etsy-shop-optimizer" skill. My serving bowls get lots of views and hardly sell. Check price, shipping and photos against similar listings.

**Etsy SEO for tags**
Hey Claude, I just added the "etsy-shop-optimizer" skill. Check which of my tags have real search demand and which ones are wasting a slot.

## What you get

1. **Where orders are lost:** every listing sorted into not found, found but not chosen, earning or too little data, using your shop's own median views and order rate.
2. **A fix list of up to 10 listings,** ranked by the revenue at stake, each with its checks (tags, title, photos, description, category and attributes, price and shipping) and the first fix to make.
3. **Shop-wide patterns** and **shop checks:** sections, logo and About, policies, shipping and customer service.
4. **Tag demand** (with DataForSEO): tags to keep, tags to drop and true candidate tags, each with its Google US monthly searches, labelled as a proxy because Etsy publishes no search volumes.
5. **Rewrite briefs** for every fix-list listing with a title, tag or description problem, or with tags to drop or add, ready for product-description-writer if you have it installed.
6. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and what to paste to fill it.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. There is no Etsy connector, so the skill never edits a listing or your shop. You make every fix in Shop Manager, and the skill tells you exactly what and where.
