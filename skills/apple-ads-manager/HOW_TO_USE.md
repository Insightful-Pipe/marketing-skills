# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. Audit my Apple Search Ads account from these exports and tell me which keywords to move, pause and negate.

## What you need

- **Your Apple Ads exports** for one period: campaigns, ad groups, keywords, search terms, negative keywords and ads. Download them from each tab in Apple Ads, or paste the tables.
- **What an install is worth:** an MMP or subscription platform cohort report with revenue per install at day 7 or day 30, or the Firebase export in BigQuery connected through InsightfulPipe.
- **Your payback target,** if you have one. Without it, the skill aims to earn back the install cost by day 30.
- **Your subtitle and keyword field,** because the App Store doesn't show them publicly.
- Optional: **the InsightfulPipe MCP** with the Apple App Store connector, so the skill reads your listing, your newest reviews and competitor apps itself.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. Here are my Apple Ads exports for the last 30 days and our MMP day-30 cohort. Audit the account and rank the fixes by money.

**Campaign structure**
Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. Is my Apple Search Ads campaign structure right? I have one campaign with everything in it. Show me how to split it into brand, category, competitor and discovery.

**Search term harvest**
Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. Go through my discovery campaign's search terms and give me the terms to move to exact and the negatives to add.

**Keyword bidding**
Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. Our installs bring in $4.80 each by day 7 and we want 40% of the install cost back by then. Which keyword bids should I lower or raise?

**Build a new account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. We're launching Apple Ads for our iOS app in the US and UK. Build the campaigns, keywords, negatives and starting bids.

**Brand campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "apple-ads-manager" skill. Do I need to pay for my own brand name in Apple Ads? Plan a test.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard** for eight areas: install value, structure, overlap and negatives, keywords, search terms, listing match, product pages and the brand test.
2. **A cost-per-install ceiling for each campaign,** set from your own revenue per install, with each campaign's actual cost against it.
3. **A decision for every keyword:** pause, lower bid (with the new bid), watch, headroom or keep, with the numbers behind it.
4. **Search term moves ready to paste:** terms to move into exact with a starting bid, negatives, and leaks between campaigns.
5. **Listing gaps:** keywords whose words your listing never uses, with the reviews that ask for them, and a check on your recent rating.
6. **Custom product page results** and where a new page would help.
7. **A brand pause test plan** with your account's numbers.
8. **The top fixes, ranked by money,** and a list of what couldn't be checked.

In build mode you get a campaign plan instead: four campaigns per storefront, ad groups, keywords, match types, negatives, starting bid caps and product pages.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. There is no Apple Ads connector, so the skill gives you every keyword list, negative and bid ready to enter or upload in Apple Ads.

## Tips

- Export tap-through installs, and use the same date range in every tab.
- Run it every two weeks: search terms need time to collect installs.
- Your first rule of thumb: brand, category and competitor campaigns use exact match with Search Match off; only discovery uses broad and Search Match.
