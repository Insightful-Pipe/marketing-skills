# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-journey-map" skill. Map how our customers find us, buy and come back, and tell me the five touchpoints to fix first.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is fine.** Describe or paste your stages, touchpoints, who owns them, customer quotes, and any export you have (GA4, Search Console, a call log, a CRM export with stage dates, a Clarity export). The skill builds the map from that and tags every cell measured or assumed.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With GA4, Search Console, Klaviyo or Mailchimp, Microsoft Clarity, CallRail, Google Business Profile or Google Sheets added, the skill pulls the evidence itself. Mailchimp, Clarity and CallRail were checked against their schemas but not yet run on a live account.
- **What counts as buying** (a booking, a first order, a signed deal), and one kind of customer to map.
- **Any target you hold,** such as the conversion rate a page used to reach.

## Example requests

**A full map from live data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-journey-map" skill. Build a customer journey map for new patients from our GA4, Search Console, Klaviyo and Google reviews, and tell me where people stall.

**From what you know, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-journey-map" skill. Here are our stages, touchpoints and owners, plus a few customer quotes. Turn them into a journey map and mark what we're only assuming.

**After-purchase only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-journey-map" skill. Map what happens after someone's first order: onboarding emails, repeat orders and reviews. Where do people drop away?

**A template filled with your own numbers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-journey-map" skill. I need a customer journey map template for our team, filled in with our real numbers, and written into our Google Sheet.

## What you get

1. **How people really move:** the channel that first brought your buyers against the one they bought through, and how many days they took.
2. **The map:** one row per stage with the customer's question and feeling, the touchpoints, the content that serves them, where people stall, the owner and the health metric. Every cell says measured or assumed.
3. **The five touchpoints to fix first,** ranked by the bookings or sales each one loses a month, each with its evidence, the fix, the owner and the metric to watch.
4. **Coverage:** how much of the map is measured, and the one source that would measure most of the rest.
5. **Unknowns** and next steps.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can write the map into a new tab of your Google Sheet. It never changes your pages, emails, ads, phones or reviews.

## Tips

- Map one kind of customer at a time. New and returning customers, or two very different buyers, get a map each.
- Give owners by name of role ("front desk lead", "agency"). A touchpoint with no owner is often the reason it stalls.
- Run it again a month after the fixes to see the stalls shrink and more cells turn measured.
