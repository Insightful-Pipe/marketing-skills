# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Build a LinkedIn campaign for our product page aimed at US marketing directors, $50 a day, and write the intro text and headlines.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your LinkedIn ad account added.** The skill reads the account itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **A short brief:** what you sell, who it's for, the goal, the landing page, the daily budget and the dates.
- **For lead gen, your privacy policy URL,** which LinkedIn requires on every lead form.
- **The facts the ads may use,** such as features, numbers or proof. Without them, the copy only says what your landing page says.
- **An image** (1200 x 628 or 1200 x 1200) when you're ready to create the ad. The plan can be drafted without it.

## Example requests

**One campaign from a brief**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Set up a website visits campaign for our demo page, aimed at IT directors at companies with 200 to 5,000 staff in the UK. $80 a day for four weeks.

**A full funnel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Plan a three-stage LinkedIn funnel for our new report: awareness, then site visits, then a lead gen form. $150 a day in total.

**Copy only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Write five intro texts and five headlines for a LinkedIn ad offering a benchmark report to heads of finance.

**Plan, then build**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Draft the campaign, show me every payload, and create it once I say yes.

## What you get

1. **What your account already tells us:** your company page as the ad author, past spend and where it really went, conversions, lead forms and test clutter.
2. **The plan:** campaign group, campaign or funnel, objective, bidding, budget, dates and targeting, with the source of every targeting code.
3. **A price check** from LinkedIn's suggested bids for that exact audience, with an estimate of the clicks a day your budget can buy.
4. **Five intro texts and five headlines,** each labelled with its angle, its length and the fact it rests on.
5. **A pre-flight checklist** that checks for the setup mistakes LinkedIn rejects ads for, such as a missing landing page or an ad that looks like a test.
6. **The exact payloads,** in the order they run, each marked "not run" until you approve.

## What it can create for you

With your yes on each exact payload, it can create:
- the campaign group
- the campaign, which always starts paused
- a lead gen form, for lead gen campaigns, after you approve its full text
- the image upload and the single image ads

It never switches a campaign on and never changes an existing one. You activate in Campaign Manager once the ads pass review.

## Tips

- Check the audience forecast in Campaign Manager before you activate: the MCP can't count audience size.
- To target job titles, skills or companies, start from a campaign that already uses them, or add them in Campaign Manager; the MCP can't look those codes up.
- After two to four weeks, run an audit skill to see which intro text and audience won.
