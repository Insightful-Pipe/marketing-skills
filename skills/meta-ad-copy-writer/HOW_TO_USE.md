# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-copy-writer" skill. Find my best Meta ads and write five new primary texts and five headlines based on what's winning.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Meta ad account added.** The skill reads your ads and their results itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **What you sell and to whom,** in a sentence or two.
- **Facts the copy may use,** such as prices, offers, numbers or reviews. Without them, the copy makes no claim your own ads don't already make.
- **Your target cost per result,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your account's own median cost per result, taken over the ads with at least 1,000 impressions and at least one result.
- **Two or three competitors,** if your account is new. The skill then reads their longest-running ads in the Meta Ad Library. If the library shows none for a competitor, it says so and gives you the Ad Library link to check by hand.

## Example requests

**From your winners**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-copy-writer" skill. Look at my Meta ads from the last 90 days, tell me which angles win on cost per lead, and write five primary text variations from them.

**Headlines only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write five Facebook ad headlines from the angles in my best ads. Keep them under 27 characters.

**Instagram**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-copy-writer" skill. I need Instagram ad copy for our spring offer. Use what works in my current ads and lead with a short first line.

**New account or startup**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-copy-writer" skill. We're a startup with no ad history. Check the Ad Library for our two biggest competitors and write our first round of Meta ad copy.

**Write, then save**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write the variants, then save them as a creative in my account. Show me the payload first.

## What you get

1. **The winners and losers,** with spend, results, cost per result and the angle and hook behind each.
2. **The pattern:** why the winners win, and what this round tests.
3. **Five primary texts and five headlines,** each with its character count, its angle, the ad it comes from and the one thing it changes.
4. **A test plan,** and when to read the result.
5. **A checks list:** length, claims, words borrowed from competitors' ads, repeats between headlines and texts, and Meta's ad rules.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact copy, it can:
- save the variants as a new ad creative
- put that creative in a new ad, created paused

It never switches ads on, and never changes budgets or targeting.

## Tips

- Run it when a winning ad's frequency climbs or its cost per result starts to rise.
- Give it your real facts: copy with a real number beats copy with none.
- After the test has spent enough, run it again: the new winners feed the next round.
