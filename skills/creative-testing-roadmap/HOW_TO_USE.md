# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. Build me a creative testing roadmap for next quarter from my live ad accounts.

## Example prompts

**Full roadmap**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. Build a creative testing roadmap for our Google Ads for next quarter. We can write about 6 new ad lines a week and have no video team.

**What to test first**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. Look at my Meta ads and tell me which concept or hook to test first, and why.

**Hook plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. My best ad has worked for months. Give me 10 new hooks for it using only claims we already run, and a plan to test them.

**Small account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. We spend about $5K a month. How many creative tests can we actually finish, and which ones are worth it?

**New launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. We launch a new product next month on TikTok. Plan the first creative tests so we find a winner fast.

## What it needs

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP** with at least one of Google Ads, Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn connected. The skill pulls the ads, copy, formats, age and results itself.
- **Your production capacity:** how many new lines, images or videos you can make and approve a week.
- **Claims you allow.** By default it only reuses claims from your approved ads.
- **Optional:** competitor names or domains, for a look at their ads in the public ad libraries.

No connection yet? Paste an ad-level export for the last 91 days with ad name, text, format, start date, impressions, clicks, spend and conversions, and it runs the same steps.

## What you get

1. **Portfolio health:** ad groups with no test running, spend concentration, copy reused everywhere, off-topic lines, old ads with no challenger, format gaps.
2. **What already wins:** every angle and hook type in your copy, with a click index against its own ad, a verdict, and the concepts you've never tried.
3. **Testing capacity:** how many tests your traffic can decide this quarter, by lane of campaigns, with the sample each test needs and how long it takes.
4. **Phased roadmap:** week-0 fixes, then concept, hook and format tests, each with a full brief: hypothesis, baseline, sample, variants, guardrail and decision week.
5. **Hook bank:** ready opening lines with character counts, each traced to an approved claim, or marked "confirm before use".
6. **Production schedule and budget:** what to write or shoot each week, and what the tests cost.
7. **Decision rules and a test log template.**

## Changes it can make (only after you confirm)

- Create the variant ads, paused: a Google responsive search ad, or a Meta ad using an existing creative.
- Switch ad rotation to "do not optimize" on Google campaigns that aren't on Smart Bidding.
- Pause a losing ad on Google or Meta, never the last one in its ad group or ad set.

These need a Read & Write connection. An owner, admin or operator can switch write actions off.

## Tips

- Test the biggest idea first: concept, then hook, then format, then wording.
- Change one thing per test.
- Plan only the tests your traffic can finish. One decided test beats three undecided ones.
- Expect about half your tests to show no clear winner. Log them anyway: the log is where the next roadmap starts.
