# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-strategy-planner" skill. Review our podcast and plan the next 12 episodes.

## What you need

- **Your episode list:** title, release date, length, and the theme and guest of each episode. A pasted list is fine.
- **Your podcast host's stats:** downloads per episode at 7 and 30 days, as a CSV export or pasted. No connector reads audio app or host stats, so this part always comes from you.
- **Apple Podcasts Connect and Spotify for Creators numbers,** if you have them: average consumption per episode and followers gained a month.
- **Your goal and audience:** what the show is for (leads, authority, product use) and who it is for.
- **Your hours:** hours a month for the show and hours per episode.
- **Optional, the InsightfulPipe MCP** with YouTube, DataForSEO, GA4 and Search Console. Then Claude reads each episode's first 30 days on YouTube, its traffic sources, search demand per theme and your episode pages itself.

Starting a new show? Skip the stats. Claude plans the first 12 episodes as tests and sets the baselines after episode 6.

## Example requests

**Review a running show**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-strategy-planner" skill. Here is our host export and episode list. Which themes and guests work, and what should the next 12 episodes be?

**Podcast launch plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-strategy-planner" skill. We want to launch a podcast for practice managers at small clinics. Plan the show and the first 12 episodes. We have 20 hours a month.

**Video podcast on YouTube**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-strategy-planner" skill. Our podcast is on YouTube too. Pull each episode's first 30 days and tell me which topics held people.

**Is it worth continuing?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-strategy-planner" skill. Our company podcast has 20 episodes. Is it working, and what should we drop?

## What you get

1. **An episode table:** every episode judged against your show's own medians on reach (downloads and YouTube views in 30 days) and hold (completion and average view %), with lead signals alongside.
2. **A verdict per theme and guest type:** Grow, Keep, Repackage, Drop or Test, with the numbers behind it and its search demand.
3. **Format:** the length that holds people best on your show, and a release pace your team can keep.
4. **Growth moves,** ranked, each backed by your own numbers.
5. **A dated 12-episode plan** with theme, guest type, working title and the bar each episode must clear.
6. **A keep-or-drop rule per theme,** and a scoreboard with baselines, goals and checkpoint dates.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can add full episodes that are missing from your YouTube podcast playlist. It never edits titles, uploads, publishes or deletes anything.

## Tips

- Tag each episode's theme and guest type yourself if you can. Verdicts are only as good as the tags.
- Run it again at the two checkpoint dates in the plan, with fresh exports.
- Episodes younger than 30 days are marked early or too new, so a run right after a release still compares like with like.
