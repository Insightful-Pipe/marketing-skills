# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Rewrite the hero and headlines of my landing page in the words my searchers actually use, and review what's there now.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** Google Ads gives your buyers' search terms and the ad lines that win. Search Console gives the organic queries for a page. DataForSEO reads the page and the results page around it. Any one of them is enough to start.
- **The asset and the one action you want:** a page URL, an ad group, an email, a tagline, a product name or a draft, and whether you want a call, a booking, a sale or a signup.
- **Proof you can back:** review counts, ratings, years, results, guarantees. The skill only writes claims you confirm are true.
- **Any rules on what you can say,** for example no promised outcomes in legal, medical or financial copy.

No connection? Paste the page text, a list of customer phrases (search terms, reviews, support messages or sales-call notes) or a draft, and the skill runs the same review and writes the same deliverables. The two extra modes need nothing but pasted text: an AI draft plus 5 to 10 pieces of copy you like, or a CSV of reviews, comments or survey answers.

## Example requests

**Landing page rewrite**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Rewrite the hero and headlines of my AC repair landing page in the words my searchers actually use, and review what's there now.

**Copy review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Run your copy review on my pricing page and tell me the three fixes that matter most.

**Taglines and names**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Give me 10 tagline options and 12 product name ideas for our new scheduling feature, built from how our customers search.

**Hooks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Write 10 hooks for short videos about our service, using the questions people actually search.

**Edit a draft**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Here's my homepage draft. Tighten it, fix the structure and the flow, and show me what you changed.

**Make AI writing sound human (Mode A)**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. This email was drafted with AI and it sounds like it. Make it sound like our best posts, check every claim, and show me the AI habits per 100 words before and after.

**Customer words from reviews (Mode B)**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Pull our Google reviews and the comments on our Facebook and Instagram posts, count the phrases customers repeat, and turn them into headlines, benefits and objection answers.

**Ad copy, then build**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copywriting" skill. Write new search ad lines from my winning headlines and search terms, then draft a paused challenger ad. Show me the payload before you create anything.

## What you get

1. **The evidence:** which ads send traffic to the page, your buyers' search terms sorted into themes (place, cost, situation, questions, choice, speed, competitor names, other languages) with the share of clicks Google shows, your ad lines ranked against the lines next to them, and what the current copy says.
2. **The results page around you:** the claims every competitor already makes (table stakes), the ones nobody makes that your buyers care about (openings), and the questions people also ask.
3. **A 10-point copy review:** buyers' words, promise, specifics, proof, objections, next step, difference, clean writing, order and truth, each graded with the line quoted.
4. **An offer check, then new copy for the job:** the offer graded on outcome, time frame, risk reversal, real urgency and anything extra, because no headline saves a weak offer; then headline options, page sections, calls to action, taglines, product names, hooks, ads or emails, with every line tagged with the evidence behind it.
5. **Claims to confirm** before anything is published, **what to test** first, and what couldn't be checked.
6. **Mode A, an AI draft humanized:** your brand's voice measured from the copy that already works (sentence length, rhythm, its own words), each AI habit counted per 100 words in the draft, the edit and your own copy, every sentence's change explained, a clean version that is never longer than the draft, and a claims table. It edits for your readers; it doesn't promise anything about AI detectors.
7. **Mode B, customer words from reviews and comments:** a phrase bank for problems, wanted results, doubts, what they tried before and proof, each phrase counted by distinct people and sources, then two lines per copy slot that cite their counts. Nobody is named or quoted, and a phrase only appears when 3 or more people used it.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can create a challenger responsive search ad built from the new copy. It's always created paused, so you switch it on, and it runs next to your current ad. The skill never edits your pages, pauses ads or changes budgets.

## Tips

- Search terms need volume: under 1,000 impressions in 30 days, the skill widens to 90 days.
- Google hides low-volume search terms, so the skill states what share of clicks it could see.
- Some sites block page readers. If yours does, paste the page text and the skill grades the parts it couldn't read.
- Every new headline is a test, not a verdict. Run the challenger before rewriting everything.
- For Mode A, give at least 300 words of copy you like. Less than that and the skill says the voice profile is thin and uses house targets.
- For Mode B, one chatty reviewer can't skew the bank: each person counts once per phrase.
