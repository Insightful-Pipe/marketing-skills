# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-deck-builder" skill. Write a sales deck for my meeting with a new prospect, check every claim first, and give me the .pptx.

## What you need

- **What you sell and who buys it,** plus the roles in the room (who signs, who uses it, who can block it).
- **Discovery notes** for a deck aimed at one deal, with the buyer's words in quotation marks. For a reusable deck, describe the segment.
- **The buyer's numbers** behind their problem (for example missed appointments a week and what each is worth), so the skill can work out what waiting costs.
- **Your proof:** case studies, customer results, review exports, product reports, the product pages behind the features you will show, and your old deck if you have one. Say where each comes from, when, and whether the customer agreed to be named.
- **What you can offer as the next step,** such as pilot or onboarding terms, so the closing slide promises only what you can deliver.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** the Ad Libraries and Crawler connectors for a competitor's live ads and pages, Google Sheets for a proof tracker, and for agencies, a client's Google Ads, Meta Ads or GA4 account for before and after results. Nothing has to be connected; you can paste everything.

## Example requests

**A deck for one deal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-deck-builder" skill. Here are my discovery notes from a 6-clinic physio group and our case studies. Write the deck for Tuesday's call and make a version for their practice managers.

**Fix an old deck**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-deck-builder" skill. Here is our current sales deck. Check every claim on it, tell me which ones a buyer could knock down, and rewrite it.

**A deck against a competitor**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-deck-builder" skill. Build our sales presentation outline for mid-size dental groups, and compare us with Competitor A using their live ads and home page.

**An agency deck with client results**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-deck-builder" skill. We are an agency pitching a regional retailer. Pull before and after results from our client's Google Ads and GA4 accounts (they agreed to be a reference, unnamed) and build the proof slides.

## What you get

1. **A readiness line:** ready or not, with the claims counted by status.
2. **A proof ledger and claims check:** every number, quote and comparison with its source and date, and the ones that fail (no source, dated, no permission, unproven comparison, or saying more than the source).
3. **The deck, slide by slide:** headline, body, source line and speaker notes, from the goal the buyer gave you to the one decision you ask for, with each blocker shown next to the capability and proof that remove it, and word counts.
4. **The cost of waiting,** computed from the buyer's own numbers with every assumption shown.
5. **A contrast with the alternative,** built only from what the competitor's own ads and pages show, with the date seen, and naming the kind of buyer who should pick them instead.
6. **A version per persona or industry,** as a table of which slides change and the new copy.
7. **A .pptx file** with speaker notes, when you ask and code can run.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads what you paste and, if connected, public ad libraries, web pages, a sheet and the accounts you name. The .pptx is a file for you.

## Tips

- Paste the buyer's exact words. Slides built on their words land better than slides built on ours.
- Give the customer's permission status for every case study; the skill keeps a named result off a slide until the customer agrees or you say it can be used unnamed.
- Rerun the claims check before each big meeting. Proof older than 24 months is flagged.
- Investor pitch decks are a different job; this skill doesn't write them.
