# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sponsorship-proposal-writer" skill. Help me get sponsors for our event: price the packages, find brands to pitch, and write the sponsorship deck and emails.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is required.** Paste your attendance, your audience, your past sponsors and what they paid, and the skill works from that, labelling each number "from the user, not measured".
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with any of GA4, Klaviyo or Mailchimp, Facebook Pages, Instagram, LinkedIn Pages and YouTube. The skill then measures your audience itself and dates every number.
- **Your past sponsor deals,** with the fee and what each sponsor got. Prices are set from them.
- **Your target,** if you have one, and what you can sell (naming, booths, emails, posts).

## Example requests

**A first sponsorship deck**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sponsorship-proposal-writer" skill. We run a 1,200-runner trail race in May. Build our sponsorship deck and packages; we need $40,000.

**Pricing only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sponsorship-proposal-writer" skill. Last year our presenting sponsor paid $6,000. What should we charge this year, given our email list and followers grew?

**Finding sponsors**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sponsorship-proposal-writer" skill. Find brands likely to sponsor our nonprofit's annual gala and write the first email to the top five.

**After the event**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sponsorship-proposal-writer" skill. Give me the sponsor report template so I can show each sponsor what they got and ask them to renew.

## What you get

1. **An audience ledger:** every audience number with its source and date.
2. **Priced package tiers,** with the math: the rate your past sponsors paid, each tier's impressions, the most people it can reach, and the sponsor's cost per 1,000 impressions.
3. **A check against your target:** sell-out revenue and the slots it takes to get there.
4. **A scored list of likely sponsors,** with the evidence for each, from your own lists and from brands already advertising to your audience.
5. **A deck outline, a one-page proposal letter, outreach emails with two follow-ups each,** and a post-event sponsor report template.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your accounts and public ad libraries and writes text. You send the emails.

## Tips

- Give it last year's deals, even small ones. Without them it asks you for a price instead of guessing a market rate. If your only past deal included naming or exclusivity, it still prices the tiers, using a default premium it labels, and asks you for one comparable price without naming.
- Upload the registration export if you have one, so attendance is a count rather than an estimate.
- Use the report template on event day: check-in counts and UTM-tagged sponsor links are what make the renewal easy.
