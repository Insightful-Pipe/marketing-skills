# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. We're launching a new feature in four weeks. Plan the launch from our own data and tell me if we're ready.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, ideally with GA4 added.** GA4 gives the baseline, past spikes and launch-day targets; without it the skill still plans from numbers you give it and labels them as yours. Search Console, Klaviyo, your Facebook Page, YouTube, Google Ads and PageSpeed make the plan sharper. The skill pulls the data itself; you don't export anything.
- **What you're launching and when:** a product, a feature, a book, a webinar or a push for something you already sell, plus the launch date.
- **The launch page and the conversion that counts** (sign-up, pre-order, registration, demo request), if they aren't obvious.
- **Your paid budget and goals,** if you have them. The plan never invents a goal.

## Example requests

**Product or feature launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. We're launching a new feature on November 3. Plan it from our data, tell me what isn't ready, and give me launch-day targets. We have $2,000 for paid.

**Go-to-market plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. Build a go-to-market plan for our new product: which channels we actually have reach on, a dated timeline and what to fix first.

**Product Hunt**
Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. Should we launch on Product Hunt? If yes, give me the prep list and the launch-day plan.

**Book launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. My book comes out in 10 weeks. Plan the pre-orders, reviewer copies, newsletter and podcast tour around it.

**Webinar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. Outline a 45-minute webinar on our onboarding topic and plan the three weeks of promotion before it.

**Promotion for an existing product**
Hey Claude, I just added the "launch-strategy" skill. Plan a two-week promotion for our main product, using the channels where we have the most reach.

## What you get

1. **A baseline from your own data:** traffic, new users and conversions per day, any tracking gaps, your channel mix (owned, rented, borrowed, paid) and how many people search for your brand.
2. **Launch memory:** your past traffic spikes, how big they were, how fast they faded and what drove each one.
3. **A readiness check:** conversion tracking, email list, waitlist form, welcome flow, launch page speed and brand search, each graded with the numbers behind it, plus the questions only you can answer (product, capacity, support, assets).
4. **A dated timeline** worked back from launch day, with phases marked on time, compressed or missed, and fixes first.
5. **A channel plan,** a launch-day run of show, Product Hunt, book or webinar steps when they apply, and launch-day targets set from your own past spikes.
6. **Draft outlines for the launch email, the launch post and the follow-up email,** and what couldn't be checked.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- create the launch email as a Klaviyo draft (it never sends or schedules it)
- mark your launch conversion as a GA4 key event, when the event already fires
- publish your approved launch post on your Facebook Page, on launch day

It never changes budgets, bids, ads, prices or discounts.

## Tips

- Run it 6 to 12 weeks before launch, so fixes have time to land. Closer than that, it still plans, but marks the phases it had to squeeze.
- If GA4 stopped recording for a while, the skill leaves those days out instead of reading them as a quiet period.
- Run it again 4 weeks after launch to compare the month after with the baseline.
