# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "case-study-writer" skill. Write a case study on how our /blog section grew in Google search since we relaunched it on 2026-03-01.

## What you need

- **For a story from your own data:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with Google Search Console added. GA4, Google Ads and Notion are optional. The skill pulls the data itself; you don't export anything.
- **For a story from a customer interview:** nothing connected. Paste the customer's form answers and the transcript or your notes, or start from scratch and let the skill write the request, the form and the interview questions.
- **What you changed and when:** the date, a sentence on what was done, and any later milestones.
- **The pages or campaigns that changed:** a URL pattern such as `/blog/`, or the Google Ads campaign names.
- **Whose results they are.** Your own, or a client's with their written OK to publish. Without that OK, the skill writes an anonymised draft.
- **A testimonial,** if you have one, with the customer's OK to quote it.

## Example requests

**SEO launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "case-study-writer" skill. We launched our templates library at /templates on 2026-04-15. Write a case study on what it did in Google search. It's our own site.

**Did the change work?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "case-study-writer" skill. We rewrote our pricing page on 2026-08-01. Check whether it really moved organic clicks before we write anything about it.

**Paid campaign result for a client**
Hey Claude, I just added the "case-study-writer" skill. Write a case study on the Google Ads restructure we did for a client on 2026-06-01: the campaigns are Brand and Generic. The client has agreed in writing to be named.

**Customer interview: ask, prepare, then write**
Hey Claude, I just added the "case-study-writer" skill. One of our customers, a 40-person accounting firm, told us they close their month two days faster since moving to our software. Write the email asking them for a case study, the pre-interview form and the interview questions. Once I paste the transcript, write the case study, the quotes they need to approve and a short paragraph for our sales team.

**Testimonial into a story**
Hey Claude, I just added the "case-study-writer" skill. Turn this testimonial into a short customer story and pair it with our data: "…". The customer agreed to be quoted as Head of Marketing at their company.

## What you get

1. **A verdict first:** result, result (already growing), not a result yet, or too early, with the numbers behind it.
2. **The case study draft:** headline, summary, starting point, what was done, results with a table, how it was measured, and what's next.
3. **A social post and 3 slide bullets** built from the same numbers.
4. **A check of each later milestone** you named.
5. **A claims ledger:** every number in the draft with the call and dates it came from.
6. **What was left out, and why** (for example GA4 months with broken tracking).
7. **A testimonial story,** or the interview kit to get one.

From a customer interview you get the request to send, the form, the interview questions in order, then the case study, a quote set for the customer to approve, a sales paragraph, an approval message and a list of numbers to confirm. Every number is traced to a form answer, a transcript line or, with the customer's OK, their connected account.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact page, it can save the draft as a private page in your Notion. It never publishes anything.

## Tips

- Name the headline metric before you look at the results, and let the other metrics show too.
- Give at least 14 days after a change, ideally 28. The skill tells you when there's enough data.
- If your traffic peaked and fell since, the skill says so in the draft. That's what makes the rest of the story believable.
- Send the form before the interview call. Numbers written down beforehand are more accurate than numbers recalled on the call, and the skill flags any that disagree.
