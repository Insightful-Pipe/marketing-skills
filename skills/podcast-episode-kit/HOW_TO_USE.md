# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-episode-kit" skill. Prep my interview with this guest, then turn the transcript into show notes, titles, chapters and quotes.

## What you need

- **For the prep:** the show's name, your target length, the guest's name with a few links (site, profile, past interviews), and 2 to 4 topics you want to cover, ranked.
- **For the package:** the transcript with timestamps and speaker names (SRT, VTT or `[mm:ss] Name: text` lines), and the URLs for anything the episode mentions.
- **Nothing to connect.** It works from what you paste.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude.** With DataForSEO it measures how many people search the phrases in your titles and finds the guest's past appearances. With Search Console it adds the words that already bring searchers to your episode pages. With YouTube it checks your titles against your best-performing episodes. With WordPress it links related episodes and can save the episode page as a draft.

## Example requests

**Before recording**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-episode-kit" skill. I'm interviewing a clinic owner next Tuesday for 45 minutes. Here are her links and my three topics. Write the podcast episode outline and the interview questions in order.

**After recording**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-episode-kit" skill. Here's the transcript of this week's episode. Write the show notes with timestamps, chapters, 3 title options and the episode description.

**Show notes template for every episode**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-episode-kit" skill. Use the same show notes layout for every episode: takeaways, chapters, quotes, links and the guest's bio. Start with this transcript.

**Titles people search for**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-episode-kit" skill. Our working title is "Ep 48: The No-Show Fix". Give me better titles that use what people search, and check them against our best YouTube episodes.

## What you get

1. **Guest brief:** what the guest's own sources say, each fact with its source, plus any facts that disagree.
2. **Past appearances,** and which of your topics the guest has already answered on another show.
3. **A timed outline** that adds up to your target length, and **the questions in order** with follow-ups.
4. **Title options,** each with 5 checks shown, and one recommendation.
5. **A short and a full episode description.**
6. **Chapters** checked against YouTube and Spotify rules.
7. **Show notes** with timestamps and links, plus a list of links you still need.
8. **Pull quotes** copied word for word, each with its timestamp and transcript line.
9. **The episode page text,** ready for your site.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact draft, it can save the episode page to WordPress as a **draft**. It never publishes, and it never changes your YouTube videos or podcast feed.

## Tips

- Run the prep a few days before recording, so you have time to listen to the guest's past interviews.
- Give it the final transcript, after edits. A transcript of the raw recording puts every timestamp in the wrong place.
- Paste the links as you go. Anything the episode mentions without a link is listed for you to ask the guest about.
