# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. Check all my Google Business Profile locations, rank them, and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Business Profile locations added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Which locations matter most,** if you have many. Without it, the skill checks them all.
- **For drafts:** the real offer, dates, terms, hours or photo links. The skill never makes these up.
- **For a suspended profile:** Google's email or banner text, and what the appeals tool shows. This mode also works with no connection, from the profile details you paste.

## Example requests

**Health check and ranking**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. Check all my Google Business Profile locations, rank them, and tell me what to fix first.

**Calls, directions and website clicks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. Show me calls, direction requests and website clicks for each location over the last four weeks, against the four weeks before.

**Search keywords**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. Which searches are bringing people to my profile, and how many of them already know my name?

**Reviews**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. How are my reviews trending, and which ones still need a reply? Draft the replies, but don't post them yet.

**Offer or event post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. Draft an offer post: 15% off all services from 1 to 14 November, code FALL15, in-store only. Show me the post before publishing.

**Hours and holiday hours**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. We're closed on Thanksgiving and open 10:00 to 15:00 the day after. Check my hours and draft the change.

**Suspended or unverified profile**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-business-profile-manager" skill. My Google Business Profile was suspended yesterday. Here's the email from Google. Work out what breaks the guidelines, tell me what to change, and prepare the appeal and the documents I need. Don't submit anything.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100 for each location,** with the math shown, across seven areas: verification and data access, profile completeness, hours, photos, posts and public content, reviews, and performance trend.
2. **A ranking of your locations** by customer actions (calls, direction requests, website clicks, messages, bookings and food orders) and actions per 1,000 impressions.
3. **The top 5 fixes across all locations,** each with the numbers behind it and who can do it.
4. **The search keywords** that brought people to each profile, split into branded and discovery searches.
5. **An unanswered review queue,** oldest first, without reviewer names.
6. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why. Q&A is always on it: Google closed the Q&A API in November 2025, so check questions in Google Maps.
7. **For a suspended or unverified profile:** the profile's state with the evidence for it, each field checked against Google's guidelines, the changes to make, and an appeal pack: the explanation, the documents to gather and a video verification shot list. You submit it yourself in Google Business Profile, because Google has no API for appeals.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- reply to a review
- publish an offer, event or update post
- edit the text of a live post
- add a logo, cover or other photo from a link you give it

Hours, holiday hours and other profile details can't be edited through the connection. The skill drafts the exact change, and you enter it in Google Business Profile.

## Tips

- Run it monthly, and before a holiday season so special hours are in place.
- Google's numbers arrive a few days late, so the skill's window ends 5 days before today.
- If a location shows "performance data refused", open it in Google Business Profile and look for a pending step or a notice.
