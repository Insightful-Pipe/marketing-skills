# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-brief-generator" skill. Write creative briefs for our best campaign from what already works in the account, with angles, a brief per angle and the ad copy.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with Google Ads or Meta added.** The skill reads your best ads, lines and search terms itself; you don't export anything.
- **Nothing else for the landing page and competitor ads.** The crawler and the public ad libraries are built in.
- **Optional:** the format you need (static, video, UGC, carousel or an agency handoff), the platform, competitors to look at, and any words you must or must not use.

No ad account connected? Paste your three best ads with a result each, and the landing page URL. The skill still reads the page and the ad libraries.

## Example requests

**Brief from the account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-brief-generator" skill. Write three creative briefs for my top Google Ads campaign, for Meta static ads and a short Reels video.

**Angles first**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-brief-generator" skill. Brainstorm ad angles from my Meta ads and my competitors' ads, score them, and tell me which three to brief.

**UGC creator brief**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-brief-generator" skill. Write a UGC creator brief for our payment-plan offer: what the creator must say, talking points and shots.

**Agency handoff**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-brief-generator" skill. Turn my best-performing message into an agency brief with specs, deliverables, approvals and success criteria.

**Short copy only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-brief-generator" skill. Write a product ad and three micro ads for my landing page, with character counts.

## What you get

1. **What the data says:** your benchmark (CTR, conversion rate, CPA), the lines that prove themselves, the words customers search with, the landing page's promise and claims, and what competitors run.
2. **An angle board:** 6 to 10 angles, each scored on the evidence behind it, with at least one new angle to explore.
3. **One brief per picked angle:** overview, audience, messaging hierarchy, creative direction, a module for the format (static, video, UGC, carousel or agency), deliverables, requirements and success criteria with your current benchmark.
4. **A copy kit:** platform copy, a product ad and micro ads, every line with its character count.
5. **A test plan,** a list of what couldn't be checked, and the next skill to use.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. The skill only reads your accounts and public pages. To launch ads from the copy kit, use a copy-writer skill that shows each ad and waits for your yes.

## Tips

- Name the format and platform up front; the brief and the specs follow from them.
- If you're in a regulated field (legal, health, finance), say what your rules require. The skill flags the usual limits but can't know your local rules.
- Some websites block crawlers. If yours does, have your brand colours and fonts ready.
- Run it again after a test round: the winning concept becomes the next proven message.
