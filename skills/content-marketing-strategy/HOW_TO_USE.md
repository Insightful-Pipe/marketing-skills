# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "content-marketing-strategy" skill. Build our content strategy for next year and a 90-day editorial calendar, starting from what already brings us sign-ups.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is required.** Paste or upload a sitemap or post list (URL, title, format, publish date), a GA4 landing-page export and a Search Console pages export for the last 90 days, and your newsletter and video counts.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with GA4 and Search Console at least, plus any of WordPress, Klaviyo or Mailchimp, YouTube and DataForSEO. The skill then pulls the data itself.
- **Your goal:** one business metric, a target and a date. Without one it uses +20% content key events as a placeholder and says so.
- **Your audiences** (up to 3) and the questions each asks before buying.
- **Your team:** who makes content, their hours per week, and any freelance rates. Plus a monthly production budget and a paid distribution budget, if you have them.

## Example requests

**Full strategy**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-marketing-strategy" skill. Build our content marketing strategy for the year: goal is 30% more demo requests from content by the end of January. We have a content lead at 6 hours a week and a freelance writer at 5.

**Editorial calendar only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-marketing-strategy" skill. Give me a 90-day editorial calendar across blog, newsletter and YouTube that my two-person team can actually keep up with.

**No connection, from exports**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-marketing-strategy" skill. Here's our sitemap, a GA4 landing-page export and a Search Console pages export. Tell me which themes to keep, which to drop and what to publish next quarter.

**Which themes should we own?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-marketing-strategy" skill. We write about five topics. Which ones bring buyers and have search demand, and which should we stop?

**Gaps by audience**
Hey Claude, I just added the "content-marketing-strategy" skill. Tag every piece we've published by audience and buying stage, and show me where buyers who are comparing or deciding have nothing to read.

## What you get

1. **What content must do:** your goal, its baseline from GA4 and the target.
2. **What works today:** sessions, key events and search clicks by theme and by format, so you see which topics bring buyers and which only bring readers.
3. **A content map:** every existing piece counted by audience and buying stage (Learn, Compare, Decide, Use), with the gaps and thin spots named.
4. **3 to 5 themes,** each tested on two questions: does it bring buyers, and do people search for it. Each gets a verdict: Core, Build, Nurture or Drop.
5. **Formats and where each gets seen:** your own channels, partners and communities, and paid distribution only for themes with proof.
6. **A pace your team can keep,** set from what you actually published in the last 12 months, with hours by person and the cost checked against your budget.
7. **Measures for each stage,** with baselines.
8. **A dated 90-day editorial calendar** across every format: week, date, format, working title, theme, audience, stage, owner, hours, channels, paid amount and the measure it moves.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can put the calendar in a new tab of your Google Sheet. It never publishes, schedules, sends emails or spends ad money.

## Tips

- Rerun it at the end of each quarter on fresh data: Build themes that start bringing buyers get promoted, and the pace resets from the new 12 months.
- Give real hours per piece if you have them. The defaults (6 hours for an article, 16 for a guide) are a starting point.
- If your GA4 key events include things that aren't buyer actions (a scroll or a test event), fix them in GA4 first. Every content number depends on them.
