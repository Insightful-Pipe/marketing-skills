# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "interactive-calculator-builder" skill. Build an ROI calculator for my website that shows visitors what their problem costs them and how fast our product pays for itself.

## What you need

- **The result your visitors want,** in one sentence, and the numbers they can type without looking anything up.
- **Your business numbers and where they come from:** prices, plans, and any effect your product has (from your own customer data or a named study). The skill never invents these.
- **Your email form's endpoint,** if you want to capture emails. Without one, the calculator just shows the result.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** DataForSEO to name the tool after what people search, GA4 to check your key events and event names, and WordPress to check the slug, find pages to link from and save the page copy as a draft.

## Example requests

**ROI calculator**
Hey Claude, I just added the "interactive-calculator-builder" skill. Build an ROI calculator for our scheduling software: hours saved a week, hourly cost, and which plan pays back fastest. Our customers save 4 hours a week on average, from our 2026 survey.

**Lead generation calculator**
Hey Claude, I just added the "interactive-calculator-builder" skill. I want an interactive calculator for lead generation on our solar installer site: monthly bill and roof size in, yearly savings out, and an email form that posts to our CRM's form URL.

**Price estimator**
Hey Claude, I just added the "interactive-calculator-builder" skill. Make a quote estimator for our cleaning company from this price list. Show a range, not a single price, and point to the booking page.

**Name it, then save the page**
Hey Claude, I just added the "interactive-calculator-builder" skill. Check what people search for before naming the calculator, then save the page copy as a WordPress draft. Show me the draft first.

## What you get

1. **One HTML file** with no backend and no outside scripts: paste the marked block into a page, or host the file.
2. **The formula written out** step by step, with every business number and its source.
3. **Worked examples** run through the file's own code, including the edge cases and invalid inputs, with the result of each check.
4. **GA4 events** for start, finish and lead, sent without any personal data, and what your GA4 property needs.
5. **The page copy:** title tag, meta description, H1, method, worked example, FAQ and links, named after the phrasings people search when DataForSEO is connected.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can save the page copy as a WordPress draft. It never publishes, and it leaves the calculator itself for you to paste in, because WordPress strips scripts from page content for most accounts.

## Tips

- Keep it to six inputs or fewer. Every extra field costs finished results.
- Use defaults that describe a typical visitor, not your best customer.
- Show the cases where your product doesn't pay back; it makes the rest believable.
- Read the completion and capture rates in GA4 two to four weeks after launch, and trim the fields if many visitors start but don't finish.
