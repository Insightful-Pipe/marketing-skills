# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ops-stack-audit" skill. Here is our tool list with costs, seats and owners. Audit our martech stack, check what really loads on our site, and tell me what to cut, fix and connect.

## What you need

- **Your tool list.** For each tool: what you use it for, the monthly cost, seats paid and used, the owner, the renewal date and notice period, and whether you think it is active or cancelled. A pasted spreadsheet or a rough list both work; anything you don't know stays "unknown".
- **Your website and up to 5 key pages:** home, pricing or product, sign-up or checkout, thank-you, and the page most ads point to.
- **Your business model** (lead generation, self-serve software or online store) and which ad platforms are live or stopped.
- **Optional, for the live checks:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude with DataForSEO (reads your pages), Google Tag Manager, PageSpeed Insights, Google Ads, Klaviyo, Shopify or Google Analytics. Without them, the skill gives you a short snippet to run in your browser console on each page, and asks you about the rest.

## Example requests

**Full audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ops-stack-audit" skill. Here's our tool list. We sell booking software on monthly plans. Audit the stack for example.com, check the home, pricing, sign-up and demo pages, and grade it.

**Cut software spend**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ops-stack-audit" skill. We pay for too many tools. Find the overlaps, unused seats and tools nobody uses, and list the renewals I need to decide on in the next 90 days.

**What's still loading?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ops-stack-audit" skill. We cancelled a few tools this year. Check whether any of them still load on our site or in Tag Manager, and whether any pixel loads twice.

**Is the data flowing?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ops-stack-audit" skill. Check whether paid customers still reach Google Ads as offline conversions and whether Klaviyo still gets our payment events.

**Choose a tool**
Hey Claude, I just added the "marketing-ops-stack-audit" skill. We're choosing a product analytics tool. Score these two candidates against our stack. Must-haves, budget and vendor answers are below.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown, across six areas: what loads, overlap, data links, ownership and cost, site weight, and process.
2. **A ledger** of every tool and every vendor found on the site that nobody listed, each with a load status and a verdict: keep, fix, consolidate, cancel, remove or investigate.
3. **A data link map** showing which links are flowing, stale, missing or unknown, with the date of the last event.
4. **Renewals with "decide by" dates** and the monthly savings the plan adds up to.
5. **A numbered consolidation and integration plan,** data fixes first.
6. **A weighted scorecard** for each candidate tool, when you are choosing one.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact tag, it can pause a Google Tag Manager tag that shouldn't load (a cancelled tool, a pixel for ads that stopped, an unclaimed vendor, or the GTM copy of a tag that loads twice). The change lands in a workspace draft; you preview and publish. It never deletes, never publishes, and never cancels a subscription for you.
