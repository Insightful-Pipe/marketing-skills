# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "cold-email-writer" skill. Here is my offer and a list of 10 prospects. Write a cold email sequence with a personal first line for each one.

## What you need

- **Your offer and the segment you sell to,** with the problem it removes in the words your buyers use.
- **Proof you can stand behind,** as a short numbered list: results, customer names you may use, features. The emails claim nothing that isn't on it.
- **The sender:** name, title, company and postal address.
- **The prospect list,** pasted or as a CSV: company, website, first name, role, email, country, where you found each address (their contact page, a public directory), and the legal form if you know it (limited company, sole trader).
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP. With it, the skill reads the list from Google Sheets, looks up a fact for each prospect on their site, in the Meta, Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft and TikTok ad libraries, in the app stores and in the tools their site runs, and puts companies already clicking your LinkedIn ads first.

## Example requests

**A full sequence for a list**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cold-email-writer" skill. Write a first email and three follow-ups for the clinics in my Google Sheet, starting next Tuesday, with a first line for each clinic.

**A cold email template from a description**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cold-email-writer" skill. I sell bookkeeping to independent dental practices in Ohio. Write me a cold email template and two follow-ups I can reuse.

**First lines only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cold-email-writer" skill. Here's a CSV of 40 prospects. Find one fact for each and write the first line, with the link and the date of the fact.

**Handle the replies**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cold-email-writer" skill. Here are the replies to last week's sequence. Sort each one and draft my answers.

**Email plus LinkedIn and calls**
Hey Claude, I just added the "cold-email-writer" skill. Write the emails, then slot in a LinkedIn request and a call for the accounts that clicked our LinkedIn ads.

## What you get

1. **A send order,** with any company clicking your LinkedIn ads moved to the front.
2. **A first line per prospect** built on one public fact, with its link, date and age, or an honest segment line when no fact is fresh and on topic.
3. **The sequence:** a first email and two to four follow-ups, with send dates in business days, subject lines and every first email written out in full.
4. **A check on every draft:** one question, at most one link, no attachment in the first email, the word count against its ceiling, each claim tied to your proof, a line saying where you found the address, an opt-out line.
5. **A send status per prospect** under the usual UK, EU, US and Canadian business email rules, with the rows to hold and why (a reminder, not legal advice).
6. **Reply labels and drafted answers** when you paste replies, sorted by what each one asks you to do: stop, away, redirect, later, reason given, wants more, or unclear (left to you).

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can put the drafts in a new tab of your Google Sheet. It never writes to your original tab, and it never sends, schedules or uploads an email.

## Tips

- Give it real proof. A sequence with one true number beats one with five vague claims.
- Keep one offer and one segment per run; a second segment gets its own run.
- Send from a separate, warmed-up domain, not your main one, and verify the list first.
- Paste replies the day they arrive, so the sequence stops before the next email goes out.
