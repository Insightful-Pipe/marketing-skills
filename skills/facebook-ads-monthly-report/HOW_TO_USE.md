# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-monthly-report" skill. Write last month's Facebook Ads report for my account and compare it with the month before.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Meta ad account added.** The skill pulls the numbers itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Your goals,** if you have them: target cost per result or ROAS, result volume and the month's budget. Without them, the report compares with last month only.
- **The result that counts** (purchases, leads, sign-ups). Without it, each campaign is judged on the event it optimises for.
- **Anything that happened outside the account:** a sale, a launch, a site problem, a holiday. The account can't see these.

## Example requests

**Monthly client report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-monthly-report" skill. Write September's Meta Ads report for my client. They care about ROAS; the target is 3.0 and the budget was $12,000. Keep the detail in an appendix.

**Explain a bad month**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-monthly-report" skill. Our cost per lead went up a lot last month. Write the monthly report and show me exactly what drove it.

**Team review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-monthly-report" skill. Give me the full internal report for last month, including audiences, placements and which ads are fatiguing.

**Weekly report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-monthly-report" skill. Write last week's Meta Ads report: compare it with the week before and my four-week average, tell me what each change I made did, which ads are wearing out, and which days are still too fresh to judge.

**Quarterly creative review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-ads-monthly-report" skill. Run the quarterly creative review for Q3: which formats and messages worked, how long ads last before they tire, and what to test next quarter.

## What you get

1. **An executive summary:** the verdict, the key numbers against goal, the main driver and the outlook.
2. **Goal tracking** with a clear status for each target.
3. **Month over month,** with the change in cost per result split into CPM, click-through rate and conversion rate.
4. **Campaigns, audiences, placements and people:** campaigns ranked with a winner and the one that needs attention, audiences typed and flagged when retargeting runs too often or takes too much budget, and placements, age and gender with the top cost sink.
5. **Creative:** top ads, side-by-side tests, Meta's rankings, fatigue and creative health.
6. **What changed in the account** during the month, from its change history.
7. **What worked, what didn't, and next month's plan,** with goals and the reason for each.
8. **Unknowns:** anything it couldn't check, and why.

The weekly report swaps the month for the week: this week against last week and your own four-week average, the days whose results can still rise, each change you made with a before-and-after read, the ads that won and the ads wearing out, and the decisions for next week.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- pause an ad it flagged as fatiguing
- set an ad set's daily budget for next month

Everything else in the plan stays a recommendation.

## Tips

- Run it in the first week of the month. Results for the last days of a month keep arriving for about a week, and the report says so if you run it earlier.
- If the month had no delivery, the skill says so and offers the latest month that did.
- Small accounts get honest reports: below about $100 of spend or 10 results, it shows values instead of percentages and doesn't name winners.
- For the weekly report, run it on Monday. With a 7-day attribution window, last week's results can still rise for a few days; the report lists those days with the date each one settles and waits for them before calling a winner or a cut.
- Not connected yet? Paste an Ads Manager export for both months (or the last five weeks by day, for the weekly report) and it runs the same checks on that.
