# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer" skill. Some of my Google Ads are disapproved. Tell me why in plain words and fix what you can.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Paste the disapproval email, the suspension notice or the verification request, or a Policy manager export. The skill works from that.
- **Or the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with your Google Ads account.** Then it reads every flagged ad, keyword and asset itself, with Google's own policy reason and evidence.
- **The InsightfulPipe crawler, optional.** It checks each flagged landing page for errors, redirects, and contact and privacy links.
- **What you sell and where you advertise.** Housing, jobs and credit ads in the US and Canada, and healthcare or financial services, have extra rules.

## Example requests

**Disapproved ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer" skill. Six of my ads say "Disapproved". Find out why and rewrite them so they pass.

**Account suspended**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer" skill. My Google Ads account was suspended for circumventing systems. Here is the notice. What proof do I need, and can you draft the appeal?

**Payment suspension**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer" skill. Google suspended my account for suspicious payment activity. What do I send them?

**Verification request**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer" skill. Google asked us to complete advertiser verification. What do we need, and is anything else in the account at risk?

**Housing, jobs or credit ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer" skill. We run mortgage ads in the US. Check our targeting against Google's rules for credit and housing ads.

## What you get

1. **A count of flagged items** in live campaigns: ads, keywords and assets, disapproved or limited.
2. **Root causes,** so one broken page behind four disapprovals is one fix, not four.
3. **Each item in plain words:** where it sits, the policy it hits, Google's evidence, and the fix. Rewrites show before and after with character counts.
4. **A landing page check:** status codes, redirects, and whether contact and privacy links are there.
5. **A targeting check** for housing, jobs and credit ads in the US and Canada.
6. **For a suspension or verification hold:** what it means, the proof to collect, and an appeal draft for you to send.
7. **The appeals worth filing,** with the reason to pick.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- rewrite a flagged responsive search ad, or point it at a working page
- pause an ad that can't be made compliant
- pause a flagged keyword

Each edited ad goes back to Google's review. Sitelinks and other assets, targeting, appeals, verification and billing stay with you in Google Ads; the skill drafts the exact change.

## What it will never do

It never suggests a workaround that Google treats as circumventing systems: copies of a disapproved ad with small changes, look-alike characters, cloaking or hidden redirects, a new domain or a new account to get around a decision, or repeated appeals. Those can turn a disapproval into a suspension.

## Tips

- Paste Google's exact words. The reason in the notice decides the fix.
- Fix the cause before you appeal. An appeal on an unchanged problem usually fails, and each ad gets 3 unsuccessful appeals before you need Google Ads support.
- For housing, jobs, credit, healthcare or legal services, check the final wording with your own compliance adviser.
