# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "display-video-demand-gen-auditor" skill. Audit my Display, YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns and tell me which placements to exclude.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill pulls placements, exclusions, ads, audiences, video view rates and Demand Gen settings itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Your target CPA,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your account's own CPA over the audit window.
- **What the campaigns are for,** if it isn't obvious from the bidding: leads and sales, or reach and awareness. Awareness campaigns aren't judged on CPA.

## Example requests

**Placement exclusions**
Hey Claude, I just added the "display-video-demand-gen-auditor" skill. Which sites, apps and YouTube channels are wasting my Display budget? Give me a list I can paste into Google Ads. My target CPA is $60.

**YouTube view rate**
Hey Claude, I just added the "display-video-demand-gen-auditor" skill. Show me the view rate of each of my YouTube video ads for the last 30 days, and where viewers drop off.

**Demand Gen review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "display-video-demand-gen-auditor" skill. Review my Demand Gen campaign: is the budget big enough for the target, are the channels right, and does it have enough creative?

**Before relaunching**
Hey Claude, I just added the "display-video-demand-gen-auditor" skill. My Display campaign has been paused for a while. Audit its last run and tell me what to fix before I turn it back on.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for seven sections:** scope and measurement, placements, exclusions and brand safety, Display ads and audiences, YouTube view rate, Demand Gen, and Search campaigns that also buy Display. A section with no campaigns of that type is marked "not graded", never passed.
2. **A paste-ready placement exclusion list,** built only from placements that ran your ads and spent with nothing back, or drew accidental clicks. Each one shows its spend, clicks, CTR and the rule that put it there.
3. **A watch list and a placement-type roll-up,** so you can see whether apps, websites or YouTube take your clicks.
4. **A view rate report** per video ad, with quartile drop-off and the split between YouTube and video partners.
5. **A Demand Gen review:** budget against target, conversion volume, channel controls, creative depth and audiences.
6. **Up to five top fixes,** ranked by the money at stake in the window.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- pause an ad or an ad group named in the report
- switch Display expansion off in a Search campaign
- pause a campaign, when you ask for it

Placement exclusions and video partner settings aren't MCP actions, so you change those in Google Ads; the skill tells you where. It never changes budgets, bids or bidding strategies.

## Tips

- Run it monthly while Display, YouTube or Demand Gen campaigns are live, and again before relaunching a paused one.
- If those campaigns haven't spent in the last 30 days, the skill finds their last run and offers to audit it instead.
- View-through conversions are shown on their own, never added to conversions.
