# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Research keywords for my topic, group them into clusters and tell me which pages to build first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with DataForSEO added.** The skill pulls volumes, difficulty, intent, SERPs and trends itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Search Console for your site,** if you have it. The skill uses it to find where you already rank, spot demand the keyword tools miss, and match each cluster to one of your pages.
- **A topic and a few seed terms.** Seeds of 2 or 3 words work best.
- **Your country and language,** if they aren't the United States and English.

## Example requests

**Research a topic**
Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Research keywords for "project management software for agencies" and give me a shortlist with volume and difficulty.

**Cluster a list I already have**
Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Here are 60 keywords. Cluster them into topic groups and tell me which ones belong on the same page.

**Check search intent**
Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Classify the intent of these keywords and tell me which ones are worth a product page and which need a guide.

**Compare trends**
Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Compare Google Trends for these five terms over the last 12 months and tell me which is growing.

**Marketplace keywords**
Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Find keywords people use when they want to buy a walking pad on Amazon.

**Build a keyword plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "keyword-research-clustering" skill. Build a keyword plan for mysite.com: which pages to build, which to upgrade, and in what order. Save the cluster table to my Google Sheet.

## What you get

1. **A cluster table** ranked by priority, with each cluster's main keyword, its secondary keywords, volume, difficulty, intent, and your current page with its impressions and position for the main keyword.
2. **The merge decisions,** with the SERP or Search Console evidence for each one.
3. **Unique opportunities:** demand the keyword tools miss, weak SERPs nobody owns yet, and quick wins where you already rank 4 to 20.
4. **A trend comparison** for the heads of the top five clusters.
5. **A page plan:** the top five clusters, each with one action (build, upgrade, quick win or defend) and the page it applies to, or "new page". When two of your pages compete for the same query, it names the one to keep. It asks you before planning a new page where an existing one might already fit.
6. **What was left out,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the cluster table to a new tab in one of your Google Sheets. It never edits your site.

## Tips

- DataForSEO bills per call. One topic takes about 10 calls, and the skill asks before going past 15.
- Tell it what you sell, so it skips keywords for products you don't offer.
- Re-run it every quarter. New terms grow fast, and the trend view shows which ones.
