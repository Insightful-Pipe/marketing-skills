# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-fatigue-detector" skill. Check my ads for creative fatigue and tell me which ones to rotate first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your ad accounts added:** Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat or X. The skill pulls ad-level history itself; you don't export anything.
- **Which campaigns are retargeting,** if the names don't say so. Retargeting gets a higher frequency limit.
- **A target CPA,** if you have one. It's optional.

No account connected for a platform? Paste an ad-level export with a daily or weekly date breakdown, and the skill runs the same checks on it.

## Example requests

**Weekly creative health check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-fatigue-detector" skill. Run a creative fatigue check on my Meta ads for the last 8 weeks. The campaigns with "RT" in the name are retargeting.

**Results are sliding**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-fatigue-detector" skill. Our TikTok cost per result has crept up for three weeks. Is it creative fatigue, or something else?

**Several platforms at once**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-fatigue-detector" skill. Check my LinkedIn, Snapchat and X ads for fatigue, and give me one rotation plan for all of them.

**Search ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-fatigue-detector" skill. Our Google Ads search ads have run for over a year. Are any of them wearing out?

**Check, then pause**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-fatigue-detector" skill. Find my fatigued Meta ads and pause them. Show me each one before you pause it.

## What you get

1. **A status for every ad:** fresh, steady, watch, tiring or fatigued, with a 0-100 score and the points behind it.
2. **Honest labels for drops that aren't fatigue:**
   - too little data
   - a drop the whole campaign shared, which points to seasonality or the market
   - a sudden one-period drop that points to an outside cause
   - a lost position on Google Search
3. **The evidence for each ad:**
   - CTR at its best period and now, and whether the gap is beyond noise
   - how the rest of its campaign moved over the same weeks
   - frequency or age, and the change in CPM or cost per click
   - for video ads, the hook rate at the best period and now, and on Meta, its relevance rankings
4. **What to do:**
   - how long a tiring ad has left
   - refresh or replace, and what to change
   - a rotation plan for this week, the next two weeks and the next check
5. **Pipeline gaps:** ad sets or ad groups with nothing ready to take over when an ad wears out.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on each ad, it can pause a fatigued ad on:
- Meta
- Google Ads
- TikTok
- LinkedIn
- Snapchat

It never pauses the last active ad in an ad set or ad group. It never creates ads, edits copy, or changes budgets or bids. On X, it tells you which ad to pause yourself.

## Tips

- Run it every two weeks on social and every month on search, so a slide shows up as "watch" before it costs you.
- Small accounts get many "too little data" results. That's the skill refusing to guess, not a bug. Check again when ads pass 30 clicks a period.
- Launch the replacement before you pause the tired ad.
