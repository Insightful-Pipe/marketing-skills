# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-impersonation-response" skill. Customers say a fake site and scam ads are using our brand name. Find them, rank them by risk and draft the takedown reports.

## What you need

- **Your brand names and everything that is really yours:** official domains, ad pages and advertiser names, social handles, and any authorised resellers. The skill never flags those.
- **Whatever started this,** if anything: customer tickets, links, a description of the ad or profile they saw.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** with Ad Libraries, DataForSEO and Search Console. With it, the skill searches the ad libraries, lookalike domains, brand search results and your scam-related search queries itself. Without it, it works from what you paste.

## Example requests

**Someone is running scam ads in our name**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-impersonation-response" skill. A Facebook ad is offering our products at 80% off and sending people to a site that isn't ours. Find every ad and site like it and tell me where to report each one.

**How to take down a fake website**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-impersonation-response" skill. Here's a fake copy of our shop: [URL]. Get the registrar and host, and draft the abuse reports and a Safe Browsing report.

**Check before it gets worse**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-impersonation-response" skill. Check whether anyone has registered lookalike domains or is advertising with our brand name. Our sites are [domains].

**Customer support is getting "is this real?" tickets**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-impersonation-response" skill. Here are five tickets from customers who paid on another site. Write the support reply and a warning notice for our website.

## What you get

1. **A findings table:** every suspected impersonator, with where it was found, the domain's creation date and registrar, and risk points per signal, sorted into act today, this week and watch.
2. **What it ruled out:** your own pages, resellers, honest competitor ads and parked domains, so you can see they were checked.
3. **An evidence pack and drafted reports per finding:** platform ad and trademark reports, registrar and host abuse reports, and phishing reports to Google and Microsoft.
4. **A customer warning notice and a support reply template,** written around what your customers are searching.
5. **A monitoring routine** that repeats the same checks and shows only what is new or gone since the last run.

## What it won't do

It doesn't send any report, contact anyone or change any account: you review and send. It doesn't give legal advice; trademark and domain dispute steps are marked for your lawyer.

## Tips

- Give the full allowlist up front. A missed reseller shows up as a suspect.
- Don't sign in or pay on a suspect site to test it. Customer reports and screenshots are enough evidence.
- Paste a Google Ads Auction Insights export from your brand campaign for a fuller list of who appears on your brand searches.
- Run it again after the reports go out: the "gone since last check" list shows which ones worked.
