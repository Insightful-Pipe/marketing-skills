# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-planner" skill. We want to start selling on TikTok Shop. Tell me which products to launch with, what affiliate commission we can afford and how many samples to send.

## What you need

- **Your product list with unit costs.** Price, unit cost, shipping cost per order, units sold in the last 90 days and stock. With Shopify or WooCommerce connected to the InsightfulPipe MCP, the skill reads most of this itself (WooCommerce has no cost field, so it asks for costs).
- **Your TikTok Shop fee rate.** From your Seller Center fee page or a settlement export. The skill never guesses it, because TikTok changes fees by region, category and date.
- **The share of each sale you want to keep** (default 15%), your launch discount (default 10%), a creator sample budget and how many LIVE hours a week you can staff.
- **Optional: your TikTok account.** With TikTok connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, the skill reads your videos' views, likes, comments and shares to see which products already work on video. Without it, paste the numbers from TikTok Studio.
- **For a shop that is already open:** Seller Center exports of orders, product analytics, affiliate orders, LIVE sessions and the settlement statement.

## Example requests

**Launch plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-planner" skill. We sell on Shopify and want to open a TikTok Shop next month. Our fee is 8% and we keep at least 15% on every sale. Plan the launch.

**Affiliate commission**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-planner" skill. What open and target collaboration commission can we offer creators on these five products without losing money? Costs are in the sheet I pasted.

**Samples budget**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-planner" skill. We have $2,000 for creator samples this month. Which products should get them, how many each, and how many orders does a sample need to pay back?

**Grow an open shop**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-shop-planner" skill. Our TikTok Shop has been open for two months. Here are the Seller Center exports. Build the weekly scorecard and tell me what to change.

## What you get

1. **A product table:** every product's price after discount, what it leaves after TikTok's fee, cost and shipping, the most commission it can pay, the gates it passed or failed, and its hero score.
2. **Three hero products,** each with a listing title, an image list, the first lines of the description, a launch price, open and target commission rates, a sample count with its payback, a LIVE flash deal decision and a break-even ROI for Shop ads.
3. **A 4-week launch calendar** and a weekly LIVE schedule.
4. **A weekly scorecard** of sales by video, LIVE, affiliates and product card, with alerts.
5. **What couldn't be graded,** and why.

## What it changes

Nothing. There is no TikTok Shop connector, and every connector call it makes is a read. You set up the listings, collaborations, samples and LIVE sessions in Seller Center.

## Tips

- Give real unit costs. Every commission, discount and sample number depends on them.
- Confirm restricted categories in Seller Center before you list.
- Rerun it in Grow mode after 30 days of shop orders, so heroes are ranked on TikTok Shop's own sales.
