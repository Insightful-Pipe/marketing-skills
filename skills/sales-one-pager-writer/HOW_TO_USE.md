# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-one-pager-writer" skill. Write a one pager for practice managers at small clinics that my reps can send after a first call.

## What you need

- **What you sell and who the sheet is for:** one persona or one industry per sheet.
- **Your proof:** numbers with their source and dates, customer quotes with permission, customer names you may show.
- **Optional, and it makes the ranking measured:** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude with Search Console, Google Ads or LinkedIn Ads, plus the crawler for your brand colors and fonts. Without a connection, paste a Search Console queries export, your ad results, or call notes and reviews.

## Example requests

**Solution sheet for one persona**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-one-pager-writer" skill. Write a product one pager for IT managers at mid-size law firms. Rank the benefits from our Search Console and Google Ads data.

**Why us against the alternative**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-one-pager-writer" skill. Write a one-page sheet on why a clinic should switch from a paper diary and phone booking to us. Here are our proof points.

**Customer result**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-one-pager-writer" skill. Turn this customer's before and after numbers into a one-page result sheet. They gave written permission to be named.

**Without connected data**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sales-one-pager-writer" skill. Here are six call notes, our win reasons and our product notes. Write a sales one pager for operations directors, on A4.

## What you get

1. **A benefit ranking** for the reader: each benefit's share of non-brand searches, how its Google Ads lines and LinkedIn ads did against the others, and how many buyer notes mention it, turned into a score out of 100.
2. **A proof ledger:** every number and quote with its source, dates and permission. Benefits without proof stay off the page and go to a "claims to prove" list.
3. **The sheet copy, zone by zone,** with each element's word count against its budget.
4. **A layout spec a designer can follow:** page size, margins, grid, type sizes, your brand colors with a contrast check, and a fit check that shows the copy fits one printed page.
5. **Ten checks** before it goes out, each pass, fail or not applicable.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads your pages, search data and ad results and writes a draft. Printing and sending stay with you.

## Tips

- One reader per sheet. For three personas, ask for three sheets; they share one proof ledger.
- Paste the ad text of your LinkedIn creatives if the connector doesn't return it.
- Run it again each quarter: what buyers search for and click on changes, and so should the order of the blocks.
