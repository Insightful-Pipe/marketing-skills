# How to use Podcast Guesting Planner

Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-guest-pitching" skill. Get me booked on podcasts my buyers listen to: find active shows that fit my topics, write my one-sheet and a pitch for each host, and set up a tracker.

## What you need

- **The guest:** name, role, company, a few approved career facts and a headshot link.
- **2 to 4 topics,** each with what a listener takes away and one proof point with its source and date.
- **Who should hear you,** in your audience's own words (for example "salon owners" or "backpackers").
- **Your offer for listeners:** a page, a download or a promo code, so each episode's results can be counted.
- **Shows you've already been on,** shows to skip, and how many pitches a week you can send.
- Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:
  - YouTube Data, to find video podcasts and check that each one is active, takes guests and gets views
  - DataForSEO, to find audio-only shows and "be a guest" pages in Google results
  - GA4 and Shopify, to count visits, sign-ups and promo-code orders from each episode
  - Google Sheets, to keep the booking tracker (written only after you confirm)

No connection at all? Paste your bio, topics and a list of shows with their recent episodes, and Claude does the same vetting, writing and tracking from that.

## Example requests

**Start a podcast tour**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-guest-pitching" skill. I'm the founder of a bookkeeping firm for restaurants. Find 10 podcasts restaurant owners listen to, drop the dead ones, and draft my pitches.

**Just the pitch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-guest-pitching" skill. Write a podcast guest pitch for this show. Here are its last 10 episodes and my one-sheet.

**One-sheet**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-guest-pitching" skill. Turn my bio and these three topics into a podcast guest one-sheet with sample questions.

**Results**
Hey Claude, I just added the "podcast-guest-pitching" skill. My last three episodes aired in September and October. Tell me what each one brought in and which shows are worth going back to.

## What you get

1. **A vetted show list:** every candidate with its newest episode, episodes in the last 90 days, guests in the last 10, median views and topic fit, marked pass, drop (with the reason) or unknown, then ranked and tiered.
2. **A one-sheet:** short and long bios, topics with dated proof, sample questions, past appearances and your listener offer.
3. **A pitch per host,** each tied to one named recent episode, with two follow-ups and the dates to send them.
4. **A booking tracker** with a tagged link and a promo code per show.
5. **Results per aired episode:** views, visits, sign-ups or orders and revenue in the 30 days after it aired, with a verdict.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact rows, it can write the tracker to a Google Sheet you name. It never sends a pitch or fills a guest form; you send every email yourself.

## Tips

- Give the channel id for any YouTube show you already know (About, Share channel, Copy channel ID). Handles can't be looked up by name.
- Ask each host to use your tagged link in the show notes. Without it, visits from the episode can't be told apart from other YouTube traffic.
- Listen to the episode a pitch hooks on before you send it, and put your own takeaway in.
- Run it again a month after each episode airs to get the verdict.
