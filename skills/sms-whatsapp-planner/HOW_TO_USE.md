# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "sms-whatsapp-planner" skill. Plan our SMS marketing strategy: which moments should be texts instead of emails, how often to send campaigns, and every message with its segment count and cost.

## What you need

- **Nothing to connect.** Answer the questions it asks (what you sell, where your customers are, your texting platform, your sending number and its registration status), and paste your current texts or an SMS campaign export if you have one.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP with Klaviyo and, if you sell on it, Shopify.** The skill then counts your real SMS-consented audience, compares your own email and SMS revenue per recipient and SMS spend, and checks each text against the email flows already running.
- **Your price per segment,** and for WhatsApp your provider's price per message by category, if Klaviyo isn't connected. Without a price, costs stay blank.

## Example requests

**A full SMS program**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sms-whatsapp-planner" skill. We're a Shopify store on Klaviyo. Plan our SMS flows and campaigns for next quarter and write every text.

**Text or email?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sms-whatsapp-planner" skill. Should our abandoned cart and win-back messages be texts or emails? Use our Klaviyo numbers.

**WhatsApp for one market**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sms-whatsapp-planner" skill. Most of our Mexico customers use WhatsApp. Plan a WhatsApp marketing strategy for them and tell me which template category each message needs.

**Compliance check before launch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "sms-whatsapp-planner" skill. Here's our checkout consent wording and our first five texts. Check them before we start sending.

## What you get

1. **Your textable audience** by country and channel, with pending and single opt-in counts.
2. **Channel economics:** email vs SMS revenue per recipient, SMS cost per recipient and your price per segment.
3. **A moment map:** text first, email first then text, email only, or WhatsApp for each moment, with the rule that decided it.
4. **Overlap checks** against your live email flows, so nobody gets a text and an email about the same cart within the hour.
5. **Cadence:** campaigns per month and the most texts one person can get, matched to your consent wording.
6. **An opt-in kit:** keyword, two-step form wording, confirmation text, HELP and STOP replies, WhatsApp opt-in.
7. **Every message** with its encoding, characters, segments, cost per send and cost per month, plus the WhatsApp template category.
8. **A compliance pass** with the fails first, and a launch order.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads and plans; you load the messages in your texting platform.

## Tips

- Remove curly quotes and emojis from texts unless they earn their cost: one can double the segments.
- Finish US registration (10DLC, toll-free verification or short code) before the first send.
- The compliance pass flags rules; it isn't legal advice. Confirm the wording with your counsel.
