# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write a new responsive search ad for my weakest high-spend ad group and check its sitelinks and callouts.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill reads your ads, keywords, search terms and assets itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **The ad group,** if you have one in mind. Without it, the skill picks the non-brand ad group with the most spend and the weakest ad.
- **Anything the ads must never say,** such as claims your legal team has ruled out.

## Example requests

**New ad for an ad group**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write 15 headlines and 4 descriptions for my "Speeding Tickets" ad group, using the keywords that convert.

**Headlines from search terms**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Turn my top converting search terms into headlines for my main campaign.

**Descriptions from the landing page**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Read the landing page of my best ad group and write descriptions from what the page actually says.

**Sitelinks and callouts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Compare my sitelinks across campaigns and add better sitelinks and callouts where they're missing. Show me the list first.

**Price, promotion and lead form check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Tell me how my promotion, price and lead form assets are doing, and which campaigns don't have them.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard** for the ad group: ad strength, headline count, keyword coverage, pins, sitelinks, callouts and snippets, and whether the landing page could be read.
2. **What your current ad does:** every headline and description with its impressions, clicks and conversions, marked keep or replace.
3. **The words that convert:** your top keywords and search terms, and how many clicks Google shows as search terms.
4. **A new ad:** 15 headlines and 4 descriptions, each with its character count, angle and source, inside Google's limits.
5. **Asset proposals:** a sitelink comparison across the account, plus callouts, snippets, promotion, price and lead form.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- create the new responsive search ad (always paused), and turn it on after you've looked at it
- rewrite an existing ad's headlines and descriptions
- add sitelinks, callouts, a structured snippet, a price asset or a lead form

It never pauses or removes your old ad in the same step, and never changes bids, budgets or keywords.

## Tips

- Keep the old ad running next to the new one for 30 days, then compare them.
- Every number or rating in the new copy comes from your own ads, assets or page. Check that the ones marked to confirm are still true.
- If the skill can't read your landing page (some sites block crawlers and web fetch), it says so and writes from your ads and assets only. Paste the page's key points and it will use them as page facts.
