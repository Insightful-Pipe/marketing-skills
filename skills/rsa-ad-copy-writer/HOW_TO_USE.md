# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write a new responsive search ad for my weakest high-spend ad group and check its sitelinks and callouts.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Google Ads account added.** The skill reads your ads, keywords, search terms and assets itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **The ad group,** if you have one in mind. Without it, the skill picks the non-brand ad group with the most spend and the weakest ad.
- **Anything the ads must never say,** such as claims your legal team has ruled out.
- **For keyword insertion, customizers or Performance Max, Demand Gen and display copy, no connection is needed:** paste your keywords, current lines, offer dates, site link or asset report, and the skill works from that.

## Example requests

**New ad for an ad group**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write 15 headlines and 4 descriptions for my "AC installation" ad group, using the keywords that convert.

**Headlines from search terms**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Turn my top converting search terms into headlines for my main campaign.

**Descriptions from the landing page**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Read the landing page of my best ad group and write descriptions from what the page actually says.

**Sitelinks and callouts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Compare my sitelinks across campaigns and add better sitelinks and callouts where they're missing. Show me the list first.

**Price, promotion and lead form check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Tell me how my promotion, price and lead form assets are doing, and which campaigns don't have them.

**Keyword insertion, customizers and a sale countdown**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Add dynamic keyword insertion and a countdown to our sale ending November 27 at midnight Mountain time in my "AC installation" ad group, plus a price customizer per ad group. Show me every version a searcher could see before anything is created.

**Performance Max, Demand Gen and display copy**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rsa-ad-copy-writer" skill. Write the headlines, long headlines, descriptions and search themes for a new Performance Max asset group from example.com/ac-installation, replace the weak assets in my current one, and list the images and videos I need to make.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard** for the ad group: ad strength, headline count, keyword coverage, pins, sitelinks, callouts and snippets, and whether the landing page could be read.
2. **What your current ad does:** every headline and description with its impressions, clicks and conversions, marked keep or replace.
3. **The words that convert:** your top keywords and search terms, and how many clicks Google shows as search terms.
4. **A new ad:** 15 headlines and 4 descriptions, each with its character count, angle and source, inside Google's limits.
5. **Asset proposals:** a sitelink comparison across the account, plus callouts, snippets, promotion, price and lead form.

For keyword insertion and customizers, you get a render table instead: every version of each line a searcher could see, with its character count, whether it fits or falls back to the default text, and any version that reads badly. Then a yes or no per ad group, the finished lines and the customizer sheet to upload. For Performance Max, Demand Gen and display, you get the current asset counts against Google's minimum and recommended counts, the weak assets with their numbers, new text per format with counts and sources, search themes and a shot list of images and videos with the ratio each one needs.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- create the new responsive search ad (always paused), and turn it on after you've looked at it
- rewrite an existing ad's headlines and descriptions
- add sitelinks, callouts, a structured snippet, a price asset or a lead form
- create an ad with keyword insertion, a countdown or customizers (paused), after you've uploaded the customizer sheet it gives you
- create a Performance Max asset group (paused), add search themes, upload and link your images and videos, swap a weak asset, or create a responsive display ad (paused)

Customizer values, new text in an existing asset group and Demand Gen ads have no connector action, so the skill hands them over as finished text to enter in Google Ads.

It never pauses or removes your old ad in the same step, and never changes bids, budgets, keywords or audience signals.

## Tips

- Keep the old ad running next to the new one for 30 days, then compare them.
- Every number or rating in the new copy comes from your own ads, assets or page. Check that the ones marked to confirm are still true.
- Keyword insertion is skipped in an ad group when even one of its keywords would print badly (an acronym in the wrong case, a price question, "near me"); the skill names the keywords that blocked it.
- Check a countdown in the ad preview in Google Ads before turning the ad on, and replace the line on the date the skill gives you.
- If the skill can't read your landing page (some sites block crawlers and web fetch), it says so and writes from your ads and assets only. Paste the page's key points and it will use them as page facts.
