# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-outreach-writer" skill. Write LinkedIn connection request messages and follow-ups for this prospect list, each one opening from something they actually posted or announced.

## What you need

- **Your offer and the ask:** what you sell, the problem it removes, and what you want from a reply.
- **Who buys:** the roles that decide and the roles that influence, plus up to 5 short problem tags in the buyer's words.
- **Proof you can stand behind,** as a numbered list. The messages claim nothing that isn't on it.
- **Your LinkedIn plan** (free, Premium or Sales Navigator), the personalised notes or InMail credits you have left, and how many invitations a week you already send.
- **The list:** pasted rows or a CSV with name, role, company, website, LinkedIn profile, LinkedIn company page and connection degree. Paste each person's recent post or company news with its link and date if you have it.

No connection is needed. With the InsightfulPipe MCP connected, the skill also reads the list from Google Sheets, finds the news, press and careers pages on the prospect's own site, links your own LinkedIn Page posts in a follow-up, and puts companies already clicking your LinkedIn ads first.

## Example requests

**A full list**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-outreach-writer" skill. Here are 40 finance leaders as a CSV. Write the connection notes, first messages and two follow-ups, and give me a daily list starting Monday.

**One connection request message**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-outreach-writer" skill. Write a LinkedIn connection request message for this VP of Sales. Here is the post she wrote last week. I'm on a free plan.

**People I can't connect with**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-outreach-writer" skill. These five CFOs are third-degree. Write InMails for them; I have 8 credits left.

**Warm up the top accounts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-outreach-writer" skill. Use my LinkedIn Ads data to find which of these companies already click our ads, and plan a week of comments on their posts before I invite them.

**Same messages for X**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-outreach-writer" skill. Adapt the first message and follow-ups for X direct messages for the prospects who are more active there.

## What you get

1. **An order:** each prospect's score, tier and route (invitation, InMail, or comments first), with the opener it starts from, its date and link.
2. **Every message written out:** the connection note, the first message after they accept, two follow-ups, and InMails, each with its character count against LinkedIn's cap and a house target, and its checks.
3. **A comment plan** for the top accounts, with the first comment written.
4. **A daily list** of what to send, to whom and when, paced to your weekly number. You send everything yourself.
5. **A "find an opener first" list** for prospects with no fresh, relevant post or news, and what to look for.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can put the daily list in a new tab of your Google Sheet. It never sends a message, an invitation, an InMail or a comment, and it never uses an automation tool: LinkedIn's User Agreement bans them.

## Tips

- Paste the post itself, not just the link: the opener is only as good as what the skill can read.
- On a free plan, check how many personalised notes your invite screen says are left before you start; the skill gives them to the top of the list.
- Run it again each week with who accepted and who replied, and it moves the follow-ups and adjusts next week's number.
