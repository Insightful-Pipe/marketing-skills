# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "store-listing-experiments" skill. Here is our App Store Connect and Play Console traffic for the last 28 days and the store tests we want to run: tell me which ones can actually reach a result.

## What you need

- **Your store traffic for the last 28 days,** by language or localization: impressions and first-time downloads from App Store Connect, and store listing visitors and acquisitions from Play Console. A CSV export or a pasted table is fine.
- **What you want to test:** the asset, how many versions, the traffic share and the locales.
- **The smallest lift worth it and the longest you'll wait.** Without them, the skill uses 5% and 6 weeks and says so.
- **For finished tests,** the result as App Store Connect or Play Console shows it, with the start and end dates.
- **For custom pages,** your app campaigns. With the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Google Ads, Meta Ads or TikTok Ads, the skill pulls the app campaigns, ad groups, spend and ad headlines itself; otherwise list them. With the App Store and Google Play connectors, it also checks what the top apps for each keyword lead with.

## Example requests

**Size a test before it starts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-listing-experiments" skill. We want to test three new icons on the App Store at 30% traffic. Here are our impressions and downloads by country. Will it read in 6 weeks?

**Read a finished test**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-listing-experiments" skill. Our Play Console icon experiment shows the new icon ahead after 9 days. Here is the result. Should we apply it?

**Plan custom pages for app campaigns**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-listing-experiments" skill. Plan Apple custom product pages and Google Play custom store listings for our app campaigns on Google Ads, Meta and TikTok.

**Full plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "store-listing-experiments" skill. Here are our store exports, four tests we want to run and our last two results. Order the work for the next two months.

## What you get

1. **A verdict for each planned test:** run, run with changes, or don't run, with the sample size, days and the math behind it, and the cheapest change that rescues a test that can't read.
2. **A schedule** that respects each store's one-test-at-a-time rule, flagged where a test crosses a holiday or sales week.
3. **A decision for each finished arm:** apply, keep the current listing, draw, or more data needed, with the lift, its interval and whether the test ran long enough.
4. **A custom page plan:** one Apple custom product page and one Google Play custom store listing per ad theme that earns one, each with its message, first three screenshot captions, promotional text or short description, link, keywords and the ad groups to point at it.
5. **The order of work,** and what to check with each ad platform before linking a page.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. There is no App Store Connect or Play Console connector, and the ad connectors are only read. You create the tests and pages in the consoles from the plan.

## Tips

- Export traffic for the same locales the test will run in. A German-only test lives on German traffic.
- Don't stop a test because one arm looks ahead. Bring the result back when the console calls it, or when the planned days are up.
- Store rules change. The skill checks Apple's and Google's current pages and names the date.
