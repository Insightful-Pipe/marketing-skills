# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "rank-tracker" skill. Check where my site ranks on Google for my top keywords today and which SERP features I could win.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with DataForSEO and Google Search Console added.** The skill pulls the live results pages and your Search Console numbers itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Up to 5 keywords to check live,** if you have them. Without a list, it takes your 5 biggest non-brand searches from Search Console.
- **Google Business Profile, for the local pack check.** Optional: only for a business people find on Maps.
- **Google Sheets, for history.** Optional: the skill can save each snapshot to a tab you choose, after you say yes. If you don't have one yet, it asks which spreadsheet to add the tab to.

## Example requests

**Rank check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rank-tracker" skill. Track where we rank in the US for these 5 keywords and tell me what moved: trail running shoes, waterproof hiking boots, hydration vest, running socks, trail shoes for women.

**One live SERP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rank-tracker" skill. Show me the live Google results page for "best trail running shoes": who ranks, which features are on page one, and where we are.

**SERP feature opportunities**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rank-tracker" skill. Which featured snippets, AI Overviews, people-also-ask boxes and video carousels could we win on our main keywords?

**Local pack**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rank-tracker" skill. Are we in the Google local 3-pack for "dentist near me" in Austin? Compare us with the businesses that are.

**Weekly tracking**
Hey Claude, I just added the "rank-tracker" skill. Run my rank check and save today's snapshot to my "Rank history" sheet. Show me the rows first.

## What you get

1. **A rank table:** today's live position and URL for each keyword, the Search Console 28-day average now and before, the dated DataForSEO history, and a flag when the numbers disagree.
2. **What changed,** with the evidence for every move, and a "recheck" instead of a false alarm when one snapshot looks odd.
3. **Page one, keyword by keyword:** the SERP features in order, which ones you already hold (including your YouTube videos), the top 3 domains and the people-also-ask questions.
4. **Feature opportunities,** ranked high, medium and low, each with what to do.
5. **A feature map** across the keywords your site ranks in the top 20 for.
6. **A local pack check** (when asked): in or out of the 3-pack, the businesses that are in it, and the review gap.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact rows, it can:
- add a "Rank history" tab to your Google Sheet
- save today's snapshot to it

It never changes your site, your Business Profile or Search Console.

## Tips

- Run it weekly on the same keywords and the same day, and save each snapshot, so movement is measured against your own history.
- Each run uses at most 10 DataForSEO calls. Up to 5 keywords get a live results page; more keywords still show their Search Console and Labs numbers.
- Search Console averages a whole month across every searcher; a live page is one search today. When they disagree, the skill says so instead of guessing.
