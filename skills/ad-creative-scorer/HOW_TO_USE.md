# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-scorer" skill. Grade this draft ad before it goes live and tell me the three edits that would raise the grade most.

## Example prompts

**Grade a draft, calibrated on your account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-scorer" skill. Grade our new 9:16 video before it goes live on Instagram Reels and Stories, using our Meta ad account to set the bar. The goal is sign-ups.

**Grade from a paste, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-scorer" skill. Here are five frames from our TikTok ad, the script and the ad text. Score it and tell me what to fix first.

**Check a static ad**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-scorer" skill. Review this LinkedIn single-image ad and its copy. Is it ready to launch?

**Compare with a competitor**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-scorer" skill. Grade my draft and Competitor A's current Meta ad the same way, and tell me where theirs is stronger.

**Re-grade after edits**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-creative-scorer" skill. I made the edits you suggested. Grade version 2 and show how the score moved.

## What it needs

- **The ad:** the video or image file, frames or screenshots, a storyboard or a script, plus the primary text, headline and button.
- **The platform and placements** it will run in, and the campaign goal.
- **The offer** in one line, and the claims you're allowed to make.
- **Optional:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn or Snapchat Ads connected. The skill then pulls your ads' results itself and calibrates the grade on your own winners and losers. For a static or LinkedIn ad, it may ask for your best and worst ads' text or first frames.
- **Optional:** a competitor's name, to find their ad in the Meta Ad Library, or a screenshot of it.

No connection? It grades against stated default weights and says so at the top. You can also paste an ad-level export (ad name, text, impressions, spend, clicks and results) and it calibrates from that.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100** with its band (launch, launch after the top edits, or rework) and a policy result.
2. **A scorecard** for nine criteria, each with the second, the line or the pixels behind its score.
3. **The top 3 edits,** each with the grade points it adds, and the grade after them.
4. **Spec notes:** safe-zone bands in pixels, anything that crosses them, size and text length, with the date the platform's spec page was read.
5. **Calibration** (when connected): your account's hook, hold and click rates and cost per result, the weights your winners and losers set, and where the draft sits between them.
6. **A competitor grade** on the same scale, if you ask for one.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads results and grades files. You make the edits, then run it again.

## Tips

- Upload the first frames of your 3 or 4 best and worst ads. The skill tags what those frames show, which sharpens the calibration.
- Give the placement, not just the platform. Reels and Feed have different safe zones and text lengths.
- Grade the competitor's ad for the idea, then write your own version. The skill never hands back their lines.
