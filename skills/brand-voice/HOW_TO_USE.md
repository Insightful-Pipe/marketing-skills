# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. Build our brand voice guide and messaging house from our live ads and pages, and check our current ads against it.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with at least one source of your own copy: Google Ads or Meta ads, a Facebook Page, Instagram or a LinkedIn Page, or a website DataForSEO can read. Google Ads search terms or Search Console add the words your customers use. The skill pulls everything itself; you don't paste exports.
- **Optional, and worth giving:** two or three pieces of copy you love and two or three that feel wrong, what your brand stands for and against, your rivals' names, and any rules your industry sets on claims.
- **Nothing connected?** Paste 10 to 20 pieces of your copy and a few rival websites, and the skill builds the guide from those, without the click data.
- **For a website messaging audit:** GA4 and Search Console connected (they pick the pages and show what visitors searched), plus your fact sheet: today's prices, plan names, counts and promises, and any names or offers you've retired. No connection? List your key URLs or paste each page's text.

## Example requests

**Write the voice guide**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. Write our tone of voice guide from our ads, posts and website, so the team and our AI tools sound the same.

**Messaging house**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. Build a messaging house for us: one core message, three pillars and the proof points we already use.

**Check live copy against the voice**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. Check our Google Ads headlines and descriptions against our voice and rewrite the five that need it most.

**Refresh a voice that feels generic**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. Our copy sounds like every competitor. Show me where the voice territory is crowded and where it's open, and set a voice we can own.

**Website messaging audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. Run a website copy audit on our top pages: check that our prices, numbers, plan names and promises match across the site and our ads, flag anything out of date, and give me the fixes in order of traffic. Here's our fact sheet.

**Starting from scratch**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-voice" skill. We're a new accounting software brand with no ads yet. Here are five pieces of copy we like and three we don't. Define our voice.

## What you get

1. **A quick reference card:** your voice in three words, a one-line reminder and five do / don't pairs.
2. **The evidence:** which messages earn more clicks than their campaign's usual, the words your customers search with, how your rivals sound, how your pages sound, and every fact you spell more than one way.
3. **The guide:** 3 or 4 voice pillars, each with the evidence behind it; we are / we are not; where your voice sits on five scales today and where it should; tone by context; style rules; words to use, avoid and own.
4. **A messaging house:** the core message, three pillars and proof points taken only from what you already say.
5. **A copy check:** every live ad line marked on voice, edit or rewrite, with five before-and-after rewrites that fit the character limits.
6. **An example post or email** in the voice, plus a writer checklist and four quick tests.
7. **In the website messaging audit:** a table of your key pages with traffic, headline match and status; every fact that differs between pages, ads and your fact sheet; out-of-date lines, mixed buttons and jargon hot spots; and a fix list ranked by traffic, with the sentence as published and the sentence to put in its place.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads your accounts and writes the guide or the fix list. To put rewrites live, edit them in the ad platform yourself, or use a writing skill such as RSA Ad Copy Writer, Meta Ad Copy Writer or Social Content if you have them installed.

## Tips

- Run it on 90 days of live ads: lines need about 100 impressions before their clicks say anything.
- Copy that feels wrong teaches the skill as much as copy you like.
- Settle any conflict it flags (two different numbers for the same fact) before the proof point goes back into your ads.
- In regulated fields such as law, health and finance, check every flagged superlative against your industry's advertising rules.
- Run it again after a rebrand, a new offer or a new market.
- Before a price change or a plan rename, run the website messaging audit with the new values in the fact sheet: it lists every page and ad that still carries the old ones.
