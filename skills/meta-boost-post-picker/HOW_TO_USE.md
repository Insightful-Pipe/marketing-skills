# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-boost-post-picker" skill. Which of our Facebook and Instagram posts should we boost to get more intro class bookings? We have $490 for the next two weeks.

## What you need

- **Nothing to connect, if you'd rather paste.** Export your post stats from Meta Business Suite (Insights, Content) and, if you've boosted before, an ad-level export from Ads Manager. Paste them or upload the files.
- **Or the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with your Facebook Page, Instagram account and Meta ad account added. The skill then pulls your posts, their stats and your past ads itself.
- **What the money is for:** bookings or sales on your site, website visits, messages, profile visits or followers, or reach.
- **Your budget and how many days,** plus the area and ages you serve.

## Example requests

**Pick the posts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-boost-post-picker" skill. Which three posts from the last three months should I boost for more website bookings? Budget $40 a day.

**How to boost a post on Instagram**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-boost-post-picker" skill. I want more Instagram followers for my bakery. Which Reel should I boost, and how do I set it up?

**Boost post vs Ads Manager**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-boost-post-picker" skill. Should I use the Boost button or Ads Manager for this post? Here's my Business Suite export.

**Did my boosts work?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "meta-boost-post-picker" skill. I've boosted posts for six months and I'm not sure it brought any customers. Compare my boosts with my Ads Manager ads.

## What you get

1. **Up to three picks,** each with the numbers behind it: how its shares, saves, comments, profile visits and clicks, measured against how many people saw it (views on Facebook), compare with your usual post.
2. **The setup for each pick:** Boost button or Ads Manager, the goal, the audience, a daily budget, the number of days and the results to expect from your own past cost.
3. **A ranking of every post,** and the posts left out (too little reach, already promoted, an offer that has ended).
4. **A review of past boosts** next to your Ads Manager ads, on cost per result and on the result you actually want.
5. **The best post for followers,** when your main goal is something else.

## What it can create for you

With your yes on the exact payloads, it creates a paused campaign, ad set and ad in Ads Manager that reuse your existing post, so its likes and comments stay on it. You switch it on in Ads Manager. It never changes your existing campaigns.

## Tips

- Boost posts that already did well with the people who saw them. The skill finds those; a post that didn't land on its own is a weaker bet with money behind it.
- Pick the goal first. A boost for engagement buys cheap likes, not bookings.
- Judge a boost after 7 days, not the first morning.
