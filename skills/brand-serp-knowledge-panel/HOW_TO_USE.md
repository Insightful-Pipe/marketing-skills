# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-serp-knowledge-panel" skill. Audit what people see when they search our brand name, and tell me how to fix our knowledge panel.

## What you need

- **Your brand name,** your main domain and any other domains you own.
- **Your facts:** legal name, founding year, founders, headquarters, logo URL and your official profiles. The skill turns them into one fact sheet and checks every source against it.
- **Either a screenshot of your brand results page** (taken in a private window, in the country you care about), **or the InsightfulPipe MCP connected** with DataForSEO. Search Console and Bing Webmaster add your brand query clicks; the crawler reads your home page markup when it can reach the page.

## Example requests

**Brand SERP audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-serp-knowledge-panel" skill. Map page one for our brand name, label every result and tell me which ones to fix first.

**Wrong knowledge panel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-serp-knowledge-panel" skill. Our Google knowledge panel shows the wrong founding year and no social profiles. Show me where Google gets it and how to correct it.

**No knowledge panel yet**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-serp-knowledge-panel" skill. How do we get a Google knowledge panel for our company? Check what we already have and give me the plan.

**Two domains**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-serp-knowledge-panel" skill. We renamed last year and still own the old domain. Which one ranks for our brand, and what should we do with the old one?

**From a screenshot**
Hey Claude, I just added the "brand-serp-knowledge-panel" skill. Here is a screenshot of what Google shows for our name. Tell me what's hurting us.

## What you get

1. **A map of page one:** every result labelled owned, official profile, third party (press, reviews, forums), competitor, negative or a different entity, and how many of the top 10 you control.
2. **A scorecard for six steps:** page one, knowledge panel, questions, reviews and negative results, entity facts and markup, and brand demand.
3. **Up to 7 fixes,** each naming the exact result it targets, who does it, and what to recheck.
4. **The fact sheet,** with every source that disagrees with it.
5. **A knowledge panel plan:** claim and edit steps if you have a panel, the steps that earn one if you don't.
6. **Ready-to-paste JSON-LD** for Organization and WebSite, with your real profiles in sameAs.

## What it changes

Nothing. It drafts the markup, page and profile edits, and the Google feedback steps; you publish them.

## Tips

- Give it the founding year, founders and headquarters you want shown. It never copies a fact from a directory just because Google uses it.
- If you renamed, list the old name as a brand variant so old-name searches are counted.
- Recheck about a month after the fixes go live. Panels and site names change as Google recrawls the sources.
