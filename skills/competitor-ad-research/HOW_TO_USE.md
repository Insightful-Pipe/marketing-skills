# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Pull the live ads my three biggest competitors are running on Meta, Google, LinkedIn and Microsoft, and tell me what to test next.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with the Ad Libraries source added.** The skill reads the public ad libraries itself; you don't need screenshots or exports.
- **Your competitors:** names or website domains, up to five. A domain works best for Google; a brand name works best for Meta and LinkedIn.
- **Your own brand,** if you want your ads compared side by side with theirs.
- **A region,** if you only care about one country. Without it, the skill looks everywhere.

## Example requests

**Full competitor scan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Research the ads Asana, monday.com and ClickUp are running right now, and compare them with ours.

**Longest-running ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Which of my competitors' Meta ads have been running the longest? Those are probably the winners.

**Offers and pricing**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Track the offers, discounts and prices my competitors put in their ads.

**Landing pages**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Map where each competitor sends ad traffic, and which pages get the most ads.

**What's new this week**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Show me only the ads my competitors launched in the last 7 days.

**Category benchmark**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. Who are the top 10 advertisers on Meta for "project management software", and what angles do they use?

**Country messaging**
Hey Claude, I just added the "competitor-ad-research" skill. How does this competitor change its ad messaging between the US, the UK and Germany?

## What you get

1. **A snapshot table:** ad counts per competitor on each library, with links to each library page. Meta counts include ads that have stopped, with how many are still running; Google's count is the undated list the library shows; a count is marked as a minimum when the library had more pages than the skill read.
2. **The proven ads:** the longest-running ones, with how long each has run.
3. **Offers and prices** found in ad text, quoted exactly.
4. **A landing page map:** which pages get the ads.
5. **Volume over time** and **what's new** in your window.
6. **A hook and angle swipe file,** grouped by angle, with real quotes.
7. **The format mix:** video, image and text, per competitor and library.
8. **Messaging by country,** when the library shows country data.
9. **What to test next:** three to five ideas, each tied to the ads behind it.
10. **The gaps:** what each library couldn't show, so you don't read too much into a count.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. Ad libraries are public and read only. The skill only reads.

## Tips

- Ad libraries don't show spend or results (outside a few EU and political cases). An ad that runs for months is a good sign it works, not proof.
- Run it weekly with "new this week" to keep a running file of competitor launches.
- Hand the swipe file to an ad copy or creative brief skill, if you have one installed, to turn the ideas into drafts.
