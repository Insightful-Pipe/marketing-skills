# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Audit my site's backlinks, compare them with my two main competitors, and give me an outreach plan.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with DataForSEO added.** The skill pulls the link data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Your domain and two to four competitors.** The first two drive the link gap.
- **Optional:** Search Console, the crawler and Bing Webmaster. They add the pages that need links, live checks of broken pages and a second opinion on links, at no extra DataForSEO cost.
- **No connection?** The link reclamation, directory and local modes also work from mention URLs or a backlink export you paste, or from a short description of your business. Anything the skill couldn't check is marked "not checked".

## Example requests

**Profile overview**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Give me an overview of the backlinks for example.com and grade them.

**Toxic and lost links**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Check example.com for spammy and lost backlinks and tell me whether I should disavow anything.

**Competitor gap**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Which sites link to competitor-a.example and competitor-b.example but not to example.com?

**Outreach and PR**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Build me a link building outreach plan and three digital PR story ideas from my backlink data, and save the prospects to my Google Sheet.

**Unlinked mentions and broken link building**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Find pages that mention Example Booking but don't link to example.com, fix links that hit our 404s or redirect chains, and find dead pages on two blogs in our niche that one of our guides could replace.

**Directory submissions**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. Which directories and review sites should I submit example.com to? Use the ones that list competitor-a.example and competitor-b.example, skip link farms, write the listing copy for each and give me a tracker.

**Local link building**
Hey Claude, I just added the "backlink-auditor" skill. I run a plumbing company in Austin at plumber.example. Find local sites that link to two or more nearby plumbers but not to us, and give me the ask and an email for each.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown.
2. **A scorecard for six areas:** spam and link schemes, natural anchor text, growth and losses, broken links to reclaim, link distribution, and the competitor gap.
3. **A straight answer on disavowing,** and the reason for it.
4. **A reclaim list:** linked pages that return an error, each with the live page to redirect it to.
5. **A tiered outreach plan:** real sites that link to your competitors, the page to pitch them and two email drafts.
6. **Three digital PR story ideas,** each backed by numbers from the run.

Ask for a mode and you get that instead:
- **Link reclamation:** unlinked mentions checked on the live page, your broken and redirected links with the 301s to add, and other sites' dead pages you could replace, sorted by site authority and the value of your page.
- **Directory shortlist:** the directories worth a listing, with traffic, rank, nofollow and paid marked, the copy for each site's fields, and a tracker.
- **Local link building:** local news, associations, sponsors, suppliers and community sites, the ones linking to nearby rivals first, with the ask and an email for each.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the prospect list, or a mode's list or tracker, to a new tab in a Google Sheet you choose.

It never sends emails, uploads a disavow file or edits your site. It drafts those for you.

## Tips

- Run it quarterly, and again after any burst of new links.
- Name competitors who are your size. A giant's link profile gives you a long list you can't win.
- One full audit uses about 10 DataForSEO calls, and up to 13 with the optional steps. Each mode uses up to 12, and none when you paste the data.
- For local links, name independent nearby rivals, not national chains.
