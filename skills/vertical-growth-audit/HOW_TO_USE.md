# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. Run a growth audit on my business for its vertical and tell me the moves that matter most.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with the DataForSEO, PageSpeed Insights and Ad Libraries sources. The games check also uses Google Play and Apple App Store, and the creator check uses YouTube Data. The skill pulls the public data itself.
- **Your business name and website,** and which vertical you're in: travel and booking, events and tickets, education and courses, a two-sided marketplace, mobile games, a nonprofit, or a creator business.
- **Optional, for the inside lens:** your GA4 property and your Google Ads, Meta or TikTok ad account connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP. With them, the skill also grades your conversion funnel and your cost per result.
- **Optional extras:** up to two rivals, your event date or intake months, your app's store ids (games), your YouTube channel id (creators), and whether your Google Ads account is an Ad Grants account (nonprofits).

## Example requests

**Travel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. Audit our tour company's booking funnel for the US market and compare us with our two biggest rivals.

**Education**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. We run online data courses with intakes in January and September. Audit our enrolment funnel, using our GA4 and Google Ads.

**Events**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. Our festival is on July 18. Build an event ticket sales report and tell me if our ads are starting early enough.

**Marketplace**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. We're a two-sided marketplace for home services. Check whether both sides, customers and pros, can find us.

**Mobile game**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. Give me a user acquisition report for our game: store health on both stores and cost per install on TikTok and Meta.

**Nonprofit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. Review our nonprofit's Ad Grants account for compliance and show where donations drop off in GA4.

**Creator**
Hey Claude, I just added the "vertical-growth-audit" skill. Audit my creator business: my YouTube channel cadence, my course page speed and my newsletter funnel.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown.
2. **A scorecard** for search funnel coverage, conversion page speed, paid presence and your vertical's own check (season readiness, supply and demand balance, store health, Ad Grants compliance or channel cadence), plus your GA4 funnel and cost per result when those accounts are connected. Category demand and seasonality come first, as context.
3. **Up to five top moves,** each with the numbers behind it.
4. **A benchmark column** for each rival you name.
5. **A list of what couldn't be graded,** and what would make it run.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. This skill only reads. It recommends changes and points you to the skill that can help with each one.

## Tips

- Run it before your peak season, not in it: the season check looks at the two months before your peak.
- Give your real head terms if you know them. They drive the demand, ranking and season checks.
- One business per run. To compare against rivals, name them as rivals; they appear as benchmark columns.
