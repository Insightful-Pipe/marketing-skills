# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "copy-transcreation" skill. Adapt our reminders page and its search ads for Germany, and tell me which blog posts to localize first for Germany and Spain.

## What you need

- **The copy to adapt:** paste it, or give the page URL, the ad group or the email.
- **The target markets as language plus country,** for example de-DE, es-ES, fr-CA or es-MX.
- **Your locked list,** if you have one: product, plan and feature names and anything that must stay in English.
- **Local facts:** your prices in local currency (and whether they include tax), and which features, payment methods and proof you have in each market. The skill never converts a price at the exchange rate.
- **A native reviewer** for each market. The skill hands them a checklist and a back-translation of every line.

Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP: **Search Console** and **GA4** show which countries and languages already find you and which pages they land on; **Google Ads** and **Microsoft Ads** give the search terms from campaigns in that language; **DataForSEO** gives search volumes and the results page per locale; **WordPress** reads the article text. No connection? Paste the copy, any local search terms and your traffic by country, and the skill runs the same steps.

## Example requests

**Landing page and ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copy-transcreation" skill. Transcreate our pricing page and its Google Ads for France. Prices are 17, 42 and 85 euros a month plus VAT.

**What to localize first**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copy-transcreation" skill. Which blog posts and guides should we localize first for Germany, Spain and Mexico? Use our Search Console and GA4 data.

**Taglines and buttons**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copy-transcreation" skill. Adapt our tagline, our sign-up buttons and the reminder SMS for Spanish speakers in Spain. Keep our plan names in English.

**Local search words**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copy-transcreation" skill. What do people in Germany actually search for instead of "appointment reminder software"? Show me the literal translation next to the term you'd use.

**Translation vs transcreation check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "copy-transcreation" skill. Here is the German version our agency translated. Check it against the English, back-translate it and flag anything that won't work in Germany.

## What you get

1. **Market evidence:** your markets ranked, and for each one the words people search, with the literal translation shown next to the term chosen and the numbers behind it.
2. **What to adapt first:** pages and articles per market in three tiers (adapt first, adapt next, wait), from impressions and key events by country.
3. **A locked list** of names and terms, used the same way in every line.
4. **The adapted copy:** every line next to its source and an English back-translation, with ad lines counted against the Google Ads and Microsoft Ads limits, and the rejected literal translations with the reason.
5. **Claims to check:** comparisons, "free", prices, outcome figures, health, money and compliance claims, per market.
6. **A long-form plan:** a local title and a swap list (examples, prices, units, sources, laws, links) for the top article per market, plus the queue.
7. **A checklist for your native reviewer.**

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- create the adapted search ad, always paused, in a campaign that targets only that market's countries (if the campaign also serves other countries, it asks for their prices and facts first)
- save an adapted article as a WordPress draft (never published)

It never publishes, edits live pages or existing ads, or changes budgets and targeting.

## Tips

- Name the country, not just the language: Spanish for Mexico and for Spain need different words and prices.
- Decide formal or informal address once per market, and tell the skill.
- Run the native review before switching anything on. The back-translation shows the reviewer what each line now says.
