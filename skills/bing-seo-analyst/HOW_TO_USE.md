# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "bing-seo-analyst" skill. Analyse how my site does on Bing, compare it with Google, and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Bing Webmaster Tools site added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Google Search Console for the same site,** if you want the Bing vs Google comparison.
- **DataForSEO,** if you want to see who ranks on Bing for your topics and a backlinks summary. That's up to 4 paid calls, and the skill asks before making them.
- **Your brand terms,** including any old names, so brand searches are kept apart from the rest.

## Example requests

**Bing overview**
Hey Claude, I just added the "bing-seo-analyst" skill. How did my site do on Bing over the last four weeks, and what changed?

**Bing vs Google**
Hey Claude, I just added the "bing-seo-analyst" skill. Compare my Bing and Google traffic and show me the pages Google likes that Bing ignores.

**Crawl errors**
Hey Claude, I just added the "bing-seo-analyst" skill. Check my Bing crawl stats, crawl errors and sitemaps.

**Who ranks on Bing**
Hey Claude, I just added the "bing-seo-analyst" skill. Show me who ranks on Bing for "standing desk" and "ergonomic office chair", and which of my Google queries never show on Bing.

## What you get

1. **A status board for seven sections:** performance, queries, pages, Bing vs Google, crawl health, backlinks and keyword research.
2. **A ranked triage list,** with the numbers behind each item and the call they came from.
3. **Your queries split into brand, lookalike and non-brand,** with striking-distance and zero-click lists.
4. **The pages Google sends traffic to that Bing never shows,** each with a verdict: submit it, work on it, or drop it.
5. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- submit pages to Bing, within your daily quota
- submit your sitemap, if Bing has none

It never changes crawl settings, never blocks URLs and never removes sitemaps.

## Tips

- Bing updates query and page data once a week, so run it weekly or monthly. Running it daily adds nothing.
- If another company shares part of your name, give that word as the lookalike word, so its searches stay out of your brand numbers.
- Run it again a few weeks after submitting pages, and check whether they show up in Bing's page rows.
