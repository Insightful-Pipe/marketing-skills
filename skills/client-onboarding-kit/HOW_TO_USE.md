# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "client-onboarding-kit" skill. We just signed a new client; here's the proposal. Build our onboarding kit and tell me which of their accounts we can already read.

## What you need

- **The signed proposal or SOW,** pasted or uploaded. No proposal? Describe the services sold, the platforms, the start date and the targets.
- **Your agency's details:** who works on the account, and the IDs clients grant access to (your Google Ads manager account, Meta business portfolio ID, TikTok Business Center ID, the email for user invites, your Shopify Partner organisation).
- **The client's details:** website, what they sell, who approves, who owns the website, and which platforms they already use.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with the client's accounts added as they arrive. The skill then checks which accounts answer and pulls a 30-day baseline itself. Without it, the kit works from what you paste or tell it.

## Example requests

**Full kit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-onboarding-kit" skill. We signed an online store for Google Ads, Meta ads, SEO and Klaviyo email. Here's the SOW. Build the full onboarding kit with a 30-60-90 day plan.

**Questionnaire only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-onboarding-kit" skill. Write the client onboarding questionnaire for this proposal. Don't ask anything the proposal already answers.

**How to give us access**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-onboarding-kit" skill. Write the access request for a new client: Google Ads, GA4, Tag Manager, Search Console, Meta and Shopify, with the smallest role we need on each.

**Access check in week one**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-onboarding-kit" skill. Check which of the new client's accounts we can read now and draft a chaser for the rest.

**Client leaving**
Hey Claude, I just added the "client-onboarding-kit" skill. This client ends on the 30th and moves to an in-house team. Build the handover pack.

## What you get

1. **A scope map** from the proposal: each service sold, the platforms it needs, and what isn't requested and why.
2. **An access request list:** the least-privilege role on each platform, where the client clicks, and when a bigger role would be needed. Never a password.
3. **An access check:** ready, ready but quiet, role too low, reconnect, not visible, or confirm by hand, with readiness as "x of y".
4. **A chaser** for what the client still has to do, drafted, never sent, plus your own internal task list.
5. **A 30-day baseline** from the accounts that answered.
6. **An intake questionnaire** fitted to the services sold, with the proposal's answers to confirm and at most 20 open questions.
7. **A timed kickoff agenda, a communication plan, a RACI and a 30-60-90 day plan** whose targets start from the baseline and flag any that are a big jump.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It drafts the requests and messages; the client grants access in each platform and you send the messages yourself.

## Tips

- Run the access check again each day of week one; it redrafts the chaser with only what's still missing.
- Send the questionnaire before the kickoff, so the call is for the questions that didn't fit.
- Grant access to your agency's business account (manager account, business portfolio, Business Center) rather than one person, so staff changes don't need the client again.
